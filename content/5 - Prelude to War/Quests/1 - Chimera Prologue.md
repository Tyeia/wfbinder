[The Man in The Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_The_Wall "The Man in The Wall") manifests itself in the [Orbiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter "Orbiter")'s [Personal Quarters](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter#Rooms "Orbiter"), luring the Tenno towards the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus")' helmet, dropped during the events of [Apostasy Prologue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Apostasy_Prologue "Apostasy Prologue"). When the Tenno touches the helmet, they are transported to [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"), where they begin to follow The Man in The Wall. The Man in The Wall leads the Tenno through the [Reservoir](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reservoir "Reservoir"), and into the Chamber of the Lotus. A portal at the end of the room transports the Tenno into a [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") chamber that seems to imprison [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas"), now malformed with Sentient growth. Cursing the Lotus for allegedly deceiving him, Ballas discreetly constructs the "Sentient Slayer" away from what he claims to be Lotus' watchful eye. The Tenno grasps the image of the legendary  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paracesis.png/32px-Paracesis.png?f1231)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) [Paracesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) , learning its blueprint in the process. — [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War") is coming.
