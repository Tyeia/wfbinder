Inside the Sentient mothership, [[Erra]] is telling [[Natah]] that their [mother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa"), who she wrongly believed to be alive, is dead and urges her to finish the war as "[she has] her fire". Natah notes that she remembers ordering the Tenno to attack Erra and that he was destroyed. Erra objects and states that she remembers wrong and will need time to completely heal from "what the [Makers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") had done to [her]". Natah is not convinced and points at leashed [[Ballas]] asking if he is one of the Makers.

Erra suddenly grabs and throws Natah at the pedestal behind him, where she is trapped in a field of energy. Erra tells her to "finish the war" and drops Ballas's leash. Revealing that his imprisonment was a deception, Ballas stands as he and Erra silently watch Natah.
