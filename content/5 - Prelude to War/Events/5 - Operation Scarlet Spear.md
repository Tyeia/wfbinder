**Operation: Scarlet Spear** is an event released on March 24, 2020 on PC and ended on April 28, 2020 at 2:00 p.m. ET. On consoles, it was released on April 9, 2020 and ended on May 14, 2020 at 1:30 p.m. ET. It introduces the [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") invasion of the Origin System as part of the [Prelude to War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prelude_to_War "Prelude to War") story arc.

# Synopsis

The [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") are invading the Origin System—their first destination: [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"). Tenno must coordinate their efforts to fend off Sentient [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") warships in space and [Condrix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condrix "Condrix") drop ships on the ground. [Little Duck](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Little_Duck "Little Duck") has set up a Scarlet Spear Flotilla Relay off Earth's atmosphere as the Tenno's primary base of operations during this invasion.

Angered by the presence of Sentient and Tenno alike, [General Sargas Ruk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/General_Sargas_Ruk "General Sargas Ruk") of the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") faction tries to sabotage the Tenno's efforts in fending off Condrixes on the forests of Grineer-controlled Earth, believing that it is the Tenno's fault for the Sentient invasion.
