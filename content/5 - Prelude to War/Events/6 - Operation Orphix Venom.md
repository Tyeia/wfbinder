> **Stop the Sentients!**
> 
> [Father](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Father "Father") is putting his [Necramechs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necramech "Necramech") to the challenge in **Operation: Orphix Venom**! The Sentients have resumed their campaign against the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Star_Chart "Star Chart") and have deployed weaponized pulses cleverly designed to disable [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe")! Arm your Necramech to take on the Sentient Orphix, or Transference into a fallen Necramech found in the Operation.

**Operation: Orphix Venom** is the second [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") invasion against the Origin System as part of the [Prelude to War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prelude_to_War "Prelude to War") story arc. This event puts the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno")'s Necramechs against Sentient enemies with [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") and [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") caught in the crossfire in normal tilesets.

The event lasted from December 18, 2020 2:50 PM ET to January 18, 2021 2:00 PM ET on PC and from January 21, 2021 3:20 PM ET to February 21, 2021 3:20 PM ET on all consoles.

Following the Tenno's victory and the Sentients' defeat in [Operation: Scarlet Spear](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Scarlet_Spear "Operation: Scarlet Spear"), [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") and the Sentients have retreated and gone into hiding. The second Sentient invasion in the Origin System thus begins, and the [Orphix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orphix "Orphix") Sentient Units return, but this time they have been taught to override Warframes by deploying weaponized pulses cleverly designed to disable them, leaving the [Necramechs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necramech "Necramech") as the Tenno's last option.

During the Operation, [Natah](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") transmits messages to the Tenno, revealing that she was the one who taught the Orphix to disable Warframes. Her messages about deploying the Orphix series, containing a letter followed by a number, reveal a code from ordering the numbers, which says: "_**I AM DYING**_".
