**Erra** is a high ranking  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [[Sentient]] commander and warlord. Son of [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]] and [Praghasa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa"), and older brother of [[Natah]], he made his first appearance in [The New War Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7ZW4cfsB6Pw) during [TennoCon 2019](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/TennoCon/2019 "TennoCon/2019"), and officially appeared in the [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Quest\) "Erra (Quest)") cinematic quest.

Implied to be more of a scientist than a warrior, Erra is the creator of the [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon") and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CalibanIcon.png/31px-CalibanIcon.png?2dd39)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Caliban) [Caliban](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Caliban), freakish amalgamations of Warframe and Sentient.

# Personality
Erra is a cruel and vicious warlord, fueled by his hatred for everything connected to the [[Orokin]] and the [[Tenno]]. When noticing a wounded, defenseless [Dax warrior](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Varzia "Varzia") on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"), slowly crawling away with her life— posing no threat to him— Erra grabbed her by the head, lifted her up to his face, and began crushing her head, only dropping her when his sister commanded him to. In Erra's eyes, if someone is even remotely connected to the Orokin, they have to die.

When interacting with his sister Natah, Erra is noticeably kinder, trying to plead with her to return to her family upon encountering her as the Lotus. And when she is eventually returned to the fold, Erra is rather patient with Natah as she suffers from the negative effects of mental manipulation. However, Erra is willing to harm her when his patience runs thin, always keeping their invasion his priority. When [[Narmer]] finally takes control of the system, Erra is proud of his accomplishments and convinced that his people are flourishing under Narmer. His father's attempts to call out his hypocrisy and blindness to Ballas' manipulations are met with disdain, as Erra believes Hunhow to be a failure.

Only later does he realize his father was telling the truth, helping the Tenno and noting with regret that his sister has to die yet again. When informed that his sister may yet be saved, Erra willingly sacrifices himself to allow the Tenno to continue their mission.

# Lore & History
### [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Quest\) "Erra (Quest)")

Late in the [Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Story_and_History#The_Old_War "Story and History"), Erra led an assault on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua") alongside another Sentient, presumably [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]]. He reached a cave full of [Reservoirs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reservoir "Reservoir"), where he grabbed a still living [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") by [her](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Varzia "Varzia") head. When the [[5 - Prelude To War/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]], surrounded by six [Operators](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"), ordered him to let her go, Erra was devastated at seeing her current self now siding with the [enemy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin"), and tried to make her remember who she used to be as Natah and return to [their people's](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") side. He failed, and the Tenno attacked him using Void Beams, making him scream in agony.

In the present, Erra and Natah reunite, as he holds [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]] on a leash. He informs her of how much the system has changed since she was "unraveled". Natah calls Ballas out for having created the [[Tenno]]. Erra asks his sister to sing for her people, to bring them back home, which she does.

### [The Maker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Maker "The Maker")

Erra is telling Natah that their mother is gone, and urges her to finish the war as she still has "her fire". Natah notes that she remembers ordering the Tenno to attack Erra and he was apparently destroyed. Erra and Ballas appear to be taken aback by this before the former objects, stating that she remembers wrong and will need time to completely heal from what the Makers had done to her. Natah points at leashed Ballas and asks if he is one of the Makers.

Erra then suddenly grabs Natah and throws her at the pedestal behind him, trapping her in an energy field. Erra tells her once more to finish the war and drops the leash from his hand. Revealing that his imprisonment was a deception, Ballas stands as both he and Erra then silently watch Natah.

### [Operation: Scarlet Spear](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Scarlet_Spear "Operation: Scarlet Spear")

Erra started the first invasion of the New War targeting [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"), the former heart of the Orokin Empire. A fleet of [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") warships were sent to the [Veil Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veil_Proxima "Veil Proxima"), an uncontrolled region of open space where [[Corpus]] and [[Grineer]] still battle for dominance, likely chosen as a safety precaution. From there, the Murex launched swarms of [Condrix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condrix "Condrix") transport ships at various points on overgrown Earth for reconnaissance.

At the same time, the various Syndicates and resistance movements in the Origin System banded together with the Tenno as front fighters to fight back against the Sentient threat. Organised by [Little Duck](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Little_Duck "Little Duck"), the Tenno counterattacked both the Murex in space and the Condrix on Earth simultaneously. Since none of their weapons were capable of harming the Murex, a different strategy than simply attacking them was required. Once a Tenno ground team assaulted the Condrix and the Sentients guarding them and swiped the recon data before it could be transmitted back to the Murex, Little Duck and the alliance repurposed that friendly data, then sent it as a trojan horse to a Tenno team on-board a Murex. They then broadcast that harmful code into the Murex's head. While this didn't kill a Murex outright, it caused it immense pain, forcing it to retreat.

Fortunately for the Sentients, [General Sargas Ruk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/General_Sargas_Ruk "General Sargas Ruk") was unwilling to cooperate with the Tenno, convinced they were to blame for the catastrophe or that it was some kind of ruse and attacking Tenno and Sentients alike. Despite this unintentional assistance, the Tenno and their allies forced most of the Murex to retreat, ending the first skirmish with their victory.

### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War")

As the Sentients begin invading the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System") with a [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") appearing above [Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus") and crashing into [The Unum's](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Unum "Unum") Tower, Erra stands before an [[Ostron]] child and removes their Excalibur mask, telling them not to be afraid. He later approaches [[Kahl-175]] on the [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon"), thwarting his attempt to destroy a [Condrix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condrix "Condrix"), disarming his bomb and telling him not to be afraid.

As Ballas tortures the [[5 - Prelude To War/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]] by forcing her to watch [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Teshin|Teshin]] succumb to a [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil"), Erra fights Teshin as he tries to resist. After Teshin forcefully removes the veil, Erra grabs him by the head and deems him a lost cause, incapacitating him by crushing his helmet. Erra is then shot by the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"), who looks on in horror at the turn of events. Erra silently watches as Ballas banishes the Lotus and the Operator to the Void.

Without the Tenno, the Sentients win The New War. The Origin System is now ruled by Ballas' [Narmer Empire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer "Narmer"), with Erra as his second-in-command. Erra's [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon"), hybridized Sentient-Warframe abominations from The Old War named [Nira](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Nira "Archon Nira"), [Amar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Amar "Archon Amar"), and [Boreal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Boreal "Archon Boreal"), served to instill fear in dissidents and ensure loyalty throughout the system. The [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") works against Narmer, teaming up with [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]] to restore the Lotus and the Operator. Hunhow unsuccessfully tries to convince his son that Ballas is just using him. When he points out that Erra is acting no better than a [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") in servitude to the [[Orokin]], Erra rebuffs this claim, stating that Narmer has created peace and prosperity for the Sentients and swearing they will reclaim the Lotus and return her as Natah.

After the death of two Archons, the partial restoration of the Lotus, and the Operator's return, Ballas senses the Tenno and is enraged. Erra attempts to placate him, stating that he has won, and that every living thing in the Origin System, biological or not, worships him. Ballas however, sees this as hollow victory, as he believes that every denizen of the Sol System all wished for "that piss-warm comfort of being told what to think." Knowing that he cannot control the Tenno, Ballas orders Erra to recall the Sentient fleet and plots to take full control of the Lotus and make her kill her child.

When Ballas announces his plan to use the Sentient Mothership [Praghasa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa") to feed on the sun to destroy the Origin System and sacrifice his Narmer empire to fuel the ship's Void translation to the [Tau System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau_System "Tau System"), Erra realizes Ballas intends to doom the Sentients and defects. As soon as the Tenno dock onto Praghasa, Erra destroys a [Typholyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Typholyst "Typholyst") and holds up his hands to surrender. Instructing the Tenno to follow him, he guides them to the third and final Archon in hopes it will fully restore the Lotus. During the journey, however, damage from the battle causes the corridor to collapse; Erra sacrifices himself to save the Tenno, holding up the corridor long enough for them to safely pass. As he succumbs to the weight, he urges the Tenno to use the power of the third Archon's shard to save his sister and stop Ballas.
