# Lore
### [Chimera Prologue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chimera_Prologue "Chimera Prologue")
> [!quote] Ballas
> Ah... Only suffering under her eye. That she sees, she punishes. But... she gave me the gift of life! Why would I betray her? Fool! This... this is no gift! She stole my death! My perfect death. Such is her power... over me. Over the devil!
> We saw what we wanted, those devils and I. A lover... a mother! But now I know. She is neither. She's a hawk - calling with sparrow's song. A viper, blending into wood. And her venom... spreads not into flesh, but into your heart. Those devils! What has their great awakening accomplished - but the destruction of potential allies? Don't they see? Love must die-- --As Margulis did... when I sent her to the Jade light. And the Lotus... just some cloud in the sky, just some shape they imagined. Only the Sentient is real. Only Natah.
> 
> So... tell me devil. Do you understand what must be done? The Paracesis. The Sentient slayer... Hurry! Take this... the idea of it... it is the only way your war can end.

After chasing [The Man in The Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_The_Wall "The Man in The Wall") through [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua") and dropping down into the shaft inside the Reservoir into the same chamber during the Apostasy Prologue and the Second Dream, the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") enters a portal and spots [[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]]. The [[Orokin]] is revealed to be [[Sentient]]-[turned](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam "Amalgam") and is mourning for the loss of his "beautiful" Orokin body all while cursing [[Natah]] for her deception by stealing his _perfect death_. As he rambles, his eye suddenly glows, which he claims to be Natah's vision. He eventually offers his sword  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paracesis.png/32px-Paracesis.png?f1231)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) [Paracesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis), which he calls the "Sentient slayer", to the Tenno in preparation for war with the Sentients. As they grab the sword, Ballas abruptly turns to face them as Natah's whisper for the Tenno can be heard.

### [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Quest\) "Erra (Quest)")

Ballas was leashed by [[Erra]] while the Sentient was discussing the war with his sister. He confirmed that the Orokin are truly gone, the Corpus and Grineer are at war against each other, and the Tenno are her enemy.

### [The Maker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Maker "The Maker")

As [[Erra]] urges [[Natah]] to provide a spark from [the Mother's](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa") fire within her to re-ignite the one aboard the ship, she refuses out of concern for the Mother. She then shows doubts in Erra's counters and remembers him supposedly being killed by the [[5 - Prelude To War/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]] and the Tenno. Erra objects and states that she remembers wrong and will need time to completely heal from what the [Makers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") had done to her. Natah is not convinced and points at leashed Ballas asking if he is one of the Makers.

Before she can further question them, Erra suddenly grabs and throws Natah at the pedestal behind him, where she is trapped in a field of energy, thereby burning her up entirely instead of only providing the spark. Erra tells her to "finish the war" and drops Ballas' leash. Revealing that his imprisonment, as well as his monologue in Chimera Prologue, was a deceptive act, Ballas stands as he and Erra silently watch Natah.
### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War")
> [!quote] Ballas
> My vision was only ever comprehensible to myself. My ambition beyond the grasp of... 'people'. Thus I have always been alone. Your death changes nothing.

Ballas forces the burned and weak [[Natah]] to watch [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Teshin|Teshin]] succumb to the effects of a [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil") as he fights [[Erra]], revealing his plan to brainwash the masses. Once the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") arrives, they look on in horror at their weakened mother figure as Ballas kisses her and steals what appears to be the last of her power/life essence. A [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") portal then opens and drags in Natah's body; as the Operator tries to hold on to the rest of her, Ballas steals their  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paracesis.png/32px-Paracesis.png?f1231)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) [Paracesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) and uses it to cut off her hand and stab the Operator, dragging both into the portal where the Tenno is imprisoned within their own history.

Without the Tenno, the [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") take over the Origin System with Ballas as the de-facto leader of [[Narmer]], using the Veils to defame the Tenno and twist the truth into his favor. The [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") works against Narmer, teaming up with [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]] to destroy Erra's [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon") and use their [power over restoration](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard "Archon Shard") to revive the [[5 - Prelude To War/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]], while also bringing back the Operator in the process.

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/BallasVeil.png/300px-BallasVeil.png?7c4f7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:BallasVeil.png)

Ballas under the influence of a Narmer Veil

Ballas learns of the restoration of Lotus and is enraged, his fury overloading and remotely detonating the veils on his nearby [Narmer Deacons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Deacon "Narmer Deacon"). Erra attempts to placate him, stating that he has won and that every living thing in the Origin System, biological or not, worships him. Ballas however, sees this as hollow victory, as he believes that all beings in the System wished to be controlled. Knowing that he cannot control the Tenno, Ballas orders Erra to recall the Sentient fleet and plots to lure and reclaim the Lotus. Ballas then uses the Sentient Mothership [Praghasa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa") to feed on the sun, revealing his twisted plan to destroy the Origin System and sacrifice his Narmer empire to fuel his escape to the [Tau System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau_System "Tau System"). Erra, realizing that Ballas intends to doom the Sentients as well, defects and sacrifices himself to guide the Tenno to the third and final Archon.

The Operator and the Lotus confront Ballas. As Ballas targets the Lotus, the Operator push her out of the way and intercept the hit, allowing Ballas to steal and absorb the final Archon shard before commanding her to kill the Tenno. After a long battle, and wrenching the Lotus out of Ballas' control, Ballas steals the Operator's Paracesis and stabs the Lotus. As Ballas appears to gain the upper hand, the Operator jumps him and forces a Narmer Veil on him, deluding him into kissing "[[Margulis]]" and allowing the Lotus to retake her power, putting an end to Ballas once and for all. His death destabilizes the Narmer threat and allows the Origin System to slowly recover, yet loyalists remain eager to fill the power vacuum left behind by their martyred leader.
