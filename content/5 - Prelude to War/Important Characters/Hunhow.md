# Lore
### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War")

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Untitled2_20230205135126.png/300px-Untitled2_20230205135126.png?56db3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Untitled2_20230205135126.png)

Hunhow's tomb as shown during The New War.

After repeated failures to eradicate the Tenno, Hunhow has seemingly given up on this pursuit and remains in his tomb in [Uranus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Uranus "Uranus"). Developing sentimentality, he patiently rots and awaits for death.

Following the collapse of the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System") and the rise of [Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer "Narmer"), the [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") seeks to revive the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") and visits Hunhow's tomb. He initially responded in condescension, but was amused by the notion of an alliance and was unwilling to allow his daughter to become a puppet to [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas")' schemes. Hunhow relinquished information on the [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon")' ability to revive Sentients as well as their strengths and weaknesses, and gave the Drifter his bow  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Nataruk.png/32px-Nataruk.png?4b210)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nataruk) [Nataruk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nataruk) to combat them as well as have the [Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stalker "Stalker") support them on standby.

During the hunt for the Archons, Hunhow attempted to reason with his son [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)"), referring to Ballas as a parasite that is merely using him and the sentients and that Erra is acting no better than a [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") in servitude to [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin"). However, Erra rebuffed that the Sentients lives are better under Narmer's rule.
