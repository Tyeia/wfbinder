# Lore
### [Chimera Prologue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chimera_Prologue "Chimera Prologue")

Following [The Man in The Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_The_Wall "The Man in The Wall") to [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua") and falling into the same chamber where the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") encountered the Lotus and [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") in the Apostasy Prologue, the Operator enters a portal where they discover Ballas, now [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient")-turned, cursing the Lotus for her deception, by stealing his _perfect death_. As the Operator overhears his rambling, his eye suddenly glows, which he implies it to be the Lotus' vision. He eventually relinquishes his sword  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paracesis.png/32px-Paracesis.png?f1231)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) [Paracesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis), which he calls the "Sentient slayer", to the Tenno in preparation for war with the Sentients. As the Tenno grabs the sword, Ballas abruptly turns to face them and the Lotus' whisper calling for the Tenno can be heard.

### The Jovian Concord

_Main article: [Fragments/Partnership](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fragments/Partnership "Fragments/Partnership")_

The Sentients manipulate [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V") from behind the scenes and force him to create the [Amalgams](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam "Amalgam"), [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer")/[Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") hybridized with [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") powers. These new units, along with their combat data against the Tenno, would further serve the Sentients in their preparation for [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War").

During the mission to kill the [Ropalolyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ropalolyst "Ropalolyst"), Natah informs the Tenno them that the belief that she spared them as an act of mercy was false: rather that she was forced into doing so by the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin"), and that they were both "imprisoned in Lua's belly", shedding light on the events of the [Apostasy Prologue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Apostasy_Prologue "Apostasy Prologue") as a result. Natah also addresses that she "has seen the wall's other face", implying that during her time in her chambers on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"), that the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") affected her as well with [The Man in The Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_The_Wall "The Man in The Wall"). Furthermore, she establishes the Tenno as the evil entity for the "destruction of my people" following the Ropalolyst's defeat, and foreshadows the events to come in [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War").

### [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Quest\) "Erra (Quest)")

A flashback of the Old War shows Natah's original defection against the Sentients, and introduces for the first time Natah's own brother: [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)"). He is shown attacking a wounded female Dax soldier in a huge cave full of Reservoirs under the surface of Lua, when Natah herself, already turned into the Lotus, intervenes with a group of [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") (among which there is the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator")) ordering him to stop. Erra shows anger and sadness seeing how the "Makers" have made Natah, the **"Queen of the Aphids"**, turn against her own family, blaming them for what happened, and then urges her to choose between her true family and the parasites, the Tenno. She, however, states that she is not Natah but the Lotus, and then proceeds to make the Tenno attack Erra with their [Void Beams](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Beam "Void Beam").

The Operator is then brought back in the present, finding themselves aboard the [Sentient Mothership](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa"). In a situation similar to [Chimera Prologue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chimera_Prologue "Chimera Prologue"), the Operator assists to a dialogue between Natah, now returned to her original Sentient form, and Erra himself, who has apparently taken the lead of the Sentient army and keeps the Sentient-turned [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") on a leash. It is shown how Natah is suffering from memory lapses, still believing the Orokin to be alive and to be during the Old War. Erra makes Ballas confirm that the Orokin are indeed dead, the "bios" (presumably [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer")/[Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus")) are divided and only the Tenno remain a threat. Natah identifies the Tenno as the enemy and blames Ballas for creating them in order to face the Sentients. Erra however, points out how Natah now has "something our people never had before" and to be "stained by their wickedness". Natah reveals to be aware that the Operator is listening to their conversation, but both Sentients show not to be concerned by it. Erra then asks Natah to "sing" for all Sentients, calling them home and Natah proceeds to do so, knocking the Operator away and calling reinforcements for the upcoming [New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War").

### [The Maker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Maker "The Maker")

Erra is telling Natah that their mother is gone and urges her to finish the war as she still has "her fire". Natah notes that she remembers ordering the Tenno to attack Erra and he was apparently destroyed. Erra and Ballas appear to be taken aback by this before the former objects, stating that she remembers wrong and will need time to completely heal from what the Makers had done to her. Natah points at leashed Ballas and asks if he is one of the Makers.

Erra then suddenly grabs and throws Natah at the pedestal behind him, trapping her in an energy field. Erra tells her once more to finish the war and drops the leash from his hand. Revealing that Ballas's imprisonment was a deception, he stands as he and Erra silently stare at Natah.

### Operation: Orphix Venom

_Main article: [Operation: Orphix Venom](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Orphix_Venom "Operation: Orphix Venom")_

Following the Sentient's defeat at [Operation: Scarlet Spear](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Scarlet_Spear "Operation: Scarlet Spear"), Natah leads the second wave by teaching the machine-disabling [Orphix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orphix "Orphix") to disable Warframes. The Tenno counter by deploying [Necramechs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necramech "Necramech"), who are immune to the Orphix's pulse fields. As the war continues, Natah reveals to the Tenno her role in the Orphixes, but secretly transmits a code to them that says "_**I AM DYING**_".

### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War")

[Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") tortures Natah/Lotus, who has been turned into a brittle husk following the events of The Maker, while forcing her to watch [Teshin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Teshin "Teshin") succumb to a [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil"). After presenting her weakened state to the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"), Ballas steals her powers and opens a Void portal that drags her in. The Operator grabs her hand but Ballas slices off her arm with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paracesis.png/32px-Paracesis.png?f1231)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) [Paracesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) before stabbing the Operator, banishing them both.

The [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System") falls to Ballas's [Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer "Narmer") Empire. The [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter"), having somehow arrived in the Origin System and recovered the Lotus, now a dying [Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon"), teams up with [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow") who desires not to see his daughter being used as a puppet. They steal [Archon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon") shards and deliver them to the Lotus, restoring her and eventually returning the Operator to the Origin System. After a long battle, they finally subdue Ballas and the Lotus steals back her power from him and ends his threat once and for all.

In the aftermath, the Lotus is conflicted with her personalities meshing between herself, Natah, and [Margulis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Margulis "Margulis"). The player can [choose](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus#Customization "Lotus") for her to revert to Natah, a Sentient that acts with decisiveness and strength; the Lotus, the protector of the Origin System that balances Margulis' compassion and Natah's strength; Margulis in her honor, a woman devoted to restoring the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System") with compassion and healing.
