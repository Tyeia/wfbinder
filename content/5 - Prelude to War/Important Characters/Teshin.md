# Lore
### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War")

As the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) invade the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System"), the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [[Tenno]], forming an unlikely alliance with the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus), push toward the fleet in the [Veil Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veil_Proxima "Veil Proxima"). On the [Railjack](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Railjack "Railjack"), Teshin instructs the Tenno to bring their ship close to the Sentient mothership and slingshots inside, but denies them entry due to the [Orphix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orphix "Orphix") fields.

Teshin drops in from the ceiling and destroys an [Aerolyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aerolyst "Aerolyst"). As he searches for the scattered Orphix fields, he tells [[Ordis]] that he doesn't need his help and orders him to switch off. Along the way, he discovers captured Grineer and Corpus, and later encounters a strange red projection that makes him slowly recall [reprimanding the Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_War_Within "The War Within") before shrugging off its effects.

After destroying all the Orphix and defeating their [Typholyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Typholyst "Typholyst") guardians, Teshin chooses not to call the Tenno but instead decides to scout ahead. After making the jump, Teshin is captured and forced to don a [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil"), causing him to become deluded into believing the Tenno killed his presumed lover, [Valeria](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valeria "Valeria"), while [[5 - Prelude to War/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]] tortures the [[5 - Prelude To War/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]] by forcing her to watch. As Teshin fights off [[Erra]] and his brainwashing, he eventually collapses and forces the veil off— possibly hinting to the Operator that the device can be disabled by force. In Teshin's final moments, Erra deems him a lost cause, grabbing him, crushing his helmet, and leaving him for dead.

In the aftermath of these events, Teshin falls into the void, presumably through the portal Ballas opens, and lands within Duviri, later becoming the Drifter's Mentor.

After the completion of The New War, Teshin is replaced by a grey hologram.
