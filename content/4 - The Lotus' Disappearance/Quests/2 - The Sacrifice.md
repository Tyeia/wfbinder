> A tortured vision sends the Operator on the hunt for a Warframe unlike any other.

> [!quote] Ballas
> If you could trade, would you? Surely. But all miracles...require sacrifice. For their life... yours.

The Tenno hears faint whispers of the recently abandoned [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus"). Her voice directs them towards her helmet within the [Orbiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter "Orbiter")'s [Personal Quarters](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter#Rooms "Orbiter"). As the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") touches the helmet, visions of a Warframe being restrained by [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") and [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") fighters flow in their mind, prompting them towards [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth") to investigate.

The Warframe in question,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Excalibur_UmbraIcon272.png/31px-Excalibur_UmbraIcon272.png?74cc1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Umbra) [Excalibur Umbra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Umbra), was out of sight — only his remains survived his fight with the Sentients. [Ordis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ordis "Ordis") attempts to recreate him in the [Foundry](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Foundry "Foundry") based on [scans](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scanner "Scanner") of his remains but lacks sufficient data to do so. Trace minerals on Umbra's  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Skiajati.png/32px-Skiajati.png?929c5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Skiajati) [Skiajati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Skiajati) Nikana suggest that more information can be found on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"). A visit to Lua yielded a [Vitruvian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vitruvian "Vitruvian") device found within a containment lab that belonged to Ballas. This Vitruvian contained recordings made by Ballas on his betrayal to the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") during [The Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Story_and_History#The_Old_War "Story and History") and the origin of [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes"). Uploading the Vitruvian into the ship's mainframe gave the ability to craft Excalibur Umbra. Upon equipping him in the [Arsenal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arsenal "Arsenal"), he immediately goes berserk and damages his own helmet to reveal an eye, then pins the Operator into a wall until they enter his mind using [Transference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Transference "Transference"). The Tenno learns of Umbra's origins as a personal punishment from Ballas upon a [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") soldier for spying on him during his late betrayal of the Orokin. The Dax soldier was subjected to injections of a [Helminth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth "Helminth") [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infestation "Infestation") strain to produce skin-grafted armor and modified organs, akin to the typical Warframe design process during the Old War.

The memory abruptly ends, and the Operator finds out that Umbra has escaped the ship, rejecting Transference. They chase down Umbra as more entries of the Vitruvian become unlocked by accessing his memories. He contains the key to the secrets of the Warframes themselves.

After multiple tries, the Operator eventually accesses Umbra's final memory: killing his own son [Isaah](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Isaah "Isaah") when the Infestation takes over his body and Ballas forces him to act through the modified Transference Bolt. The Operator intervenes in this event, telling Umbra that he doesn't have to relive this event in solitude, instead, they will face this grief together through the duality of mind and body. Umbra as the hand, the Operator as the soul.
> [!quote] Ballas
> And it was not their force of will — not their Void devilry — not their alien darkness... it was something else. It was that somehow, from within the derelict-horror, they had learned a way to see inside an ugly, broken thing — and take away its pain.

In this revelation, the minds of Umbra and the Operator are linked — Transference was successful. With this newfound power, the Operator and Umbra fend off a Sentient assault and return to Earth to face Ballas.

During the final confrontation, Umbra successfully stabs Ballas in the gut, defying Ballas' subjugation thanks to the Operator. After questioning him on the whereabouts of the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus"), she appears from the sky in a [Sentient battle form](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Natah "Natah"), taking Ballas with her as she returns back to her [Mother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa"). The Lotus seems to no longer be the motherly figure that the Tenno loved.
