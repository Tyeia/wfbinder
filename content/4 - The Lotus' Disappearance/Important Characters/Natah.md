# Lore
### [Apostasy Prologue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Apostasy_Prologue "Apostasy Prologue")

The Operator meets the [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]] on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"), but before they can speak, [[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]] suddenly appears and promises not to abandon "[[Margulis]]" again. The Lotus removes her helmet and holds his hand without question, and the pair vanish before the Operator. The Operator, now confused and alone, collects Lotus' helmet. With her absence, [[Ordis]] synthesizes her mission directives until they can figure out her whereabouts.

### [The Sacrifice](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Sacrifice "The Sacrifice")

The Operator searches for the now missing Lotus. They hear a voice in their Orbiter's personal quarters, which shows them a vision of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Excalibur_UmbraIcon272.png/31px-Excalibur_UmbraIcon272.png?74cc1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Umbra) [Excalibur Umbra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Umbra) being confronted by [[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]], a pack of [[Sentient]] [Mimic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mimic "Mimic") who are subsequently destroyed by Umbra, and an unusual and unique type of Sentient who kills Umbra.

It is later revealed that the unusual Sentient who vaporizes Umbra is none other than the Lotus herself, now in her true Sentient form, descending from the sky to rescue Ballas after he was fatally wounded by the Operator and Umbra. Shocked and confused, the Operator asks the Lotus what Ballas did to her, but the Lotus replies saying that Ballas did nothing as this is what she is before blasting off into the sky leaving behind a confused and troubled Operator.
