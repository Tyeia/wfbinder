**Vala Glarios** is a [[Corpus]] captain who serves [Parvos Granum](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parvos_Granum "Parvos Granum") and is embarking on a revenge mission against the [Tempestarii](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sevagoth/Main#Lore "Sevagoth/Main") Ghost Ship.

She later serves as Granum's right hand woman, and the leader of the [Sisters of Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos "Sisters of Parvos").

# Lore
### [Call of the Tempestarii](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Call_of_the_Tempestarii "Call of the Tempestarii")
> I was working in an exosuit when the Tempestarii destroyed Lucretia Platform. I drifted for days among the frozen fragments of my crew. My Sisters. I dreamed their dead, gaping mouths were [singing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sleeping_In_The_Cold_Below "Sleeping In The Cold Below") with me yet.

—Vala Glarios
Vala was the sole survivor of an attack from the Tempestarii, an Old War rescue Railjack, on the Lucretia space station. She drifted for days in her exosuit amongst the fragments of her dead crew, dreaming that her "sisters" were [singing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sleeping_In_The_Cold_Below "Sleeping In The Cold Below"), leading her to seek revenge against the ship, now lost to the [Void Storms](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Storm "Void Storm").

[[Cephalon Cy]] and the Tenno investigate Corpus scavengers in the [Veil Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veil_Proxima "Veil Proxima"), where they are greeted by Vala. Shortly after, the Tempestarii emerges, repelling both the Tenno and the Corpus. The Tenno escape to raid Corpus logs to find out what they know about the Tempestarii.

The Tenno respond to the Tempestarii's calls, appeasing it, and accidentally allowing Vala to capture it. The Tenno destroy Vala's beam generators before using the Tempestarii's ordnance to banish Vala into the Void, who swears to be avenged by her [sisters](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos "Sisters of Parvos"). She wakes up in the [Granum Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Granum_Void "Granum Void") and collapses, where she is greeted by [Parvos Granum](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parvos_Granum "Parvos Granum") who extends his golden hand to her.

### [Sisters of Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos "Sisters of Parvos")

[Parvos Granum](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parvos_Granum "Parvos Granum"), having desired for a reliable sister in the past, recruits Vala Glarios as his right-hand woman. She is put in charge of selecting and growing [Candidates](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Candidate "Candidate") for his new army, the Sisters of Parvos.
