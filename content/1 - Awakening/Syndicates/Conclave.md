> We were led astray. We forgot the Conclave, so when that new evil came, we were not prepared. This is how we failed the Orokin. You went into stasis, but not me. I searched the long path for redemption, for the kind of balance only The Conclave can create. Now I return, only to find the Tenno still herded like livestock. Horrors from beyond the Outer Terminus are coming. You must prepare. You must accept The Conclave. Let it be your teacher and I its humble guide. For true balance can only be found in the face of the ultimate enemy. Yourself.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ConclaveSigil.png/32px-ConclaveSigil.png?b6dad)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conclave) Conclave is a neutral [Syndicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate "Syndicate") led by the Conclave Master, [**Teshin Dax**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Teshin "Teshin"). Teshin resides within the Conclave Hall of each [Relay](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Relay "Relay"). He is the one responsible for the management of the Conclave [PvP](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/PvP "PvP") System, providing incentives through various rewards that can be earned through Conclave battles.
