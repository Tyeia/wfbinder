> No matter how far we reach into the stars New Loka knows it is Earth that has always been our home. Turning away from genetic tampering that has twisted the Grineer and the technological meddling that has corrupted Earth it is New Loka that fights for the repopulation of a world once abandoned.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NewLokaSigil.png/32px-NewLokaSigil.png?3f339)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/New_Loka) New Loka is one of the six faction [syndicates](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicates "Syndicates"), led by **Priestess [Amaryn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amaryn "Amaryn")**. New Loka preaches a belief of a pure and whole [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth") to which humanity can return, for as the result of the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin")'s aggressive attempts to terraform the planet, humanity has found itself scattered across the Origin System. New Loka seems to favor many of the Tenno weapons in their arsenal, as seen through the **Sancti** signature weapon set, and has a set of Warframe Augments dedicated to more supportive roles like Oberon, Trinity, and Equinox.
