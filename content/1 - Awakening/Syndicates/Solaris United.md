> Indentured workers to the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"); the indispensable backbone of the Corpus business model on [Venus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venus "Venus"). Manning factories, rail tractors and mines they work to pay off the artificial bodies (rigs) they bought in order to work.

> Risen from the ashes, Solaris United returns to free the Solaris people from their mental shackles and physical servitude. No being should have to work to own their own mind, their own body, their own future.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SolarisUnited1.png/32px-SolarisUnited1.png?9ee79)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United) Solaris United are a rebel group of **[Solari](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solari "Solari")** workers, seeking to liberate their fellow unhappy laborers who work for [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo") underground in [Fortuna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fortuna "Fortuna"). The Solari possess body augmentations that make them appear as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus)-machine hybrids, and are responsible for processing the underground network of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) terraformation coolant and refining it for use back on the scorched surface of [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis") on [Venus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venus "Venus").

## Notable Members
### [Eudico](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eudico "Eudico")

_Main article: [Eudico](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eudico "Eudico")_

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Eudico_2SH.png/250px-Eudico_2SH.png?d2116)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Eudico_2SH.png)

**Eudico** is the floor boss of Fortuna, and secretly goes by her stage-name [Vox Solaris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vox_Solaris_\(Syndicate\) "Vox Solaris (Syndicate)") during operations where she leads the resistance movement of **Solaris United**. She offers [Bounties](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bounty "Bounty") for players to undertake in Orb Vallis, which offers both Solaris Standing and various other rewards.

  

### [Rude Zuud](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rude_Zuud "Rude Zuud")

_Main article: [Rude Zuud](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rude_Zuud "Rude Zuud")_

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Zuud_2SH.png/250px-Zuud_2SH.png?b923c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Zuud_2SH.png)

**Rude Zuud** is a gunsmith whose trade centers around [Kitguns](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kitgun "Kitgun"), modular primary and secondary weapons that players can craft with different parts and stats as they desire, along with other weapons related services such as Renaming.



### [Legs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Legs "Legs")

_Main article: [Legs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Legs "Legs")_

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Legs_2SH.png/250px-Legs_2SH.png?ca305)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Legs_2SH.png)

**Legs** (formerly named Thursby) sells [MOA Companions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/MOA_\(Companion\) "MOA (Companion)") and the parts to create them, and can also help assemble [Hound Companions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hound_\(Companion\) "Hound (Companion)") (though parts must be sourced elsewhere). He also offers a small selection of robotic weapon blueprints.



### [The Business](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Business "The Business")

_Main article: [The Business](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Business "The Business")_

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/The_Business_2SH.png/250px-The_Business_2SH.png?6fc6a)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:The_Business_2SH.png)

**The Business** (**Biz**) works to preserve the wildlife on Venus, who are being driven to extinction by the Corpus. He sells the equipment necessary to find and capture the wildlife for [conservation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conservation "Conservation") as well as [fishing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fishing "Fishing") spears and baits for the Servofishes found in the Orb Vallis and [Companion Mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Companion_Mods "Companion Mods").

### [Smokefinger](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smokefinger "Smokefinger")

_Main article: [Smokefinger](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smokefinger "Smokefinger")_

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Smokefinger_2SH.png/250px-Smokefinger_2SH.png?352c4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Smokefinger_2SH.png)

**Smokefinger** works with [mining](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mining "Mining") equipment suited for the Orb Vallis.



### [Ticker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ticker "Ticker")

_Main article: [Ticker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ticker "Ticker")_

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Ticker_2SH.png/250px-Ticker_2SH.png?fbe0a)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Ticker_2SH.png)

**Ticker** allows the player to pay off Solaris NPCs' debts for rewards, in the form of [Debt-Bonds](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Debt-Bond "Debt-Bond"), which can be exchanged for Solaris United standing or Landing Craft decorations. She also has [Railjack](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Railjack "Railjack") [Crew](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Railjack/Crew "Railjack/Crew") for hire.

### [Vox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vox "Vox")

_Main article: [Vox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vox "Vox")_

**Vox** is [Eudico](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eudico "Eudico")'s alter ego serving as the voice and leader of Solaris United.

### [Solaris United Agent](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United_Agent "Solaris United Agent")

_Main article: [Solaris United Agent](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United_Agent "Solaris United Agent")_

The '_Solaris United Agent_ is placed outside of the Fortuna elevator in the [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis"), offering [Bounties](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bounty "Bounty") without needing to return to Fortuna. They will also appear upon capturing Corpus bases within the Vallis.
