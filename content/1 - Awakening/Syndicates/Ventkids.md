The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VentkidsIcon.png/32px-VentkidsIcon.png?70259)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ventkids) Ventkids are a group of children from [[Solaris United]] in [Fortuna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fortuna "Fortuna") who get by stealing parts from the [[Corpus]] and running [K-Drive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/K-Drive "K-Drive") races out in the [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis").
