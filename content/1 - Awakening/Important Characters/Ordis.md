**Ordis** is a [Cephalon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon "Cephalon") of [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") origin who serves aboard the [Orbiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter "Orbiter"). His visual form is that of a cracked hexahedron which emits radio wave-like ripples as he speaks.

Ordis assists the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno"), whom he refers to as "[Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator")". He manages the various systems of the Tenno's landing craft, and provides them with information in between missions.
# Lore
### General

Ordis is a damaged Cephalon programmed to assist the Tenno. He is mostly functional, though he exhibits symptoms of residual damage inflicted on him. This damage has erased portions of his memory, including his knowledge of the Old War.

Ordis' speech and demeanor are occasionally altered in momentary blips of rage and aggression. It is initially unclear whether this is a symptom of his damaged subsystems or whether it is a subsumed personality breaking free. These outbursts are markedly different from the usual calmness exhibited by other non-[Conclave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conclave "Conclave") Cephalons. After an outburst, Ordis is prone to correct himself.
### [Vor's Prize](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vor%27s_Prize "Vor's Prize")

After [Captain Vor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Captain_Vor "Captain Vor") shoots down the [Liset](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Liset "Liset") sent in to rescue the newly awakened Tenno, the Tenno encounters their original ancient [Liset](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Liset "Liset") (with Ordis onboard) impounded nearby. Ordis initially mistakes the Tenno for another Grineer soldier, but soon realizes their true identity and helps them escape.

As the Tenno is being familiarized with their surroundings, the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") instructs the Tenno to retrieve replacement components to restore the [Orbiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter "Orbiter")'s functions (to Ordis' delight, as this allows the Tenno to exact revenge on the Grineer and the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") for gutting him of his components). Along the way, the Tenno rescue a black market merchant named [Darvo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Darvo "Darvo"), who—while willing to help the Tenno remove the [Ascaris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ascaris "Ascaris") implanted by Vor—gets into a small squabble with Ordis due to Darvo's former ties with the Corpus.

### [Howl of the Kubrow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Howl_of_the_Kubrow "Howl of the Kubrow")

Upon hearing news of the Tenno's desire to obtain a [Kubrow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kubrow "Kubrow"), Ordis makes his distaste for them clear, viewing them as smelly, unhygienic creatures that threaten to soil the pristine interior of his ship. Over time however, Ordis becomes more accepting of the newly hatched Kubrow commenting that its barking and panting are "not displeasing".

### [The Limbo Theorem](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Limbo_Theorem "The Limbo Theorem")

When Ordis receives a series of mysterious, incomplete proofs, he initially refuses to look into it further but theorizes that the Tenno can decipher the equations by tapping into spaceborne [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") communication towers to collect additional proof fragments. As Ordis and the Tenno piece the proof fragments, Ordis realizes that the proof belonged to the operator of [a Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Limbo "Limbo") that could access [another dimension](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rift_Plane "Rift Plane") studied in his equations. Eagerly awaiting new discoveries, Ordis requests the Tenno to locate Limbo's components to further unravel the theorem.

After the Tenno locate and build the last of Limbo's components, Ordis promptly reads the next equation, only to discover that it was Limbo's last, having miscalculated his last jump. Disheartened, Ordis urges the Tenno to be more careful when they receive the Warframe for their own use.
