[[Entrati]]

The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [[Tenno]] are summoned to the surface of [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos") by servants [Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid "Loid") and [Otak](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Otak "Otak") and meet the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EntratiIcon.png/32px-EntratiIcon.png?cafef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati) [[Entrati]], an  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [[Orokin]] family that researches the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") but have now become partially infected by the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [[Infested]]. [Mother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mother "Mother"), leader of the Entrati, wishes to fix the [Heart of Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Heart_of_Deimos_\(Object\) "Heart of Deimos (Object)"), which powers all Void devices across the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System"), including Warframes. However, the Entrati are a dysfunctional family, with [Father](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Father "Father") refusing to lend his aid while [Daughter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Daughter "Daughter") and [Son](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Son "Son") are at odds.

Upon arriving at the Heart, a rogue [Necramech](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necramech "Necramech"), a combat platform that was used in [The Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Old_War "The Old War"), ambushes the Tenno and destroys it. This forces the Entrati family to put aside their differences and unite. With the Warframe losing functionality, they are brought aboard a loaner Necramech, repair the Heart, and destroy the rogue.
