[[Teshin]]

> Corpus incursions into Grineer territory lead to showdown with a forgotten rival.

Following [Captain Vor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Captain_Vor "Captain Vor")'s defeat, a force of [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") led by one of [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo")'s [Treasurers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Treasurer "Treasurer") raids his camps in search of [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") technology: a [Mod](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod "Mod") Segment. As the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") seek to claim the Mod Segment first, they are confronted by [Teshin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Teshin "Teshin"), a [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") warrior that survived the Orokin Era, who decided to witness the return of the Tenno. Showing disdain toward the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus"), he proceeds to capture the Tenno.

Recognizing that the Lotus sealed away the Tenno's memories of [The Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Old_War "The Old War"), Teshin decides to train them in the way of Mods to enhance their Warframes and weapon. The training is soon interrupted by Corpus; Teshin allows himself to be captured so the Tenno can escape with the Mod Segment.

The Tenno upgrade themselves further and storm the Corpus Ship to rescue Teshin. However, the Lotus instead orders his capture, then destroy the ship. Teshin converses with the Lotus, conceding that The Old War was necessary, but the Tenno's memories of their history will be regained within time; for now, they need a mentor and a Lotus to guide them.
