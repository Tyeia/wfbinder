The **Grineer** (![|20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_G.svg?ea196)![|20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_R.svg?3ed52)![|20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_I.svg?1e6c3)![|20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_N.svg?b21ac)![20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_E.svg?21785)![|20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_E.svg?21785)![|20](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Grineer_R.svg?3ed52)) is one of the major [Factions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Factions "Factions") of the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Star_Chart "Star Chart"). They are the first enemies encountered by newly [awakened](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Awakening "Awakening") [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno").

The expansion of their militaristic, violent Empire across the Origin System through the raiding or conquest of peaceful colonies serves as the primary motivator of the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus")' call to her slumbering warriors.

[Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Grineer) take increased damage from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgImpactSmall64.png/32px-DmgImpactSmall64.png?27a4e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Impact_Damage) [Impact](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Impact_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage). Their elite [Kuva Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Kuva_Grineer) forces however are resistant to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgHeatSmall64.png/32px-DmgHeatSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage).
# Overview
As the products of ancient, half-remembered [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") technology, Grineer are produced rather than born, and in industrial quantities along with their weapons and equipment. Each one is genetically defective to some degree, their genome both originally stunted by their creators, and further damaged by the invasive cloning procedures they themselves have been using to birth each generation in the centuries after the fall of the Orokin. As a result, they have a life cycle that is limited to only a fraction of a normal human lifespan and are prone to a host of degenerative disorders such as decaying limbs and skin or wildly varying levels of intelligence and sapience. However, the genetic defects have often undone the original genetic conditioning, allowing more human-like behaviors, ranging from lack of absolute loyalty to the Queens, to developing a sense of dignity. They compensate for this by the widespread use of crude cybernetic prosthetics and robust augmentations. Indeed, it could be argued that the Grineer are better at fixing broken bodies than they are at creating ones that function to begin with. Most of their equipment, weapons, and armor are technologically crude but undeniably effective, and many Tenno have fallen because they underestimated the firepower of Grineer machines.

In other words, Grineer can be considered as a form of cyborgs. Most of them have been altered with mechanical prosthetics and crude cybernetic augmentations, with higher ranking Grineer having robust cybernetic augmentations.

# Lore and History
### Origins

The Grineer were once a caste of genetically modified, purpose-bred clones of the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") Empire. Programmed to obey their masters, Grineer slaves were tasked to perform manual labor, gathering resources for the advancement of the Empire.

During [The Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Old_War "The Old War"), the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") were desperate to defeat their [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") assailants, so a new breed of Grineer was created using the gene sample of an individual who had managed to defeat one. These militarized Grineer would be deployed in the Orokin's war efforts but proved futile against the Sentient faction's strategies and defenses.

Eventually, the [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes") were created and led the Orokin to victory, only to be betrayed by the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") that controlled these war-suits. After the collapse of the Orokin Empire, Grineer started to congregate to take over territory and technology once belonged to the Empire. At some point in time, the Grineer encountered Orokin twins that helped them fight against remnant Orokin in the Origin System who wanted to restore the Empire. In awe of their close kinship, the Grineer adopted them to be their [queens](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens"), believing that they attained "Something more than a whole / A true understanding / Of each other soul", a quality that they aspire for.

### Present-Day

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Grineer1.jpg/300px-Grineer1.jpg?6776c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Grineer1.jpg)

Grineer [Lancer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lancer "Lancer")

Their nominal "homeworld" is a [feral, overgrown](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Forest "Grineer Forest") [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"), over which they hold only the weakest measure of control through their attempts to poison the planet's ecology to make it more suitable for themselves. Despite their efforts, [some areas of the planet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon") remain completely outside of their control, such as the [Ostron](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron "Ostron") safe haven and trading hub of **[Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus")** or the secret [Steel Meridian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Steel_Meridian "Steel Meridian") enclave known as [Iron Wake](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Iron_Wake "Iron Wake"). Several other planets fall under more complete control of the Grineer, such as the [planet-wide factories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Shipyard "Grineer Shipyard") of [Ceres](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ceres "Ceres") and [Sedna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sedna "Sedna"), the [underwater research laboratories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Sealab "Grineer Sealab") of [Uranus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Uranus "Uranus"), and the [occupied desert cities](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Settlement "Grineer Settlement") of [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"). They also have complete control over the space regions around the [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth_Proxima "Earth Proxima") and [Saturn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Saturn_Proxima "Saturn Proxima"), as well as having outposts even in the outermost location of the Origin System, called [Veil Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veil_Proxima "Veil Proxima"). Their [main fortress](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Fortress "Kuva Fortress"), the home of the [Twin Queens](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Twin_Queens "Twin Queens"), is located on an asteroid constantly moving thanks to [Fomorian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fomorian "Fomorian") engines, and is heavily fortified.

They are units acting on ultimate xenophobia, fueled by genetic madness, and a burning hatred of anything and anyone not like themselves and have absolute loyalty, not dissimilar from religious fanaticism, to their Queens, although very few have actually been granted the permission to see them. In the end, should they succeed, there will only be Grineer left alive in the Origin System.

In battle, the Grineer use heavy [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") coupled with attrition and ambush tactics. While they are fairly weak individually, they are a potent threat in groups, able to lay down withering streams of fire at all ranges from behind cover. Specialist troops use more advanced weapons, and many are even issued comparatively advanced reactive armor and cybernetics to give them an edge.

The armor on an average Grineer deserves special mention because it makes them one of the toughest factions to face in a battle. Coupled with several elemental resistances and the fact that even the relatively common [Elite Lancer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Elite_Lancer "Elite Lancer") is heavily armored makes them extremely durable, with their Medium and Heavy units becoming absolute bullet sponges at higher levels. Furthermore, their special corps tasked to defend their positions on space and to assault enemy ships such as [Railjacks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Railjack "Railjack") have even higher defenses. The Grineer also make notable use of rudimentary teleportation, either to transport [small strike forces on daring missions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Siphon "Kuva Siphon") even within hostile territory held by the rival [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"), or on an individual level: their [Flameblades](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Flameblade "Flameblade") utilize it to blink close to the enemy for a vicious melee attack, and [Commanders](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Commander "Commander") employ an ability similar to the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SwitchTeleport130xWhite.png/32px-SwitchTeleport130xWhite.png?d94e0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Switch_Teleport) [Switch Teleport](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Switch_Teleport) of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/LokiIcon272.png/31px-LokiIcon272.png?1a214)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loki) [Loki](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loki) to leave the victim confused and in the midst of the Grineer troops. Infamous Grineer officers who utilize teleportation are [Tyl Regor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tyl_Regor "Tyl Regor"), who blinks constantly to evade and confuse the player in his battle, and [Captain Vor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Captain_Vor "Captain Vor"), who uses teleportation in battle to keep a distance from the player (though the latter's abilities might originate from his [Janus Key](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Keys "Void Keys")).

Although notoriously known for their short lives, some [selected Grineer units](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Larvling "Kuva Larvling") can be granted from their Queens the gift of immortality, in the form of the [Old Blood](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_\(Resource\) "Kuva (Resource)"), after facing a Warframe in battle. These super-soldiers called [Kuva Liches](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Lich "Kuva Lich"), inherit some of the abilities of their "progenitor", as well as possessing a high level of adaptability and intelligence, making them by far the most powerful Grineer units to face.

# Culture
Militarism defines all aspects of Grineer society, with little value placed upon its lowliest subjects beyond their service to the Queens. With a combat doctrine based in overwhelming numbers, sacrifice is to be expected of the lower ranks in completion of their objectives; even the lowly [Drudges](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drudge "Drudge") are known to charge Warframes with little more than their tools in an entirely suicidal display of the Grineer's expectations of fervent loyalty. Any trait amongst the lower ranks that does not serve the Grineer's goals, such as creativity, are rarely seen.

Even with a genetic predisposition to fealty for their Queens, automated drones like [Regulators](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Regulator "Regulator") serve as loudspeakers for broadcasting propaganda from the Grineer leadership for troops in the field. Within the Kuva Fortress, broadcasts featuring the [Worm Queen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Worm_Queen "Worm Queen") herself are televised to their royal guards.

However, even their genetically-enforced allegiances are not infallible; a there exists several known 'defects' that can occur during a Grineer's creation that may lead to them lacking loyalty to their Queens. This defect is apparent enough that a sizable force made up of such Grineer organize under [Steel Meridian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Steel_Meridian "Steel Meridian"). Other defects can be seen in the [Kavor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kavor_Defector "Kavor Defector"), whose pacifistic tendencies affront Grineer values to the point that [General Sargas Ruk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/General_Sargas_Ruk "General Sargas Ruk") leads efforts to exterminate them entirely. Even amongst the seemingly mindless hordes of Ghouls, some instigators have been known to rally their peers against [Councilor Vay Hek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Councilor_Vay_Hek "Councilor Vay Hek").

### Language

_Main article: [Grineer Language](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Language "Grineer Language")_

The Grineer speak and write in a heavily corrupted form of English, intermixing grunts, simplified terms, and onomatopoeia into their writing and naming conventions as well as a form of substitution that highlights their degenerative speech impediments.

They use their own unique set of barcode letters with dot-matrix-styled 'cuts' made along its edges (left for letters, right for numbers), to various depths. A dot present under each letter's bottom-right corner helps to determine the correct orientation and makes it easy to tell if the text has been flipped or rotated.

In many cases, [Digital Extremes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Digital_Extremes "Digital Extremes") did not intend for this script to be translated and read by players. Therefore, many cases of text longer than a few words (such as the readouts on monitors) tend to be random letters strung together and occasionally flipped or rotated. Since the dot-marks are supposed to be on the bottom-right, it is easy to tell when this has been done.

#### Naming

The names of Grineer soldiers that make up the bulk of their forces seem to borrow the name of their original genetic template, followed by a numerical designation of up to three numbers. An exception to this is the names of Grineer in leadership roles or are of some notoriety, such as [Lech Kril](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lieutenant_Lech_Kril "Lieutenant Lech Kril"), [Dr. Tengus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dr._Tengus "Dr. Tengus"), and [Kela De Thaym](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kela_De_Thaym "Kela De Thaym").

# Tech & Weaponry
Fittingly for a military power like the Grineer, they have developed a wide variety of weapons and equipment used in their ceaseless conflicts with the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno"), [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"), and [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested"). This ranges from [simple pistols](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kraken "Kraken") to heavier [rifles](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Karak "Karak"), [shotguns](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sobek "Sobek"), and [machine guns;](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gorgon "Gorgon") to [missile launchers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ogris "Ogris"), [flamethrowers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ignis "Ignis"), [melee weapons with superheated surfaces](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Cleavers "Dual Cleavers"), [hydraulically-assisted axes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Manticore "Manticore"), [hammers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brokk "Brokk"), and [flail-like whips with hooked grinding blades](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Atterax "Atterax").

Grineer weaponry are largely conventional or industrial in operation. Grineer aesthetics tend to employ a curved, rounded, and heavily plated spot-weld look. This is indicative of the extensive use of tough, temperature, and shock-resistant [Ferrite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ferrite "Ferrite") and [Alloys](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alloy_Plate "Alloy Plate") used in Grineer construction and manufacturing. As opposed to weapons built by the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"), Grineer weaponry are not laboriously cast or machined for precision, but instead pressed from molds, quickly filed down, and assembled by the billions, designed with loose tolerances to streamline the process. Many Grineer weapons also [lack full stocks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hind "Hind"), and instead rely on their wielder's augmented strength and heavy armor to absorb the recoil.

Grineer weaponry are generally not designed to be used _against_ the Grineer since they are simply too heavy or large for other races to utilize without the aid of something like a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe"). They are also less effective against the Grineer since they are primarily engineered to deal  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgPunctureSmall64.png/32px-DmgPunctureSmall64.png?59103)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Puncture_Damage) [Puncture](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Puncture_Damage) damage for use against [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") shield systems and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgSlashSmall64.png/32px-DmgSlashSmall64.png?bab47)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) [Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) damage for use against the [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested"). Of course, [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") with their adaptation abilities can efficiently resist to almost every attack of a Grineer weapon, and are for this reason studied and researched on, in order to develop even more advanced technology.

Despite their manufacturing processes being centered around mass-production, Grineer engineers are fiendishly clever in incorporating unusual and unorthodox ideas and new technologies into their weaponry, even if such technology is something they are unfamiliar with. An example of this can be seen in [Captain Vor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Captain_Vor "Captain Vor")'s  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Seer.png/32px-Seer.png?5f753)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seer) [Seer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seer); this is a long-ranged pistol with sniper optics that incorporates [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") technology. Other examples include the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JatKittag.png/32px-JatKittag.png?23945)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jat_Kittag) [Jat Kittag](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jat_Kittag) - a Grineer warhammer that incorporates a series of jet engines to allow a more destructive impact.

Despite the best efforts of [the Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus"), the Grineer have also developed primitive [microwave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nukor "Nukor") and [plasma-aided projectile weapons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kohm "Kohm"), as well as [a rifle that fires guided projectiles](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Buzlok "Buzlok"). These weapons are reserved for the elite of the elites among the Grineer, such as the [Nightwatch corps](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nightwatch_Corps "Nightwatch Corps") and the guards of [their Queens' fortress](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Fortress "Kuva Fortress").

The most powerful Grineer weapons are the ones used by the Kuva Liches, their super-soldiers, inheriting a percentage of elemental damage based on their progenitor. Some are just [special variants of normal Grineer weapons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_\(Variant\) "Kuva (Variant)"), others are totally unique and with high-destructive power, such as a [bow that shoots explosive arrows](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Bramma "Kuva Bramma") or a [heavy weapon normally impossible to use on field](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Ayanga "Kuva Ayanga").
