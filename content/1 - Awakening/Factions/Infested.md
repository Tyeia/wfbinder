The **Infested** are a multitude of deformed, twisted creatures, originated from the mutation of normal living beings who have been taken over by the parasites of the _**Infestation**_, a microscopic parasitic techno-organic pathogen. The majority of Infested are former [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") Crewmen or [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") Lancers, or "Mutalist" versions of Corpus robotic proxies. The eldest individuals, the Infested [Ancients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested#Ancients), are creatures overtaken by the plague so long ago that their original form is unrecognizable, perhaps dating back to the Old War itself.

[Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Infested) take increased damage from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgSlashSmall64.png/32px-DmgSlashSmall64.png?bab47)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) [Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgHeatSmall64.png/32px-DmgHeatSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage). The mutant [Infested Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Infested_Deimos) on [Cambion Drift](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cambion_Drift "Cambion Drift"), [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"), and in [Shrine Defense](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shrine_Defense "Shrine Defense") on [Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus"), are instead vulnerable to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgBlastSmall64.png/32px-DmgBlastSmall64.png?7a683)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Blast_Damage) [Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Blast_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgGasSmall64.png/32px-DmgGasSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage) [Gas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage), but resist  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgViralSmall64.png/32px-DmgViralSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage) [Viral](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage).

# Overview
Mentioned and referenced through in-game text or by the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus"), the Infestation turns organisms such as the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") and [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"), as well as machines and robotics, into what is known to the Tenno as the "Infested."

In the past, the Infestation, known as the Technocyte Virus, was utilized by the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") to combat the [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient"). Much of the game's lore is presented in an ambiguous fashion; however it can be inferred that the virus seems to have no prejudices as to whom it affects, be it Grineer, Corpus, robotic or the normal fauna of a planet; only the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") possessing void abilities and their [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes"), as well as the Sentients themselves, appear to be truly immune to the technocyte virus. As such, Warframes were often used to quell Infested outbreaks in the past. Not even the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") themselves are immune to it, as shown by the [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati "Entrati") family, although they managed to keep their consciousness intact through special treatments.

The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Mire.png/32px-Mire.png?a4c1e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mire) [Mire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mire) references an event called the "[**Great Plague**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Great_Plague "Great Plague")" in its description. The Great Plague may be a reference to the Infestation itself or a period after its creation. The Mire's resemblance to the Infested suggests a connection between the Great Plague and the Infested themselves. However, it is the only mention of "Great Plague" throughout the game and could be speculated to also be the "Plague Year" of 1999, as mentioned in Albrecht Entrati's codex entries and by Loid in the Lotus Eaters.

Despite the Infestation being highly dangerous and mutagenic, some beings have learned to live alongside the Infested, such as the [Myconians](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mycona_Colony "Mycona Colony"), who harvest and trade Infested matter, and feral [Kavats](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Feral_Kavat "Feral Kavat"), who reside in infested [Derelicts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Derelict "Orokin Derelict") and are able to consume Infested matter without harm.

# Lore & History
### Pre-Orokin

The earliest known instance of the Infestation is the [Techrot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot "Techrot") being used in 1997 in an attempt to produce [games](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Correspondence "Suspicious Correspondence") in [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania"), and the Hollvanian government colluding with HitMaker Music to harness the Technocyte to make [clones](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Technocyte_Coda "Technocyte Coda") of the [On-lyne](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/On-lyne "On-lyne") band members.

Further outbreaks are known to have happened past this time period and before the [Radiation Wars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radiation_Wars "Radiation Wars") all the way up until the Orokin era. Outbreaks would begin to cease due to Orokin intervention and culling of the Technocyte Virus until it was virtually eradicated from the Origin System.

### Early-Orokin

Not much is known about how the Orokin treated and dealt with the Infestation before they weaponized it for The Old War; however, what is known is that the Orokin had beat back the Infestation, with it usually only Infesting minor outer colonies and colonies of peasants that served the Orokin.

### Old War Era

During The Old War, the Orokin Empire was desperate in defeating their [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") adversaries, so they rebooted Infestation research to find ways to weaponize the parasite against their enemy. Once notable attempt was the use of bio-bombs filled with Infested matter. During the War, the Sentients would attempt to invade [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"), a strategically important planetoid. A bio-bomb that contained Infested matter was set off, stopping the Sentient invasion, but releasing an full-on Infestation on Deimos in the process.

### Tenno Era

After the rise of the Tenno, the Infestation has been somewhat contained to the [Dark Sectors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dark_Sectors "Dark Sectors"), however there are still some [outbreaks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invasion#Infestation_Outbreaks "Invasion") that happen from time to time.

There has been one event where the Infestation displayed a more sophisticated and advanced form of assimilation, known as Arlo, the "Emissary of Eris." After arising on Eris, the mute Arlo would gather a congregation of believers by spreading his healing word and healing any ailments he came across. However, many of his followers were assimilated into the Infested hivemind, and would willingly expose themselves to the Infestation. This would lead to the creation of an army of Zealots who attempt to invade the System.

# Weaponry
More so than any other weapon category, weapons involving the Infestation vary significantly. They range dramatically in both design and function, from [Corpus mining equipment that feature infestation altering their functions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mutalist_Quanta "Mutalist Quanta"), to [Grineer attempts to weaponize infested bio-toxins](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acrid "Acrid"). Most, however, are weapons that are, in and of themselves, created from either living or inactive-but-preserved infested tissue; these range from [swords created from infested bio-growth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mire "Mire") to [pistols which smother opponents in broiling clouds of toxic infested liquids](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Embolist "Embolist"). The most unusual Infested weapons seem to be multi-part organisms, ranging from [slugthrowers that hurl toxin shards with muscular impulse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tysis "Tysis") to [launchers that hurl a living infested bio-growth at the target like a grenade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Torid "Torid"). The most extreme cases are weapons that can [project streams of viral bio-energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Phage "Phage") or [crackling corrosive energy via neural impulse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synapse "Synapse").

Infested weapons are often hideous to behold, visibly pulsing and squirming in their use. Even ones crafted from inactive infested growth are often visually grotesque, with edges made from chitin and bone and flecked with veins that thrum with organic fluids. Ranged weapons using infested material often very literally devour their ammunition. In many ways, Infested weaponry indicate how desperate many factions have become involving the Infested, with the Grineer attempting to fight poison with poison, the Corpus finding the infested altering their existing equipment as [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V")'s enhanced Mutalist strains consume mechanical constructs, and the Tenno trying to find new ways to do their job with the new tools as the Infestation spreads – meaning that the Infested are a great threat, but also promise opportunities to any who can find a way to exploit them against their enemies.

# Biology
According to [Cephalon Cordylon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Cordylon "Cephalon Cordylon"), Infested tissue can be easily grown, thrives in any environment, and is as easy to manipulate for the creation tools and weapons as any non-organic material. Once a host has been infected, the first signs of infection are usually the appearance of small, benign skin growths, usually warts or tumors.

### Infested Hivemind

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Trachons.png/300px-Trachons.png?b1be4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Trachons.png)

**[Trachons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Trachons "Trachons")** - An organ found on Infested Ships believed to facilitate communication within the Infested Hivemind.

> GET OUT - I AM THEIR VOICE - THIS IS JUST THE BEGINNING - NO ONE LEAVES - LOST LITTLE THING - JOIN US - EMBRACE US, WE ARE YOUR FLESH - NOW WE HAVE BECOME IT
  
—The voice of the [Jordas Golem](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jordas_Golem "Jordas Golem")

The Hivemind is the collective consciousness of the Infested strains. And despite popular belief, there is not a single Hivemind, but rather multiple, which one Hivemind branching into more when some Infestation branches off enough and then grows enough to form a new Hivemind. As a Hivemind, instead of it being a single mind that controls all the Infested within it, it is the collection of the Infested's minds all merged into one, sharing thoughts, memories, feelings, and so on. The Hivemind combines the consciousness and intelligence of everyone in it, with each addition to it serving as another neuron added into the overarching brain of the Infestation.

The Hiveminds of the Infestation give many traits to the mutated horde, including:

#### Shared Knowledge

Per being a hive mind, all members of the Infestation have access to the collective knowledge of the group. As such, any knowledge learned by a single Infested is immediately hared and utilized by the rest of the horde. This allows the Infestation to adapt and solve problems far more efficiently than any single Infested ever could.

#### Near Instant and Extremely Efficient Coordination and Communication

The Hivemind allows all members of the Infestation to communicate near instantly and seamlessly, allowing for them to coordinate attacks and other things with extreme speed and efficiency.

#### Collective, Purpose-Driven Behavior

The Hivemind allows them to think as one, therefore, all of the Infested are unified in their goals and purposes. This allows them to further optimize resources, completely eliminate the chance of infighting, and more efficiently solve problems, allocate resources, and more.

#### Lack of Individual Autonomy

Due to the Hivemind dictating the whole's actions and even thoughts, there is a lack of individuality among the Infested, with all those taken by it always using "we" instead of "I" or "our" instead of "my" and similar wording when referring to oneself. Furthermore, this collective consciousness also suppresses and even overrides individual thoughts and beliefs that the Hivemind doesn't want, silencing any dissenters from going against the whole. This individuality can be retained via special means.

  

The effect of the Hivemind can be seen in-game when encountering Infested with the near-impossibility of utilizing stealth in an Infested-controlled area; if even one unit in the area detects an enemy, every single other Infested in the area will become alerted to their presence and begin hunting them down, even from across the entire [tileset](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tileset "Tileset").

  
The ability to form, use, and access the Hivemind may be both a biological ability and a telepathical one. With the Infested nanites communicating telepathically and the life form itself eventually producing organs that facilitate better and more efficient communication. This is further explained by numerous resources found throughout the Origin System, often located in Infested Ships or within Infested entities themselves that have been described to exist solely for the purpose of Hivemind communication.

### Reproduction and Spread

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/InfestedSpores.jpg?87ee2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:InfestedSpores.jpg)

Infested spores as seen during missions

The Infestation primarily infests or corrupts its victims through swarms of airborne  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NanoSpores.png/32px-NanoSpores.png?5a439)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nano_Spores) [Nano Spores](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nano_Spores), possibly the same kind of found floating around in the air on Infested tilesets as moving colonies of orange particles. However, no enemy or ally can ever become Infested during gameplay. The only exception is during [Defection](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Defection "Defection") missions, where it is possible to witness the only current "infestation" of a target in real-time; allowing a Grineer Defector to die to the poisons in the air will eventually cause them to sprout a large Infested mass around them, which explodes in a horrifying display of light gore and rot to reveal a uniquely colored [Carrion Charger](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Charger#Variants "Charger"). The transformation to these combat forms is seemingly quick, likely due to the Defectors weakened state due to the exposure to the spores for extended periods and possibly due to the Grineer's already weakened cloned bodies.The Infestation will assimilate mostly anything organic and inorganic with exception being that they are unable to digest crystals and have trouble digesting some ores.

The Infestation can be cured, as seen with [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V") and [Neewa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neewa "Neewa"), however, the Infestation must not be too severe, as it is otherwise incurable. However, even if one is unable to cure themselves of it, it can still be slowed down with remedies and treatments, as seen with the [Entrati.](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati "Entrati")However these treatments can only prolonged the inevitable, as also seen with the Entrati, despite their miraculous treatments, top of the line Orokin brilliance and technology, and centuries spent researching the Infestation, they can only keep their individuality and wills, with their bodies and minds still being taken slowly by the Infestation.

  
Though the primary method of spread is through spores, there are other means to be infected. Coming in contact with infested DNA, most notably through contact with infested saliva or blood, is known to spread the infestation to other creatures. Furthermore, especially powerful Infested have been known to Infest the terrain around them through contact alone. This apparent near instant Infestation of terrain can be seen with juggernaut's fungal slam attack and [Nidus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nidus "Nidus")' [Virulence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Virulence "Virulence") and [Ravenous](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ravenous "Ravenous").

### Physiology

The physiology of the Infestation is extremely complex, as not only does it vary extremely from instant to instance, but it is constantly changing as well. However, there are traits that are common across all Infested of nearly every Strain:

#### Extremely Durable Flesh

The flesh of the Infested is known to be extremely tough and durable, likely due to it utilizing its mastery over evolution and biology to strengthen itself physically as much as it can. This durability can be increased to monstrous proportions, as with enough feeding and evolving, an Infested life form can go from having slightly tough skin to having nearly impenetrable outer skin with flesh comparable to steel in durability.

#### Weaponized Toxins and Viruses

The Infested commonly uses toxins and viruses to attack their enemies. This includes evolving acidic saliva, toxic venom seeping through orifices or limbs, paralyzing venom injected via teeth, forming viruses within themselves and injecting it via sharp limbs, and even emanating or otherwise using toxic gas to gas their enemies.

#### Extremely Powerful Muscles

Using their biological mastery and rapid evolution, Infested creatures develop extremely powerful and often flexible muscles, that allows them to perform amazing feats strength, speed, agility and overall athleticism that would otherwise be impossible without technological augmentations.

#### Regeneration

Infested life forms are often able to heal themselves using both natural biological processes and via nanites. Of course, the potency of this ability is dependent on the power of the life form, with less powerful Infested being able to heal themselves at a much faster rate than normal creatures to a limited degree, to more powerful entities surging with so much infestation, and having evolved so much that they can not only regenerate entire lost limbs in moments but can even ignore and heal from what would normally be fatal blows and, in some cases, even resurrect themselves from death.

#### Sharp Appendages and Limbs

They commonly develop sharp Appendages such as claws, large fangs, pincers, and more as a method of evolving to become more dangerous. These traits are often used in tandem with their biochemical attacks, usually by coating with toxins or viruses with the intention of stabbing it into whatever is unfortunate enough to cross its path.

# Behavior & Psychology
### Psychology

The Infestation is typically animalistic and ravenous. Acting seemingly like mindless, feral creatures full of aggression and malice. The Infestation is capable of higher-level thought and complex reasoning, as shown with the hive mind constantly communicating directly with the player and employing the tactics detailed below. Though, this intelligence is linked to the size of the Hivemind, as the more creatures that join the Hivemind, the more intelligent it gets overall, even if the creatures it assimilates or creates have only a feral consciousness. Despite being part of the Hivemind, all of the creatures are actually distinct individuals with their own thoughts and minds. Furthermore, the Infestation is capable of more than just constantly assimilating and evolving, as shown with certain creatures disconnected from the Hivemind, the Infested creatures can be docile, playful, caring, and more, even to the point of being able to be domesticated or kept as pets. However, as with any creature that gets exposed to the hive mind, the domesticated or isolated creature will give into the hive mind's will and do its bidding as any creature would if exposed to it. The Infestation is also known to exhibit fear. Lesser infested creatures do show a fear of death if not connected heavily to the Hivemind.

The Hivemind itself is also known to show emotion in rare circumstances. As a whole, the Infestation displays confusion and fear when encountering the Tenno's Warframes and constantly asking why they would attack their own via transmissions. This fear is elaborated upon in [The Glast Gambit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Glast_Gambit "The Glast Gambit") quest, where [The Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") reveals that the Infested fears hybrids, specifically those that combine themselves with the Infestation without actually succumbing to it. For example, the space-bound colony of [Mycona](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mycona_Colony "Mycona Colony") takes advantage of this to harvest Infested materials from the ship on which they live without individual colonists risking death or infection via passing down the Triuna. The [Zealoid Prelate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zealoid_Prelate "Zealoid Prelate"), however, doesn't seem to be afraid of the Warframes, but actually sees them as something "beautiful." This can imply that the Infestation views the Warframes as a beautiful way to use the Infestation, with Warframes being unfathomably powerful Infested lifeforms due to being enhanced by Orokin technology and void attunement. Furthermore, the Infestation has also shown a fear of [The Man in the Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_the_Wall "The Man in the Wall"), with Infested all across Deimos showing signs of fear, hiding and overall displaying the behaviors associated with fear after the The Man In The Wall awakens.

The Hivemind also shows emotions such as Jealousy, as shown in [Eleanor Nightingale](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eleanor_Nightingale "Eleanor Nightingale")'s potential [KIM messages](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kinemantik_Instant_Messenger/Eleanor#Conversation_5 "Kinemantik Instant Messenger/Eleanor") to the Drifter, the Techrot is jealous of Eleanor's connection to the Drifter believing that the Drifter is the reason for her refusing to reconnect with the Hivemind.

### Tactics

Their main strategy for achieving what they want is simple: They throw themselves at potential hosts in large numbers, seeking to drown them in a tide of mutated flesh. And use themselves to Infest the surrounding environment. In the event that they completely take over an area, they seek the next area to Infest to fuel their propagation and evolution.

In the event that they are unable to take over areas and creatures via brute force, they are known to employ much more subtle, cunning tactics. Examples include hiding the Infestation in Individuals and using them to spread and build numbers until they grow enough numbers to launch attacks on their foes, capturing and holding people hostage in order to lure high-profile individuals into Infested territory, using newly Infested people to communicate directly with others, piloting Derelict ships and using them to launch attacks on planets, stashing spores in places to Infest unsuspecting individuals, and even launching colossal clumps of Infested mass at planets in an attempt to start an outbreak on them.
