> Granted a second existence through the mysterious power of the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"), the four Holdfasts struggle to keep their sanity and their vessel intact. The return of the Tenno may turn the tide.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TheHoldfastsIcon.png/32px-TheHoldfastsIcon.png?8bad1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Holdfasts) The Holdfasts are a [Syndicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate "Syndicate") comprised of the four last "survivors" of the adult crew of the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero") and conducting their operations within the [Chrysalith](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chrysalith "Chrysalith"). Returned to life by the mysterious powers of the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"), they have fought off conversion into [Void Angels](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel"). While not strictly human anymore, instead being Void manifestations resurrected through the [Conceptual Embodiment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conceptual_Embodiment "Conceptual Embodiment") of the memories of the original Zariman Crew, they retain their human mindset and guide the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") in preventing the further degradation of the Zariman.
