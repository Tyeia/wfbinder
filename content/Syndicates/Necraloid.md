# Lore & History
Necraloids are the loyal [Cephalon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon "Cephalon") servants of House Entrati. They inhabit archaic, but reliable medallion-embossed chassis that allow them to interact with the physical world. The only known Necraloid is **[Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid "Loid")**, who still provides services and assistance to the Entrati.

After repairing the [Heart](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Heart_of_Deimos_\(Object\) "Heart of Deimos (Object)"), Loid now collaborates with [Father](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Father "Father"), providing the Tenno with [Necramech](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necramech "Necramech") parts in secret.
