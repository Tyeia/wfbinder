 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NightwaveSyndicate.png/32px-NightwaveSyndicate.png?e0486)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nightwave) Nightwave is a pirate radio station hosted by the enigmatic [Nora Night](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nora_Night "Nora Night"). In-game, Nightwave serves as a unique [Syndicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate "Syndicate") system that offers various player rewards such as [Mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod "Mod"), [Resources](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resources "Resources"), [Cosmetic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe_Cosmetics "Warframe Cosmetics") items and more through the completion of various mission challenges.
