> Solaris United's core personnel operating under the name of that group's enigmatic and anonymous figurehead. SU is the public face of the struggle. Vox Solaris is something else entirely; something known only to Eudico... and the Quills.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VoxSolarisIcon.png/32px-VoxSolarisIcon.png?e6889)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vox_Solaris_\(Syndicate\)) Vox Solaris is a covert operation known only to the leaders of the [Solaris United](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United "Solaris United"), with a known association to [The Quills](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Quills "The Quills"). They co-ordinate and orchestrate the anti-[Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") resistance of the broader SU, and specifically they organize [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") [Heists](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Heist "Heist") to take down the gargantuan [Profit-Taker Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Profit-Taker_Orb "Profit-Taker Orb") and [Exploiter Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exploiter_Orb "Exploiter Orb") roaming the [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis").
