**Release Date:** March 7th, 2019

Reinforced by her stalwart physique, the Herculean cyborg, **Hildryn**, fortifies her allies' defenses as enemy barricades crumble beneath her overwhelming might. Rocket skyward at full thrust, achieve atmospheric flight, and launch shield-powered warheads to raze the battlefield with heavy artillery firepower. Hildryn first rained death from above on [Update 24.4](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_24#Update_24.4 "Update 24") (2019-03-08): [Buried Debts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Buried_Debts "Operation: Buried Debts").

**Hildryn** is a hulking [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that utilizes [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") instead of [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") as her strength and defense. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") provides longer [Shield Gate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield_Gate "Shield Gate") duration.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Balefire130xWhite.png/32px-Balefire130xWhite.png?90acf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Balefire) [Balefire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Balefire) summons the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/BalefireCharger.png/32px-BalefireCharger.png?6752e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Balefire_Charger) [Balefire Charger](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Balefire_Charger) to deal significant damage.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Pillage130xWhite.png/32px-Pillage130xWhite.png?90acf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pillage) [Pillage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pillage) releases a wave that seizes enemy shields and [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") to restore her own.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Haven130xWhite.png/32px-Haven130xWhite.png?90acf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Haven) [Haven](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Haven) creates a link that enhances allies with bonus shields, shield recharge rate, and longer shield gate, while enemies receive damage.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AegisStorm130xWhite.png/32px-AegisStorm130xWhite.png?90acf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aegis_Storm) [Aegis Storm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aegis_Storm) has Hildryn hover off the ground, forced to wield her Balefire, while releasing an aura that lifts enemies into aerial stasis while continuously generating [Energy Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Orb "Energy Orb").

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HildrynIcon272.png/31px-HildrynIcon272.png?ef866)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hildryn) [Hildryn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hildryn) utilizes her advanced [Shielding](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") systems to power all of her abilities. Although she has no [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") capacity, her shields boast additional passive benefits and defensive capabilities:
 - Hildryn's [Shield Gating](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield#Shield_Gating "Shield") is more advanced than other Warframes, bolstering her defenses
 - All of Hildryn's abilities drain her Overshield and Shield points, as she lacks any energy capacity.
 - Energy Orb pickups restore **25** shield points and instantly reset shield recharge delay.

### **[Balefire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Balefire)**
Charge and launch devastating bolts of fire.
> [!note]
> Equip a wrist mounted rocket launcher


### **[Pillage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pillage)**
Pillage a percentage of Shields and Armor of nearby enemies to replenish Hildryn's own Shields and Overshields.

### **[Haven](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Haven)**
Create a shield aura around allies. Enemies that approach shielded allies will take damage.

### **[Aegis Storm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aegis_Storm)**
Take the skies and rain Balefire rockets down on the enemy. Nearby enemies are blasted into the air where they will create an Energy Orb every few seconds. When shields run out the enemies are smashed into the ground.
> [!note]
> Hover and stun enemies near you. if they die, you get more shields.
