**Release Date:** October 25th, 2012

There unseen yet where has been, come hither the Trickster God, **Loki**. Nimble yet frail, in shadow he dwells, swift their deaths shall be. Foes oft find, their prey in plain sight, such fools made ill at ease. Kickstart the brawl, turned a melee free-for-all, mischief achieved in his favor.

**Loki** is a trickster [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with stealth and crowd control. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") grants increased [Wall Latch](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wall_Latch "Wall Latch") duration.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Decoy130xWhite.png/32px-Decoy130xWhite.png?4c6dd)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Decoy) [Decoy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Decoy) summons a holographic clone that taunts enemies into attacking it.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Invisibility130xWhite.png/32px-Invisibility130xWhite.png?cafca)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisibility) [Invisibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisibility) turns him [Invisible](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisible "Invisible").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SwitchTeleport130xWhite.png/32px-SwitchTeleport130xWhite.png?d94e0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Switch_Teleport) [Switch Teleport](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Switch_Teleport) swaps positions with the target, stunning enemies while temporarily increasing Loki's [Movement Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RadialDisarm130xWhite.png/32px-RadialDisarm130xWhite.png?cbe1d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Disarm) [Radial Disarm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Disarm) releases a wave of energy that  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Halikar.png/32px-Halikar.png?99ebd)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Disarm) [Disarms](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Disarm) enemies.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/LokiIcon272.png/31px-LokiIcon272.png?1a214)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loki) [Loki](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loki)'s [Wall Latch](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Wall_Latch "Maneuvers") duration is increased to a maximum of **60** seconds, ten times longer than any other Warframe.

### **[Decoy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Decoy)**
Loki deploys a holographic copy of himself that draws enemy fire and absorbs a portion of enemy health and shields.

### **[Invisibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisibility)**
Loki camouflages himself, becoming invisible to enemies.

### **[Switch Teleport](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Switch_Teleport)**
Loki instantaneously swaps positions with a target, confusing the enemy.

### **[Radial Disarm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Disarm)**
Lets forth a wave of energy, disrupting the projectile weapons of enemies in range and forcing them to revert to melee combat.
> [!note]
> Disarms all enemies in range
