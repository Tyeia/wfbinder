## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Sevagoth's roving spirit, formed from concentrated Void energy.

---

When the Tempestarii became a void phantom, Sevagoth set aside his body and became a Shadow, to continue his solemn duty of saving lives.

---
## Abilities

### **[Embrace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Embrace)**
Ghostly hands pull enemies into a flailing mass.

### **[Consume](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Consume)**
Dash through enemies and rip out their souls to heal Shadow.

### **[Death's Harvest](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death%27s_Harvest)**
Inflict nearby enemies with a harrowing condition.
> [!note]
> Makes enemies more vulnerable to damage


### **[Reunite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reunite)**
Sevagoth and his Shadow become one.
