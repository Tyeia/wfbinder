## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Broken Warframes adrift in the Void converged to create Xaku. Together, they deal high damage. Xaku proves that the sum of the whole is greater than its parts.

---

> Forged from the remnants of lost Warframes, they channel the spirits of the Void. This is Xaku, the fragmented, the restored.
> 
> In the concert of mayhem, Xaku is a law unto themselves, Tenno.

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:** August 25th, 2020

Of the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") ethereal, yet coalesced into corporeal shape, the fractured anomaly **Xaku** exists as an entity thrice entwined. Fused from the shattered remnants of three warriors, they bridge reality to the horrors from beyond the fold. Xaku reassembled in [Update 29.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_29 "Update 29") (2020-08-25).

**Xaku** is an amalgamation of [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") fused by the mysterious powers of the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgVoidSmall64.png/32px-DmgVoidSmall64.png?e62bb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Void_Damage) [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Void_Damage), blessed with high damage output. ## Abilities

### Passive
Shifting their pieces unpredictably,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/XakuIcon272.png/31px-XakuIcon272.png?ad108)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xaku) [Xaku](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xaku) has a **25%** [Dodge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dodge "Dodge") chance to harmlessly phase through incoming enemy weapon attacks, while protected by a **25%** [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") against area of effect damage. Passive dodge chance and area damage reduction both raise to **75%** while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TheVastUntime130xWhite.png/32px-TheVastUntime130xWhite.png?5e329)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Vast_Untime) [The Vast Untime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Vast_Untime) is activated.

### **[Xata's Whisper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xata%27s_Whisper)**
Wield Void damage for all attacks from equipped weapons when activated.

### **[Grasp of Lohk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grasp_of_Lohk)**
Void Tendrils steal weapons from nearby enemies to use as your own floating, auto-targeting armament. Damage output increases based on enemy level. The number of weapons grabbed also determines the damage multiplier for The Lost: Deny's Void beam.
> [!note]
> Steal enemy weapons, become walking turret


### **[The Lost](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Lost)**
Cycle through a trio of lost Warframe powers. Accuse manifests a Void fissure to corrupt enemies into allies. Gaze grasps targets in Void tendrils that capture damage and inflict it when the target is released. Deny annihilates with a beam of Void energy, wiping Sentient resistances.

### **[The Vast Untime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Vast_Untime)**
Temporarily shed the outer pieces of Xaku in a destructive blast, then stalk the battlefield in a new, swifter skeletal form. Enemies damaged by the body shrapnel are rendered weaker to Void Damage. Xaku's other active abilities' duration is frozen in time and resumes once The Vast Untime expires.

## Lore

> This Warframe Xaku. Somewhat of a family themselves. A sum of many, a composite of others we lost in the ages that our Entrati family has served here.

—[Grandmother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grandmother "Grandmother")

Xaku is a composite of three Warframes that used to be beneficiaries of the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EntratiIcon.png/32px-EntratiIcon.png?cafef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati) [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati). Throughout the ages, the trio were lost to the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"). Driven by void spirits, their shattered remnants fused together into the being that is now known as Xaku.
