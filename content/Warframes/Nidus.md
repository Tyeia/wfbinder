**Release Date:** December 22nd, 2016

Epitome of the endlessly evolving [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infestation "Infestation"), **Nidus** bends the vile corruption to his will, mutates his genetic potency to adapt and assimilates his victims with an insatiable parasitic hunger. Nidus and his strain developed in [Update 19.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_19#Update_19.5 "Update 19") (2016-12-22).

**Nidus** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that harnesses the Infestation to mutate his deadly and crowd controlling powers. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") grants Mutation Stacks from abilities to enhance them while allowing him to negate [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Virulence130xWhite.png/32px-Virulence130xWhite.png?94560)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Virulence) [Virulence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Virulence) stomps the ground to release a trail of damaging fungal growth.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Larva130xWhite.png/32px-Larva130xWhite.png?6d3e3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Larva) [Larva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Larva) creates an Infested pod that latches onto enemies to group them.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ParasiticLink130xWhite.png/32px-ParasiticLink130xWhite.png?1f7d1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parasitic_Link) [Parasitic Link](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parasitic_Link) tethers a link that grants weapon damage and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AbilityStrengthBuff.png/32px-AbilityStrengthBuff.png?3d71c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ability_Strength) [Ability Strength](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ability_Strength) on allies or [Damage Redirection](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Redirection "Damage Redirection") on enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Ravenous130xWhite.png/32px-Ravenous130xWhite.png?6277f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ravenous) [Ravenous](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ravenous) summons a field of Infested growth that provides [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") restore over time while hatching [Maggots](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maggot "Maggot") that latch onto enemies and explode.
## Abilities

### Passive
- **Mutation Stacks** are a resource unique to Nidus, indicated by the Mutation counter and gauge displayed in the lower-right on the HUD, above the ability icons.
- If Nidus suffers fatal damage with at least **15** stacks of Mutation, Nidus will consume the 15 stacks to avoid [bleedout](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bleedout "Bleedout") while receiving **5** seconds of invulnerability and restoring **50%** of his health.

### **[Virulence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Virulence)**
Rupture the ground with a damaging fungal growth that steals energy from each enemy it strikes. For every five enemies hit, the Infestation mutates, multiplying its destructive force.
> [!note]
> Ground spikes go from you to the enemy


### **[Larva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Larva)**
Spawn an Infested pod that erupts with tendrils, latches onto nearby enemies and pulls them in.

### **[Parasitic Link](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parasitic_Link)**
Bind to a target with a parasitic link. For allies, both the host and Nidus gain increased Ability Strength. Linked enemies take the damage inflicted on Nidus.

### **[Ravenous](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ravenous)**
Gluttonous maggots swarm nearby enemies, feasting until they burst with Infestation. The maggots benefit from Mutation and each enemy consumed adds to the Mutation stack.

## Lore

> Fair, but the [Myconians](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Myconian "Myconian") aren't asking for charity. Their ancestral understanding of the Infestation comes from an Old War relic. A relic that I know, will be of extreme interest to [you](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno"). Return the Triuna, and the relic will be yours. Fair compensation for the risk.

—[Ergo Glast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ergo_Glast "Ergo Glast") on Nidus during The Glast Gambit

Little is known about the history of the Nidus Warframe aside from its Orokin era origins. However, the Myconians have apparently used information learned from their time possessing the relic, the Nidus blueprint, to produce the **Triuna**, an infection passed down from generation to generation, which the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infestation "Infestation") fears due to the hybrid nature of the resulting host, allowing the Myconians to harvest Infested flesh as part of their livelihood.

Nidus has a mysterious connection with the [Helminth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth "Helminth") being that resides in the Orbiter, being the only Warframe that can purposely command the Helminth to infect him with the Helminth Virus; the extent of this relationship is currently unknown.
