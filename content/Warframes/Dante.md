## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Seeker of knowledge. Keeper of history. Daring researcher of Leverian lore. Dante composes arcane tales to support allies and devastate enemies.

---
**Release Date:** March 27th, 2024

Recite the epic tales of yore and chronicle our heroic deeds, woven therein the gilded tapestry of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) history. Since foreshadowed from the  **[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Noctua.png/32px-Noctua.png?eeab5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua_\(Weapon\)) [Noctua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua_\(Weapon\))'**s luminous pages, authored by the Keeper of History, **Dante**, he illustrates for defiant foes their predestined demise. Don his occult quills and tome, with gun and blade in tow, to inscribe our legacy as fate unfolds. Rescind the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) plot to denude [Leverian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leverian "Leverian")'s exhibition halls, thus devise narratives yet unwritten upon its walls. Dante's new tale began in [Update 35.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_35#Update_35.5 "Update 35") (2024-03-27): Dante Unbound.

## Abilities

### Passive
Enemies slain by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Noctua130xWhite.png/32px-Noctua130xWhite.png?3cf7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua) [Noctua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua)'s primary fire, alt-fire, and page fragments are scanned into the [Codex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Codex "Codex") upon death, with each enemy death recording **1** scan entry. Enemy types with completed Codex research become **permanently** debuffed with **Chronicler's Mark**, which grants Dante's equipped weapons, including the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Noctua.png/32px-Noctua.png?eeab5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua_\(Weapon\)) [Noctua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua_\(Weapon\)) itself, and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FinalVerse130xWhite.png/32px-FinalVerse130xWhite.png?02663)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Verse) [Final Verse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Verse)'s **Wordwarden** Noctua copies an extra **50%** [Status Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Chance "Status Chance") versus these foes.

### **[Noctua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Noctua)**
Open Noctua, Dante’s Exalted Tome, and unleash a tale of woe upon his enemies.

### **[Light Verse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Light_Verse)**
Dante’s vitalizing composition grants him and his allies Overguard and increases their Health.
> [!note]
> More or less just heals


### **[Dark Verse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dark_Verse)**
Dante’s composition draws blood from nearby enemies, inflicting Slash Damage upon them.

### **[Final Verse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Verse)**
Dante must compose two other Verses before his Final Verse.

- TRIUMPH: Two Light Verses cast in succession invigorate allies with a buff that increases Health and grants Overguard. Kills and assists briefly regenerate Overguard.

- TRAGEDY: Two Dark Verses cast in succession attack enemies. Damage Over Time from Slash, Heat and Toxin Status Effects accumulates in a single blast.

- WORDWARDEN: A Light Verse followed by a Dark Verse supports allies with copies of Noctua. Noctua copies synchronize their attacks with their designated ally's attacks.

- PAGEFLIGHT: A Dark Verse followed by a Light Verse summons Paragrimms that swoop at enemies, making them vulnerable to Status Chance and Status Damage, as well as drawing enemy attacks away from allies.
> [!note]
> Paragrimms = Owls


## Lore

Dante served as scribe and a historian. Additionally, he was the main chronicler of the **[Leverian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leverian "Leverian")**, a little-known museum named after [Drusus Leverian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drusus "Drusus"), its curator. A student of history and avid seeker of knowledge, Dante soon became close friends with both Drusus and [Albrecht Entrati's](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati") assistant, [**Loid**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid_\(Original\) "Loid (Original)").

Due to his nature as a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes"), Dante was unable to speak with a conventional voice; instead, he spoke through his writing, and his voice was indeed beautiful. A natural scholar, Dante possessed an affinity for knowledge, and often shared the Entrati's interest with bones and the dead. He even went on to publish some of the finest works detailing ancient [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth") archaeology, something that was considered impossible for a mere Warframe to accomplish.

Dante was one of the few warframes who didn't immediately go mad and join in the Warframe Insurrection. However, he became a wanted fugitive.

When Albrecht disappeared, Dante became eager to unveil the mystery behind the disappearance. After the reappearance of the martian moon, **[Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos")**, he ventured into [Albrecht's Laboratories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht%27s_Laboratories "Albrecht's Laboratories"), despite promising Drusus otherwise. Within the hauntingly silent halls, Dante encountered the lab's malfunctioning [Necramechs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necramech_\(Enemy\) "Necramech (Enemy)"); all acting violent and chanting in eldritch [Voidtongue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voidtongue "Voidtongue").

As Drusus watched, the wrong-tongued dialect began to worm into Dante's mind, tempting him with his one weakness; the promise of knowledge. He had discovered **The Book**, an unknown, unearthly tome of knowledge that was laid as a trap by [The Indifference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Indifference "The Indifference") as an attempt to seize control of him. As Dante struggled to resist its knowledge, he heard his dear friend say, _"Dante, what you have found can never be brought forth. [Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parvos "Parvos") must never know of this."_

Thus, Dante gave up, allowing himself to succumb to the Book's influence and take his life. However, the Book and the hellish words within did not allow Dante to die, and instead altered the very fabric of who the Warframe was. Transported to stars unknown, that wrong-tongued Voice now murmurs endlessly within his head, longing to be known, to exist, and to survive.
