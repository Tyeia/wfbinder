#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Unleash the draconic. Chroma employs Heat, Electricity, Toxin, and Cold to deal high damage while maintaining survivability. Soar with winged fury.

Bind the elements and unleash untold destruction with Chroma Prime. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

Overwhelming his foes with fiery rage, shocking touch, venomous breath, or a cold embrace, the draconic hunter **Chroma** wields elemental caprice to reign supreme.

## Abilities

### Passive

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ChromaIcon272.png/31px-ChromaIcon272.png?e852e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chroma) [Chroma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chroma) possesses an extra midair jump and bullet jump. Wings matching Chroma's energy colors will form during these maneuvers.
 
 Chroma can alter his abilities to match any basic (uncombined) elemental attribute of the player's choice. Customizing Chroma's [primary emission color](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe_Cosmetics#Colors "Warframe Cosmetics") determines what abilities he starts with. Color choices are grouped into four categories with each category corresponding to a given element:

- red, magenta, brown, orange and bright yellow hues being  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgHeatSmall64.png/32px-DmgHeatSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage); 
- blue and purple hues being  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage); 
- green, lime, teal and dark yellow hues being  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgToxinSmall64.png/32px-DmgToxinSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) [Toxin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage);
- white, grey, black, and some faded colors being  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage).

Note that players do _not_ need to purchase [color palettes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Color_Picker_Extension "Color Picker Extension") to unlock the full potential of this trait. While customizing his emission color, Chroma's active element is displayed in the form of an icon ( [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgHeatSmall64.png/32px-DmgHeatSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgToxinSmall64.png/32px-DmgToxinSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) [Toxin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage)). Customizing the secondary emission and the energy colors will have no effect on Chroma's chosen element. Abilities can be cycled mid-mission using  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SpectralScream130xWhite.png/32px-SpectralScream130xWhite.png?7842c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spectral_Scream) [Spectral Scream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spectral_Scream).

### **[Spectral Scream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spectral_Scream)**

Exhale a deep breath of elemental destruction. Chroma's energy color determines the element.
### **[Elemental Ward](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Elemental_Ward)**

Depending on Chroma's elemental alignment, an offensive area-of-effect is created. Chroma and his nearby allies are imbued with defensive energy.
### **[Vex Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vex_Armor)**

Chroma fortifies squad Armor when his Shields are damaged or he kills an enemy with a melee weapon. He increases squad Weapon Damage when he loses Health or kills an enemy with a ranged weapon, double the effect on weak point kills.
### **[Effigy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Effigy)**

Chroma turns his pelt into a massive sentry that strengthens nearby allies and engulfs enemies in elemental attacks.
