**Release Date:** March 24th, 2017

Rhythmic and imaginative, **Octavia** conducts her myriad siren songs and ambient anthems through the mystical [Mandachord](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mandachord "Mandachord"). Entice enemies with musical instruments that inflict melodic maladies, as the Tenno chorus synchronize their rhythm to sound the drums of war. Octavia was composed in [Update 20.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_20#Update_20.0 "Update 20") (2017-03-24).

**Octavia** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that utilizes music for support. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") recovers [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") over time for herself and allies after casting abilities.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MalletIcon.png/32px-MalletIcon.png?a7094)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mallet) [Mallet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mallet) hurls a device that taunts enemies into attacking it while releasing damaging music to nearby enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ResonatorIcon.png/32px-ResonatorIcon.png?8d3ea)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resonator) [Resonator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resonator) summons a roller that charms enemies into following it, while picking up Mallets as it moves around.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MetronomeIcon.png/32px-MetronomeIcon.png?21146)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Metronome) [Metronome](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Metronome) grants buffs from timed actions: jump to gain Vivace for [Movement Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed"), crouch to gain Nocturne for [Invisibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisibility "Invisibility"), fire guns to gain Opera for [Multishot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Multishot "Multishot"), and melee to gain Forte for melee weapon damage.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AmpIcon.png/32px-AmpIcon.png?08f54)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amp_\(Ability\)) [Amp](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amp_\(Ability\)) projects a field that strengthens ally weapon damage and increases the damage and range of Mallet.

## Abilities

### Passive
- Mandachord
	- The [Mandachord](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mandachord "Mandachord") is a [step sequencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_sequencer "wikipedia:Music sequencer") that allows the player to compose unique songs and conduct them in full symphony through Octavia's abilities.
- Inspiration
	- When casting an ability, Octavia grants herself and allies within **15** meters the **Inspiration** buff, generating **1** [energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") per second for **30** seconds.

### **[Mallet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mallet)**
Rhythmically beats damage into nearby enemies and draws their fire. Damage inflicted on the Mallet increases its lethality.

### **[Resonator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resonator)**
Launches a rollerball that charms foes to follow it. Combines with the Mallet to create a roving ball of sonic destruction.

### **[Metronome](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Metronome)**
Grants buffs to those who consistently perform actions in time to Octavia’s music. Timed jumps offer the Vivace speed buff. Crouching on the beat grants cloaking with the Nocturne buff. Firing rhythmically bestows Opera multishot buff. Timed melee swings give the Forte damage buff.

### **[Amp](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amp_\(Ability\))**
Draws power from the decibel level of sound in the area and uses it to amplify a damage buff for Octavia and her allies. It also doubles the damage and range of nearby Mallets.
