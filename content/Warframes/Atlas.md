## Overview
#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Titan of stone, lord of the earthly elementals.

In bygone ages, mountains were said to house warriors. In this age, the mountain has become the warrior.

---


> _This is Atlas, the brawler, the strong._
> 
> _Moving mountains is no easy task, Tenno. Atlas endures._

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:** October 1st, 2015

With stone cold fists and a titan's physique, **Atlas** bends rock to encrust and bulldoze through all challengers in his brawl. Champion of elemental earth, he manifests formidable ramparts, crushing boulders, and golem brethren to rampage his stomping grounds. Atlas was unearthed in [**Update 17.5**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Update_17#Update_17.5) (2015-10-01).

## Abilities

### Passive

Strong as the mountain, Atlas is immune to [**Knockdown**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Knockdown) while in contact with the ground.

·Knockdown immunity does **not** apply while in the air.

·Does **not** apply to pushback effects.

·Does **not** prevent the recoiling effect from firing powerful [**Arch-guns**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Arch-gun) such as [**Corvas**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Corvas), [**Velocitus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Velocitus), and [**Larkspur**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Larkspur) in non-[**Archwing**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Archwing) missions.

Rubble is a pickup item unique to Atlas that drops from [**petrified**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Petrify) enemies and [**Rumblers**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Rumblers) on death. Rubble restores [**Health**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Health) to Atlas if he is injured; if Atlas is at full health, Rubble instead grants additional [**Armor**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Armor) that decays over time.

### [Landslide](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Landslide)

Bash enemies with an exploding sliding punch, and repeat for a devastating combo. Petrified enemies take extra damage, and drop Rubble when destroyed. Rubble can heal Atlas and bolster his armor.

### [Tectonics](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Tectonics)

Summon a rock-wall, activate again to send the rocks crashing towards the enemy.

### [Petrify](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Petrify)

Atlas' hardened gaze will fossilize foes, heal Rumblers, and create Petrified Bulwarks. When shattered, petrified enemies drop healing Rubble for Atlas.
> [!note]
> Turn enemies in front of you to stone


### [Rumblers](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Rumblers)

Summon two elemental stone brawlers to the melee. Summoning petrifies enemies in close proximity to Atlas. When finished, Rumblers collapse into a pile of healing Rubble.

## Lore
[Temple Telamon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Temple_Telamon "Temple Telamon") was a fanatic cult that believed in the coming of a great stone destroyer whose arrival would signify imminent destruction followed by a new rebirth. The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) paid no heed to their chants, until the Telamons hijacked an asteroid and sent it on a collision course with [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth").

Rather than sending destructive weapons or [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") soldiers, **Atlas**, a stone warrior who defied the Telamon's ideals, was dispatched to intercept the asteroid, using  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Petrify130xWhite.png/32px-Petrify130xWhite.png?05fb0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Petrify) [Petrify](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Petrify) on the Telamon defenders and fashioning  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Rumblers130xWhite.png/32px-Rumblers130xWhite.png?f69f9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rumblers) [Rumblers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rumblers) from petrified remains of their tableau. Atlas then delivered a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Landslide130xWhite.png/32px-Landslide130xWhite.png?8bc68)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Landslide) [Landslide](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Landslide) that reverberated into the rock, shattering the asteroid and dissolving it into nothing, silencing the Telamon's hymns.
