**Release Date:** March 18th, 2013

Unleashing an endless epidemic of contagious spores, the Mother of Malady **Saryn** eradicates her foes through horrific decay. Erupt caustic pustules to spread her disease, shed deceptive husks to draw enemy fire, as she injects lethal venom and exhales deadly viruses to cleanse the System with her toxic touch. Saryn blossomed in [Update 7.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_7#Update_7 "Update 7") (2013-03-18).

**Saryn** is a disease-creating [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that deals lethal damage to her surroundings. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") provides increased [Status Duration](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Duration "Status Duration").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Spores130xWhite.png/32px-Spores130xWhite.png?2ac87)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spores) [Spores](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spores) infects a target with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) spores that spread to other enemies when burst by striking them or upon the initial target's death.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Molt130xWhite.png/32px-Molt130xWhite.png?21a37)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molt) [Molt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molt) sheds her skin to cleanse [Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") and grants [Movement Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed") while leaving behind a decoy.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ToxicLash130xWhite.png/32px-ToxicLash130xWhite.png?96553)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Toxic_Lash) [Toxic Lash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Toxic_Lash) laces weapons with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgToxinSmall64.png/32px-DmgToxinSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) [Toxin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage), strengthened for melee weapons, which can burst Spores on striking afflicted targets.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Miasma130xWhite.png/32px-Miasma130xWhite.png?a95e1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Miasma) [Miasma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Miasma) releases a sickening wave of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgViralSmall64.png/32px-DmgViralSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage) [Viral](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage) that further propagates spores.

## Abilities

### Passive
[Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") inflicted by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SarynIcon272.png/31px-SarynIcon272.png?8c654)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Saryn) [Saryn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Saryn)'s weapons and abilities last **25%** longer.

### **[Spores](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spores)**
Inflict a target with a pox of [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CorrosiveSymbol.png/14px-CorrosiveSymbol.png?355c2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:CorrosiveSymbol.png)Corrosive spores. Spread spores to nearby enemies by destroying them or killing their host. The longer the Spore spreads, its damage will increase.

### **[Molt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molt)**
Shedding her skin like a snake, Saryn leaves a decoy behind to draw fire from enemies.

### **[Toxic Lash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Toxic_Lash)**
While active, attacks deal additional [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ToxinSymbol.png/14px-ToxinSymbol.png?5eb83)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:ToxinSymbol.png)Toxin damage; this effect is doubled for melee strikes. Instantly burst spores when attacking afflicted enemies.

### **[Miasma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Miasma)**
Releases a poisonous miasma that deals [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ViralSymbol.png/14px-ViralSymbol.png?bdf42)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:ViralSymbol.png)Viral damage to enemies in range. Foes afflicted by spores are more susceptible to the mist.
