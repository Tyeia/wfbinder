**Release Date:** October 24th, 2014

Tapping into the Rift, **Limbo** demonstrates his art of dimensional travel that disrupts the space-time continuum, blurring the borders between realms and reality. **Limbo** arrived from the Rift Plane in [Update 15.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_15#Update_15.0 "Update 15") (2014-10-24).

**Limbo** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that commands the dimensional rifts for crowd control. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") has [rolling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rolling "Rolling") leave behind a portal for allies to enter the [Rift Plane](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rift_Plane "Rift Plane"), providing [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") restore over time while entities in different planes cannot interact with each other.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Banish130xWhite.png/32px-Banish130xWhite.png?a60fe)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Banish) [Banish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Banish) creates a wave that forces the target into the other dimension.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Stasis130xWhite.png/32px-Stasis130xWhite.png?5d7ab)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stasis) [Stasis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stasis) freezes enemies and gunfire that are in the Rift.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RiftSurge130xWhite.png/32px-RiftSurge130xWhite.png?d69b5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rift_Surge) [Rift Surge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rift_Surge) produces a surge on enemies in the Rift that when killed forces other nearby enemies into the Rift.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Cataclysm130xWhite.png/32px-Cataclysm130xWhite.png?2dc81)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cataclysm) [Cataclysm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cataclysm) creates a large dome that bridges the material and Rift planes to all entities that enter it.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/LimboIcon272.png/31px-LimboIcon272.png?a04a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Limbo) [Limbo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Limbo) has access to the **Rift plane** (**Rift** for short), an alternate dimension to the normal environment, or **material plane**. By performing a _[Roll](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Rolling "Maneuvers")_ (default Shift ), a _[Sidespring](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Sidespring "Maneuvers")_ (A  or D  + Shift ), or a _[Backspring](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Backspring "Maneuvers")_ (RMB  + S  + Shift ), Limbo slides into and out of the Rift at will. This is better known as _Rift Walk_. Several of Limbo's abilities impose the Rift upon the world around him, affecting himself, the environment, enemies, and even allies.

### **[Banish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Banish)**
Casts a wave of Rift energy that damages hostiles while pushing enemies and allies out of Limbo’s current plane of existence.

### **[Stasis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stasis)**
Freezes Rift-bound enemies. While active, enemy projectiles are arrested in mid-air, resuming its trajectory when stasis ends.

### **[Rift Surge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rift_Surge)**
Surges nearby Rift-bound enemies with Rift energy. When killed the Rift Surge is transferred to a nearby enemy outside the rift. Surged enemies that leave the Rift perform a radial Banish.

### **[Cataclysm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cataclysm)**
A violent blast of void energy tears open a pocket of rift plane which can sustain itself for a short period before collapsing in another lethal blast.
> [!note]
> Summons a bubble where inside is Rift and outside is not. It shrinks over time.
