## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Equinox rises from the edge of day and night. Her Day form offers offensive support while her Night form offers defensive support. She thrives between realms.

---

> This is Equinox, a deadly duality.
>
>A true Tenno must master the divide between night and day. Do you have what it takes?

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:** July 31st, 2015

A duality of contrasting forces in a single entity, **Equinox** unleashes wrath like the onset of daybreak, or draws life to a close like dusk's approach. Shifting between two forms allows her to bolster Tenno strength in light or provide recuperative respite in darkness. **Equinox** dawned in [Update 17.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_17#Update_17.0 "Update 17") (2015-07-31).

**Equinox** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") of duality of night and day to provide supportive offense and defense. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") converts [Health Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") and [Energy Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Orb "Energy Orb") into additional ## Abilities

### Passive
#### Equilibrium
When  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EquinoxIcon272.png/31px-EquinoxIcon272.png?ea0fc)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equinox) [Equinox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equinox) collects [Health Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") or [Energy Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Orb "Energy Orb"), **10%** of the restored amount is converted into additional [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") and [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") respectively.

-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EquinoxIcon272.png/31px-EquinoxIcon272.png?ea0fc)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equinox) [Equinox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equinox)'s passive stacks additively with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equilibrium) [Equilibrium](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equilibrium), for a total conversion of **120%**.
- Allows for the pickup of orbs while the associated resource pool is full, for the purpose of conversion.
- The effect of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ArcanePulse.png/44px-ArcanePulse.png?1765c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Pulse) [Arcane Pulse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Pulse) does not apply to health from converted energy orbs.
- The effects of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ArcaneEnergize.png/44px-ArcaneEnergize.png?cc86e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Energize) [Arcane Energize](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Energize) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FocusEnergyPulse.png/32px-FocusEnergyPulse.png?c04d5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Zenurik#Energy_Pulse) [Energy Pulse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Zenurik#Energy_Pulse) do not apply to energy from converted health orbs.
#### Day Form/Night Form
Equinox can shift between two forms to alter her abilities: the offensive-focused **Day-Form** or the defensive/supportive-focused **Night-Form**. Customizing Equinox's [primary emission color](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe_Cosmetics#Colors "Warframe Cosmetics") determines what form she starts with:

- Bright hues change default form to Day-Form;
- Dark hues change default form to Night-Form.

Note that players do _not_ need to purchase [color palettes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Color_Picker_Extension "Color Picker Extension") to unlock the full potential of this trait. While customizing her emission color, Equinox's active form is displayed in the form of an icon. The icon with a hollow diamond in the center represents Night, while the icon with the filled-in diamond represents Day. Upon changing colors, her combined form in [Arsenal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arsenal "Arsenal") preview will also change her stance animation to reflect the chosen form's personality. Customizing the secondary emission and the energy colors will have no effect. Forms and abilities can be cycled mid-mission using  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Metamorphosis130xWhite.png/32px-Metamorphosis130xWhite.png?60882)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Metamorphosis) [Metamorphosis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Metamorphosis).

### **[Metamorphosis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Metamorphosis)**
Switch forms, temporarily gaining bonus shields and armor in Night-Form, or bonus damage and speed in Day-Form.

### **[Rest & Rage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rest_%26_Rage)**
In Night Form targets are put to sleep. In Day Form targets become more vulnerable to damage.

### **[Pacify & Provoke](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pacify_%26_Provoke)**
In Night Form reduces damage inflicted by nearby enemies. In Day Form increases Ability Strength of nearby allies.

### **[Mend & Maim](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mend_%26_Maim)**
In Night Form, allies are healed with each nearby enemy killed. In Day Form, nearby enemies are bled and then subjected to a wave of slashing force.
> [!note]
> Day form does slash damage to all enemies. Both forms build up a counter as they are active to either do massive healing or massive damage when the ability ends.
