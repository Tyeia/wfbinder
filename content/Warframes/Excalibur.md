## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Excalibur epitomizes the warrior spirit. His master swordsmanship deals high damage. He is the embodiment of martial excellence.

---

> The Sentients had won. They had turned our weapons, our technology, against us. The more advanced we became, the greater our losses. The war was over unless we found a new way. In our desperation we turned to the Void. The blinding night, the hellspace where our science and reason failed.
> 
> We took the twisted few that had returned from that place. We built a frame around them, a conduit of their affliction. Gave them the weapons of the old ways. Gun and blade. A new warrior, a new code was born. These rejects, these Tenno, became our saviors. Warrior-Gods cast in steel and fury striking our enemies in a way they could never comprehend. Excalibur was the first.

**Release Date:** October 25th, 2012

Shine his beacon of hope in every era of strife, unsheathe now our sword in the stone. Wield his valiant blade to bring balance to Origin, and strike down mortal foes as the Radiant Swordsman, **Excalibur**. Witness a blaze as blinding as brilliant Sol, where he leads the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [[Tenno]] to triumphant victory. Choose wisely, Tenno. Start your new journey with Excalibur as one of the three beginner Warframes.

**Excalibur** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with mastery of the blade. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases the damage and [Attack Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Attack_Speed "Attack Speed") of equipped [Swords](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sword "Sword"), [Dual Swords](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Swords "Dual Swords"), [Nikanas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nikana "Nikana"), and [Rapiers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rapier "Rapier").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SlashDash130xWhite.png/32px-SlashDash130xWhite.png?05875)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slash_Dash) [Slash Dash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slash_Dash) has him rapidly dash forward with a powerful sword slice.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RadialBlind130xWhite.png/32px-RadialBlind130xWhite.png?4ca3b)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Blind) [Radial Blind](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Blind) emits a bright light that  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RadialBlind130xDark.png/32px-RadialBlind130xDark.png?2817c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blind) [Blinds](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blind) enemies, while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RadialJavelin130xWhite.png/32px-RadialJavelin130xWhite.png?dd14e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Javelin) [Radial Javelin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Javelin) launches javelins that impale enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedBlade130xWhite.png/32px-ExaltedBlade130xWhite.png?9edd4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade) [Exalted Blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade) summons the ethereal  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedBladeWeapon.png/32px-ExaltedBladeWeapon.png?654f4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade_\(Weapon\)) [Exalted Blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade_\(Weapon\)) melee.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExcaliburIcon272.png/31px-ExcaliburIcon272.png?3bcf7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) [Excalibur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) has improved mastery in the Tenno [art of the blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee "Melee"), receiving **+10%** [Attack Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Attack_Speed "Attack Speed") and **+10%** [damage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage "Damage") bonuses for melee attacks while wielding the following weapons:

- [Swords](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Sword "Category:Sword"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Skana.png/32px-Skana.png?67dfa)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Skana) [Skana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Skana).
- [Dual swords](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Dual_Swords "Category:Dual Swords"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DualSkana.png/32px-DualSkana.png?af2c5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Skana) [Dual Skana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Skana).
- [Nikanas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Nikana "Category:Nikana"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DragonNikana.png/32px-DragonNikana.png?20efa)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dragon_Nikana) [Dragon Nikana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dragon_Nikana).
- [Rapiers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Rapier "Category:Rapier"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Destreza.png/32px-Destreza.png?52b66)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Destreza) [Destreza](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Destreza).
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedBladeWeapon.png/32px-ExaltedBladeWeapon.png?654f4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade_\(Weapon\)) [Exalted Blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade_\(Weapon\))
- Bonuses are additive to mods such as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pressure_Point) [Pressure Point](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pressure_Point) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fury) [Fury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fury).

### **[Slash Dash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slash_Dash)**
Dash between enemies while slashing with the Exalted Blade.

### **[Radial Blind](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Blind)**
Emits a bright flash of light, blinding all enemies in a small radius for several seconds.

### **[Radial Javelin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Javelin)**
Launches javelins towards enemies, dealing high damage and impaling them to walls.

### **[Exalted Blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade)**
Summon a sword of pure light and immense power.
