## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Blazing with glamor and armed with an Infested guitar, Lizzie. Temple's power chords light the flames of revolution.

---

**Release Date:** March 19, 2025

Hear them scream aloud 'neath the midnight sun, betwixt stardust glamour and ephemeral flames. Raze the [Höllvanian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hollvania "Hollvania") underground for fame and glory, then steal the spotlight from the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TechrotIcon.png/32px-TechrotIcon.png?5b58e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot) [[Techrot]] menace. Rock whole damned worlds with scorching chords, blasting hellish harmonies ad nauseam... their last concert gone global, shredding duo at center stage: the Devil's Guitar, **Temple** and **Lizzie**! Temple brought the stage down in [Update 38.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_38#Update_38.5 "Update 38") (2025-03-19): Techrot Encore.

enhanced effects on ## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TempleIcon272.png/31px-TempleIcon272.png?9d1ab)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Temple) [Temple](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Temple) tracks their rhythmic mayhem by keeping in sync with the **Backbeat** metronome, shown below their aiming reticle. The metronome's pendulum swings from left to right and vice-versa at a pace of 78 BPM, while passing through a central zone that doubles as a fuel gauge for  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Lizzie.png/32px-Lizzie.png?6c8fe)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lizzie) [Lizzie](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lizzie), the living Techrot guitar wielded during  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedSolo.png/32px-ExaltedSolo.png?20c86)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Solo) [Exalted Solo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Solo).

When in the zone, the pendulum's bulb above its needle lights up. Cast an ability while the Backbeat is in the zone to amplify Temple's ability with a **50%**  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EnergyOrb.png/32px-EnergyOrb.png?bcca9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ability_Efficiency) [Ability Efficiency](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ability_Efficiency) bonus (**35%** on [Helminth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth "Helminth") subsumed abilities), fuel up the gauge by **55** ammunition, and earn an extra effect specific to each ability:

-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Pyrotechnics.png/32px-Pyrotechnics.png?4aa91)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pyrotechnics) [Pyrotechnics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pyrotechnics): **2x** additional flame pillars.
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Overdrive.png/32px-Overdrive.png?f48e2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overdrive) [Overdrive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overdrive): **2x** [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") vulnerability.
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Ripper%27sWail.png/32px-Ripper%27sWail.png?eee6b)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ripper%27s_Wail) [Ripper's Wail](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ripper%27s_Wail): **4** second [Invulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerability "Invulnerability") duration.
    - Additionally striking enemies with Pyrotechnics or Overdrive, or recasting Ripper's Wail, increases its weapon  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgHeatSmall64.png/32px-DmgHeatSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) damage bonus by [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AbilityStrengthBuff.png/32px-AbilityStrengthBuff.png?3d71c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ability_Strength)**2% / 3% / 4% / 5%** up to a maximum of **750%**  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgHeatSmall64.png/32px-DmgHeatSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) damage and extend its duration by [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AbilityDurationBuff.png/32px-AbilityDurationBuff.png?d3e3b)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ability_Duration)**3 / 4 / 5 / 6** seconds up to a maximum of **60** seconds.
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedSolo.png/32px-ExaltedSolo.png?20c86)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Solo) [Exalted Solo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Solo): [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") cost reduced to **0**.
    - Sonic sigils become larger and inflict **3** guaranteed  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgViralSmall64.png/32px-DmgViralSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage) [Viral](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage) (1 ),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgMagneticSmall64.png/32px-DmgMagneticSmall64.png?80be3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) [Magnetic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) (2 ),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) (3 ), or  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) (4 ) status effects.
- Successful Backbeat timings play a crowd cheering sound.

Enabling the "Metronome Audio" option in Temple's ability screen will have timed backbeats casts play up to seven ticking sounds when the pendulum crosses the central zone.

### **[Pyrotechnics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pyrotechnics)**
Blast targeted enemies with pillars of [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HeatSymbol.png/14px-HeatSymbol.png?8e0e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HeatSymbol.png)Heat Damage.

**Backbeat Timing:** Ignite even more pillars. Increases the Duration and [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HeatSymbol.png/14px-HeatSymbol.png?8e0e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HeatSymbol.png)Heat Damage of Ripper's Wail when it deals damage.

### **[Overdrive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overdrive)**
Drive loudspeakers into epic distortion to create a damaging wave of [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HeatSymbol.png/14px-HeatSymbol.png?8e0e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HeatSymbol.png)Heat Damage while also increasing vulnerability to Critical Chance.

**Backbeat Timing:** Doubles Critical Chance. Increases the Duration and [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HeatSymbol.png/14px-HeatSymbol.png?8e0e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HeatSymbol.png)Heat Damage of Ripper’s Wail when it deals damage.

### **[Ripper's Wail](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ripper%27s_Wail)**
Rip on the guitar to make Temple briefly invulnerable while healing them. Ally’s weapons in [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Affinity.png/14px-Affinity.png?aad53)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Affinity.png)Affinity Range are lit-up with extra [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HeatSymbol.png/14px-HeatSymbol.png?8e0e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HeatSymbol.png)Heat Damage each time an ability is used on the Backbeat and successfully hits enemies.

**Backbeat Timing:** Invulnerability lasts longer.

### **[Exalted Solo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Solo)**
Once a charge has been built up on the Backbeat metronome, set Lizzie aflame and torch enemies with [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HeatSymbol.png/14px-HeatSymbol.png?8e0e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HeatSymbol.png)Heat. While aimed, Lizzie blasts enemies with thunderous elemental sound waves, use ability controls to change the Elemental Damage type.

**Backbeat Timing:** No Energy consumed.

> Flare/Temple

Prior to meeting [[Albrecht Entrati]], [Flare](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Flare "Flare") was already a rockstar with the stage name Temple of their band "Temple and the Rippers." Flare received their [Protoframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protoframe "Protoframe") injection of a Warframe strain that was never built in the future due to its inherently rebellious nature, making Flare the original Temple Warframe born in [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999").

Accepting the fate that they will fully turn eventually, Temple would hibernate on a comet for an untold amount of time until its round trip for [The Collapse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Collapse "The Collapse"), where they would join their fellow Warframes of the future and ignite the flames of revolution.
