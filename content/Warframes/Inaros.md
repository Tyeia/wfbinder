**Release Date:** March 4th, 2016

Entombed in his sarcophagus, as shifting sand pours through the hourglass, resurrect the Cursed Pharaoh **Inaros** to consume and conquer all life in his sandstorm burial. Engulf the outlanders in barren desert, where his sacred scarabs and [Kavats](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kavat "Kavat") thrive. Inaros arose in [Update 18.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_18#Update_18.5 "Update 18") (2016-03-04).

**Inaros** is a mummy-like [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that calls forth the sands to bolster his survivability and crowd control. ## Abilities

### Passive
- On fatal injury and entering [Bleedout](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bleedout "Bleedout"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/InarosIcon272.png/31px-InarosIcon272.png?aa366)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Inaros) [Inaros](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Inaros) entombs himself into a [Sarcophagus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarcophagus "wikipedia:Sarcophagus") and incarnates as a sand version of himself, able to revive himself by melee attacking (default E ) enemies and siphoning their life force. Alternatively, allies may manually revive Inaros by interacting with his Sarcophagus

- Killing enemies with melee [Finishers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Finisher "Finisher") restores **20%** of Inaros's maximum [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health").
### **[Desiccation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desiccation)**
Blast enemies with a wave of cursed sand that blinds them and steals their health.
> [!note]
> Pocket sand

### **[Sandstorm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sandstorm)**
Become a sandstorm. Inaros devours enemies pulled into his whirlwind, healing himself.
### **[Scarab Shell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scarab_Shell)**
Activate to form Armor by draining Inaros' Health. Activate again to stop the formation early. The protective layer reduces incoming Damage and absorbs Status Effects.
### **[Scarab Swarm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scarab_Swarm)**
Summon a Scarab Swarm to attack enemies with guaranteed Corrosion Status. Damage scales with Inaros' Health. Enemies killed while immersed in the swarm summon a Swarm Kavat that fights alongside Inaros and guides the scarabs to other enemies.

## Lore

[[Sands of Inaros| Quest: Sands of Inaros]]

>Hush my little dune. You don't need to be scared. There, there, that's right, shake out the sand, but remember, not all of it, and I'll tell you the story. Long ago, the lands of our colony were cursed, they were soaked in fear. The [Golden Skymen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") would come and take our children away. Young and old alike, cowered before them, afraid to lose their most beloved. But then he came. The Fear-eater. The skykiller. He was called Inaros!

Inaros was a warrior who served the [Golden Skymen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin"), but did not approve of them taking away the children of the devout sky-worshippers of [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"). Inaros eventually defected to protect the colony. The Skymen set their armies upon Inaros, but none could prevail against him. The Skymen fled, and Inaros was hailed as the God King of the Mars people before he ascended to watch over the colony from his throne in the sky.

Years later, the Mars colony was attacked by [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested"). Inaros came to their defense and swept away the plague, but his metal body was found broken. Believing his spirit returned to the sky, the colony entombed his body in Sacred Vessels, knowing he will one day reclaim his body and rise again.

A young [Baro Ki'Teer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Baro_Ki%27Teer "Baro Ki'Teer") was part of the Mars colony that worshipped Inaros, but one day it was beset by [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer"). Despite Baro Ki'Teer's prayers, Inaros never came and the colony was eradicated, and he, as the colony's sole survivor, grew to resent the God King.

In present day, Baro Ki'Teer hires the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") to excavate Inaros's tomb, but a mysterious voice narrates the legend of Inaros, leaving Baro Ki'Teer distressed. While completing the challenges left on the Sacred Vessels, the voice is revealed to belong to Baro Ki'Teer's late mother. Coming to peace with his past, Baro Ki'Teer thanks the Tenno, remarking that the God King will rise again.
