**Release Date:** July 6th, 2021

Rise from the cold depths below and brave the waves above, across an azure expanse basked in starlight's gleam. Submerse into the deep marine with **Yareli**, the nymphean sailor. Fluid as tidal ebb and flow, surf swiftly and ripple through her foes, as slender teardrops cascade into a sanguine ocean. Yareli and Merulina splashed into battle on [Update 30.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_30#Update_30.5 "Update 30") (2021-07-06).

**Yareli** is an aquatic rider [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that deals damage and battlefield control. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases secondary weapon [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") while she is mobile.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SeaSnares130xWhite.png/32px-SeaSnares130xWhite.png?7b638)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sea_Snares) [Sea Snares](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sea_Snares) forms water globules that seek enemies to place them in aerial stasis while increasing [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Merulina130xWhite.png/32px-Merulina130xWhite.png?f2d7c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Merulina) [Merulina](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Merulina) summons her trusty [K-Drive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/K-Drive "K-Drive") which possesses [Damage Redirection](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Redirection "Damage Redirection").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Aquablades130xWhite.png/32px-Aquablades130xWhite.png?55e3d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aquablades) [Aquablades](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aquablades) summons a trio of orbiting blades.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Riptide130xWhite.png/32px-Riptide130xWhite.png?7b638)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riptide) [Riptide](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riptide) summons a water cyclone that pulls enemies into its spiral before exploding.

## Abilities

### Passive
Everflowing with the seas, whenever  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/YareliIcon272.png/31px-YareliIcon272.png?0b164)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli) [Yareli](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli) moves for at least **1.5** seconds, she gains the **Critical Flow** buff that increases [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") on her secondary [Weapon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Weapon "Weapon") by **200%**. Critical Flow lasts until Yareli stops moving for **1** second.

### **[Sea Snares](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sea_Snares)**
Form three water globules that seek out enemies and expand on contact, simultaneously damaging and immobilizing their victims.

### **[Merulina](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Merulina)**
Summon Merulina, a rideable creature of the waves, and the inspiration for K-Driving. Merulina protects Yareli by absorbing a large portion of incoming damage.

### **[Aquablades](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aquablades)**
Tear through foes with a trio of orbiting aquatic blades.

### **[Riptide](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riptide)**
Drag enemies into a crushing maelstrom and then blow them away in a watery burst. Each enemy trapped in the vortex increases the burst's Damage.

## Lore

### Creation

> Of all the [Yuvan Theater](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvan_Theater "Yuvan Theater"), your vivacity most captivated me. I would have [worn that face with joy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Continuity_\(Lore\) "Continuity (Lore)"). But you concealed a deep disrespect beneath shallow waters.
> 
> You betrayed our trust, mocked our ways, even dishonored the sacred [Kuva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva "Kuva").
> 
> So [others shall wear you now](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"), fatuous nymph. Little ocean flower. Little... Yareli.

—[Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas")

The person that became Yareli was formerly a [Yuvan](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvan "Yuvan"), who was originally noticed by [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") in a [Yuvan Theater](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvan_Theater "Yuvan Theater"), drawing his attention as a candidate for the [Continuity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Continuity_\(Lore\) "Continuity (Lore)"), but he became frustrated with her thinly-veiled disrespect for Orokin customs and beliefs, which went so far as to "dishonor the sacred [Kuva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva "Kuva")." As retribution, he had her turned into a Warframe, so that her body would be "worn" (through [Transference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Transference_\(Lore\) "Transference (Lore)")) by many individuals.

### The Waverider
Yareli was the savior of children who were enslaved by a slave-driver named **Vulgran**. The rescue inspired the children to rise up against their slaver before she blasted him into the sky. Her tale is passed down through a comic Graphica dubbed **Waverider #1**.

Yareli also served as the originator of [K-Driving](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/K-Drive "K-Drive") and inspired the [Ventkids](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ventkids "Ventkids") to follow in her footsteps.
