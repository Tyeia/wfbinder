**Release Date:** November 20th, 2013

Driven by feral rage, **Valkyr** attacks rend her prey with fury and ferociousness, while her tortured cries break the imposing silence of the Tenno presence. Valkyr was unleashed in [Update 11.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_11#Update_11.0 "Update 11") (2013-11-20).

**Valkyr** is a berserker-themed [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") boasting high [health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") and [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") as she relentlessly rushes enemies with flurries of [melee](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee "Melee") assaults. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") builds a gauge that increases her melee damage while also allowing her to negate [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RipLine130xWhite.png/32px-RipLine130xWhite.png?630e6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rip_Line) [Rip Line](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rip_Line) is a gap closer that can also group enemies, while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Warcry130xWhite.png/32px-Warcry130xWhite.png?a4609)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warcry) [Warcry](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warcry) strengthen Valkyr and her teammate's armor and [Attack Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Attack_Speed "Attack Speed") and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paralysis130xWhite.png/32px-Paralysis130xWhite.png?1502d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paralysis) [Paralysis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paralysis) weakens enemies with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/UnidentifiedItem.png/32px-UnidentifiedItem.png?6eed9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) [Slow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) and melee [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Hysteria130xWhite.png/32px-Hysteria130xWhite.png?1e748)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hysteria) [Hysteria](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hysteria) renders Valkyr immune to [Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") while her attacks heal her, as she bears her deadly  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ValkyrTalons.png/32px-ValkyrTalons.png?d8e5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr_Talons) [Valkyr Talons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr_Talons).

## Abilities

### Passive
- **Rage**: Driven by fury,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ValkyrIcon272.png/31px-ValkyrIcon272.png?ef9f1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr) [Valkyr](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr) builds **Rage** from meleeing and killing enemies, gaining up to **300%** additional melee damage bonus. Additionally, if Valkyr receives a fatal hit while at or above **150%** Rage, all of it is consumed to prevent [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death"), grant her **5** seconds of [invulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable") and regenerate **100%** health. This ability has no cooldown.
- **Nimble**: Valkyr is immune to [Hard Landings](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hard_Landing "Hard Landing"), landing with no touchdown delay, without the need to roll, slide, or aim-glide beforehand.
### **[Rip Line](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rip_Line)**
Valkyr hurls forth a hook and pulls herself to whatever it hits. If it hits an enemy, nearby foes are pulled as she unleashes a coordinated Melee attack.

### **[Warcry](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warcry)**
Valkyr lets out a rallying cry that bolsters Armor and Attack Speed for allies in [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Affinity.png/14px-Affinity.png?aad53)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Affinity.png)Affinity Range.

### **[Paralysis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paralysis)**
Unleash a damaging blast, slowing its victims while increasing their Melee Damage Vulnerability.

### **[Hysteria](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hysteria)**
Valkyr bares her deadly claws, unleashing devastating attacks that also heal her. Valkyr becomes immune to Status Effects and her Armor bonus from Warcry is multiplied.

## Lore

Previously having thrived among the Tenno in her original [Gersemi form](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr_Gersemi_Skin "Valkyr Gersemi Skin"), Valkyr is known for her capture and subsequent torment at the hands of [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V"). The Warframe-obsessed Corpus scientist brought Valkyr to his lab on [Jupiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jupiter "Jupiter"), where she was [restrained](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr%27s_Bonds "Valkyr's Bonds") and subjected to [harsh experiments](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka_Project "Zanuka Project"), including having her skin flayed. This mad science would greatly expand the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus")' knowledge of [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") design and influence the design of their own robotic proxies, and consequently left Valkyr visibly scarred, maddened, and enraged. Upon comparison with Gersemi, it can be seen that Alad V has added forearm restraints and gauntlets relating to the claws in her fourth ability [Hysteria](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hysteria "Hysteria"). This addition may have been inspired by the armor on her original [Prime Design](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr/Prime "Valkyr/Prime").

Valkyr's skin was used as the outer armor for the [Zanuka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka "Zanuka") prototype, Alad V's companion proxy— hybridized with components from several Warframes.

Valkyr's [abilities](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr/Abilities "Valkyr/Abilities") reflect her madness, accompanied by [cries of anguish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr/Media#Voicelines "Valkyr/Media"). It is not confirmed whether Gersemi shared these cries, though it is likely, since her Prime version does due to torture at Orokin hands.
