## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Gaze upon the Crystal Bastion. Citrine's crystalline might supports allies on the battlefield. Combat only enhances her fractal beauty.

---


**Release Date:** February 15th, 2023

Glisten bright with gemstone purity and become bejeweled in adamant amber. Revealed by luminous lustre, witness her pristine beauty as the Crystal Bastion, **Citrine**, ascends from the cavernous unknown. Embrace beloved kin in her diamond lattice, then fracture brilliant light into its elemental spectrum. Summon saffron shards and fractal fissures soaring toward the surface, to embed and embalm the fallen within exquisite mementos.

## Abilities

### Passive

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CitrineIcon272.png/31px-CitrineIcon272.png?d91d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Citrine) [Citrine](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Citrine) emits a healing aura within a radius of **50** meters, granting the **Geoluminesence** buff to herself and allies that continuously recover a base **5** [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") points per second. Whenever she picks up a regular [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Dispensary130xWhite.png/32px-Dispensary130xWhite.png?b3988)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary) [Dispensary](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary) produced Empowered variant, or  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TransmutationProbe130xWhite.png/32px-TransmutationProbe130xWhite.png?e7a81)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Transmutation_Probe) [Transmutation Probe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Transmutation_Probe) converted [Universal Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Universal_Orb "Universal Orb"), regeneration rate is permanently increased by **0.1** points, up to a maximum heal rate of **25** points per second after collecting **200** Health Orbs.

- Until her passive reach max stacks, Citrine innately can always collect Health Orbs to increase her passive healing even when unharmed.
- Allies gain or lose Geoluminesence whenever they enter or exit Citrine's Passive radius.
    - Passive radius matches [Affinity Range](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Affinity_Range "Affinity Range") distance, but is separate from it therefore [Fosfor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fosfor "Fosfor") and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FocusMendingUnity.png/32px-FocusMendingUnity.png?dacbd)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Vazarin#Mending_Unity) [Mending Unity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Vazarin#Mending_Unity) do **not** affect Geoluminesence.
- Passive heal rate is indicated by a diamond-shaped UI indicator above the ability icons. The accumulated passive value occupies the upper red triangle, while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/PreservingShell130xWhite.png/32px-PreservingShell130xWhite.png?2ad2f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Preserving_Shell) [Preserving Shell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Preserving_Shell)'s real-time damage reduction and remaining duration countdown curve occupy the lower orange triangle, only appearing when the ability is active.

### **[Fractured Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fractured_Blast)**
Slash and stagger enemies with a crystal blast that inflicts Bleed. Enemies afflicted with this Status Effect have an increase chance of dropping Health and Energy Orbs.
> [!note]
> Imagine shooting a shotgun shell loaded with gems

### **[Preserving Shell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Preserving_Shell)**
Citrine guards herself and nearby allies with a crystalline shell that gradually decays. Kills and assists increase the defensive power of the shell.

### **[Prismatic Gem](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prismatic_Gem)**
Deploy a gem that shoots prismatic beams. The gem targets enemies that are taking weapon damage from Citrine and her allies. Its beams inflict Heat, Cold, Toxin, and Electricity Status Effects. Status Chance and Status Duration increase for nearby allies.

### **[Crystallize](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Crystallize)**
Citrine summons crystal fractals. The fractals rush forward, seeking enemies. Enemies touched by the fractals are paralyzed crystalline growths. Hit the growths to deal Critical Damage.
> [!note]
> Enemies in range get frozen and get a weakspot


## Lore

During [The Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Old_War "The Old War"), Citrine was the guardian Warframe of **[Belric and Rania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Belric_%26_Rania "Belric & Rania")**. When the [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") began their advance on [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"), the couple set off a bio-bomb, releasing the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested") on the moon. Instead of fleeing, Citrine chose to sacrifice herself, transmuting the couple's bodies into crystals to prevent them from being infested.

After Deimos returned to Martian orbit, smugglers discovered that separating Belric and Rania's remains would create a [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") tunnel through the fabric of reality, linking the two together; a [Conceptual Embodiment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conceptual_Embodiment "Conceptual Embodiment") of their love. In addition to the tunnel, the Void would also generate **Citrine's Remnants**; small crystal fragments formed from Citrine's desire to protect the couple in her final moments. Collecting enough of them will spawn one of Citrine's  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/PrismaticGem130xWhite.png/32px-PrismaticGem130xWhite.png?dad60)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prismatic_Gem) [Prismatic Gem](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prismatic_Gem)s above one of the crystal formations, aiding in its protection.
