## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Achieve redemption through vengeance. The monastic Harrow increases damage to support allies. Defy enemies and ascend.

---

> This is Harrow, the monastic, the ritualist.
>
>Harrow is suffragan to the Void. Will you believe, Tenno?

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:** June 28th, 2017

Dauntless and devout, the dark priest **Harrow** bestows upon his coven three virtues: salvation through sacrifice, redemption through vengeance, and ascension through defiance. Nemeses beware, for only death awaits. Enchain the tainted, silence their heresy, and deliver their tormented souls unto the Void's embrace. Harrow was absolved in [Update 21.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_21#Update_21.0 "Update 21") (2017-06-29).

**Harrow** is a monastic [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that provides damage and support. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") doubles the amount of [Overshields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overshield "Overshield") he can gain and enters missions with full [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Condemn130xWhite.png/32px-Condemn130xWhite.png?bedc9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condemn) [Condemn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condemn) releases chains that binds enemies and restores his [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Penance130xWhite.png/32px-Penance130xWhite.png?d9ca7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Penance) [Penance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Penance) increases [Fire Rate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fire_Rate "Fire Rate") and [Reload Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reload_Speed "Reload Speed") and grants Harrow lifesteal for himself and allies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Thurible130xWhite.png/32px-Thurible130xWhite.png?7f218)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Thurible) [Thurible](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Thurible) channels a ritual to recover energy on kill, with bonus energy from [headshots](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Covenant130xWhite.png/32px-Covenant130xWhite.png?a5bf7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Covenant) [Covenant](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Covenant) protects Harrow and his allies with brief [Invulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerability "Invulnerability"), after which absorbed damage is converted into increased [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance"), further amplified for headshots.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HarrowIcon272.png/31px-HarrowIcon272.png?bebac)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Harrow) [Harrow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Harrow) possesses **200%** [Overshields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overshields "Overshields") capacity, granting him a maximum of **2,400** Overshields, and spawns into missions with **100%** [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") filled, identical to how the mod  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Preparation) [Preparation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Preparation) works.

### **[Condemn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condemn)**
Cast a wave of energy that chains them where they stand. Each enemy held reinforces Harrow’s shields.
> [!note]
> Stuns enemies

### **[Penance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Penance)**
Sacrifice shields to boost reload, and fire rate while converting damage inflicted on enemies into health for Harrow and nearby allies.

### **[Thurible](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Thurible)**
Channel Harrow’s energy into the Thurible to generate a buff. Once finished, kill enemies to bestow nearby allies with bursts of energy. The more energy channeled the greater the reward for each kill. Headshots produce extra energy.
> [!note]
> Energy on Kill. Charge up to increase amount of energy per kill


### **[Covenant](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Covenant)**
Protect nearby allies with an energy force that absorbs all damage and converts it to a Critical Chance bonus for all those under the Covenant. Headshots are amplified even further.
> [!note]
> All allies become invulnerable. Based on damage avoided, increase crit chance
