Death's crimson maiden sharpens her talons. Garuda weaponizes the blood of her foes to deal high damage. Sanguine beauty complements her deadly power.

>Cleansed by the blood of her enemies, she draws strength from vulnerability.

**Release Date:** November 8th, 2018

Stain her talons crimson, steep the soil in red. To quell her endless bloodlust, the murderous maiden **Garuda** slays her prey with coldblooded elegance and strides upon their corpses in a horrific display of visceral carnage. Garuda slaughtered her way to a blood soaked unveiling in [Update 24.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_24#Update_24.0 "Update 24") (2018-11-08).

**Garuda** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that thrives on the blood of her enemies. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") grants increased damage on enemy kill, and becomes armed with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GarudaTalons.png/32px-GarudaTalons.png?e60fe)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Garuda_Talons) [Garuda Talons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Garuda_Talons) when not wielding a melee weapon.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DreadMirror130xWhite.png/32px-DreadMirror130xWhite.png?dd3a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dread_Mirror) [Dread Mirror](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dread_Mirror) pounces an enemy to seize their life force to create a protective shield and channel a heart that can be thrown.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/BloodAltar130xWhite.png/32px-BloodAltar130xWhite.png?524d6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blood_Altar) [Blood Altar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blood_Altar) impales an enemy to create a field that regenerates ally [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Bloodletting130xWhite.png/32px-Bloodletting130xWhite.png?524d6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bloodletting) [Bloodletting](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bloodletting) sacrifices health to recover [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SeekingTalons130xWhite.png/32px-SeekingTalons130xWhite.png?dd3a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seeking_Talons) [Seeking Talons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seeking_Talons) hurls talons at enemies who have a chance to receive  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgSlashSmall64.png/32px-DmgSlashSmall64.png?bab47)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) [Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) damage upon being injured.

## Abilities

### Passive
The more  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GarudaIcon272.png/31px-GarudaIcon272.png?55b93)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Garuda) [Garuda](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Garuda) kills the more vicious her attacks become; Garuda gains up to **100%** additional damage bonus for her abilities and weapons for each kill or assisted kill.

### **[Dread Mirror](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dread_Mirror)**
Rip the life force from an enemy and use it as a shield that captures damage. Charge to channel the captured damage into an explosive projectile.

### **[Blood Altar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blood_Altar)**
Impale an enemy on an altar of talons and siphon health for Garuda and her allies.

### **[Bloodletting](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bloodletting)**
Garuda sacrifices her health to generate energy.

### **[Seeking Talons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seeking_Talons)**
Charge to expand the targeting area, release to send Garuda’s talons careening toward each target in area. Surviving enemies are prone to bleeding.
