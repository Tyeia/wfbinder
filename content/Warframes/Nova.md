## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Harness antimatter. Nova uses antiparticles to provide crowd control and amplify damage output. She obliterates enemies by destabilizing matter itself.

---

> This is Nova, wildly unpredictable.
> 
> Nova's presence on the battlefield is complex and deadly. Handle with care, Tenno.

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:** July 13th, 2013

Traverse across the universe at lightspeed by rematerializing through spatial portals. Destabilize molecular bonds with potent antimatter, triggering an explosive cascade of atomic chain reactions. Simulate the nuclear destruction of dying stars, and harness the astral phenomena as the advanced prototype, codename: **Nova**. Nova materialized in [Update 9.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_9#Update_9.0 "Update 9") (2013-07-13).

**Nova** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with antimatter and molecular crowd controlling powers. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") has increased [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") drop chance on slain enemies that are  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/UnidentifiedItem.png/32px-UnidentifiedItem.png?6eed9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) [Slowed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow), or [Energy Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Orb "Energy Orb") drop chance on slain enemies that are sped-up.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NullStar130xWhite.png/32px-NullStar130xWhite.png?c6be5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Null_Star) [Null Star](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Null_Star) surrounds her with particles that provide [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") and seek out nearby enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AntimatterDrop130xWhite.png/32px-AntimatterDrop130xWhite.png?0a61f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Antimatter_Drop) [Antimatter Drop](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Antimatter_Drop) condenses a particle of antimatter that absorbs all damage which is accumulated into an explosion upon collision.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/WormHole130xWhite.png/32px-WormHole130xWhite.png?71091)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wormhole) [Wormhole](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wormhole) creates a portal for instantaneous travel on its exit point.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MolecularPrime130xWhite.png/32px-MolecularPrime130xWhite.png?5ac56)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime) [Molecular Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime) releases a wave of antimatter that slows or speeds up enemies while applying [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability") and exploding upon death.

## Abilities

### Passive
Enemies killed by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NovaIcon272.png/31px-NovaIcon272.png?fea10)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nova) [Nova](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nova) while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/UnidentifiedItem.png/32px-UnidentifiedItem.png?6eed9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) [Slowed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) have a **15%** chance to drop a [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pickups#Orbs "Pickups"); enemies killed while sped-up have a **15%** chance to drop an [Energy Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pickups#Orbs "Pickups"). The slow/speed-up can be from any source, including Nova's  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MolecularPrime130xWhite.png/32px-MolecularPrime130xWhite.png?5ac56)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime) [Molecular Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime).

### **[Null Star](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Null_Star)**
Creates anti-matter particles that orbit Nova and seek nearby targets.

### **[Antimatter Drop](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Antimatter_Drop)**
Launches a contained particle of antimatter that will detonate upon collision.

### **[Wormhole](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wormhole)**
Creates a wormhole allowing instantaneous travel.

### **[Molecular Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime)**
Primes all enemies in a radius with anti-matter.
> [!note]
> Enemies are slowed or sped up and take double damage

## Lore

**Holsom Yurr** was a notorious [Solar Rail](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solar_Rail "Solar Rail") pirate who was hired by an unknown party to neutralize Nova. In his calculated plan, the pirate lured Nova onto an  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) passenger vessel known as the _**[Masker's Theodolite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Masker%27s_Theodolite?action=edit&redlink=1 "Masker's Theodolite (page does not exist)")**_, and ruptured its antimatter reactor as it was being drawn into a gravity well of a planetoid. Once Nova was aboard, he jettisoned all of the ship's escape pods and then the antimatter core. Without the core, the ship's reactor ceased to function, threatening the lives of its 10,800 crew. Nova, a being that contained and harnesses antimatter, would be forced to sacrifice herself in order to replace the antimatter core.

Turning to the technicians she had saved, Nova threw her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Hikou.png/32px-Hikou.png?e3ccb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hikou) [Hikou](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hikou) stars at Yurr's surveillance cameras and devised a strategy. She then walked into the reactor chamber, only for the technicians to override the blast doors to allow her to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/WormHole130xWhite.png/32px-WormHole130xWhite.png?71091)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wormhole) [Wormhole](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wormhole) into the bridge and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MolecularPrime130xWhite.png/32px-MolecularPrime130xWhite.png?5ac56)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime) [Molecular Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime) Yurr and his mercenaries, picking them off with her [Alamos Sniper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alamos_Sniper_Skin "Alamos Sniper Skin"). She then grabbed Yurr and threw him back into the series of Worm Holes, bringing him into the reactor chamber, before the portals collapsed and the blast doors sealed shut.

The pirate's molecules reached critical and his body went nova, and the ship's engines roared to life. Yurr met his fate as antimatter fuel for the _Masker's Theodolite_.
