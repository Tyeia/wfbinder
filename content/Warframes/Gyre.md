## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Annihilate enemies with the conductivity of coils and transmitters. Gyre's electric attacks provide crowd control. She is the embodiment of elegant theory.

---

**Release Date:** April 27, 2022

Graceful in every step with clockwork precision, her mechanized pirouette captivates the audience in electrifying shock and awe. Execute and electrocute elegantly as **Gyre**, the dancing spark. Flash across the stage as a living thunderbolt and strike true as lucent lightning. A gentle facade conceals her lethal touch, thus with regal poise she bids adieu in her danse macabre finale. Gyre graced us with her presence in [Update 31.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_31#Update_31.5 "Update 31") (2022-04-27).

The graceful ballerina [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") **Gyre** utilizes her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) for crowd control. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases ability [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") to enemies with Electricity status.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Arcsphere130xWhite.png/32px-Arcsphere130xWhite.png?74c85)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcsphere) [Arcsphere](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcsphere) hurls a sphere that produces an Electricity field, while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CoilHorizon130xWhite.png/32px-CoilHorizon130xWhite.png?b890d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Coil_Horizon) [Coil Horizon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Coil_Horizon) spheres detonate to group enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CathodeGrace130xWhite.png/32px-CathodeGrace130xWhite.png?b5682)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cathode_Grace) [Cathode Grace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cathode_Grace) grants weapon and ability Critical Chance and [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") over time with its duration extendable from kills.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Rotorswell130xWhite.png/32px-Rotorswell130xWhite.png?383b3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rotorswell) [Rotorswell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rotorswell) generates a field of electricity around Gyre as her Critical Hits release electrical chaining discharges.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GyreIcon272.png/31px-GyreIcon272.png?4c4ae)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gyre) [Gyre](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gyre)'s abilities gain a flat **10%** [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") per active  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) status proc affecting an individual enemy to deal **2.0x** [Critical Damage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Damage "Critical Damage") against that enemy.

### **[Arcsphere](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcsphere)**
Launch a Gyratory Sphere that will deal high damage on impact and periodically deal electrical shocks to nearby enemies. Hit multiple enemies at once with the initial launch to enhance damage.
> [!note]
> Creates a large electric field that shocks enemies
> 

### **[Coil Horizon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Coil_Horizon)**
Throw forward a Gyratory Sphere that will implode after a few seconds or can be manually triggered.

### **[Cathode Grace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cathode_Grace)**
Gain a brief burst of increased Critical Chance and Energy Regen, with each kill extending duration of Cathode Grace. Casting is on a cooldown.

### **[Rotorswell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rotorswell)**
Gyre’s mechanisms spin at incredible speeds, generating an Electric Field that shocks nearby enemies. When Gyre gets a critical hit, a large electrical discharge will chain from the enemy that was hit to nearby enemies.
> [!note]
> Crits = Lightning Bolts


## Lore

>Every ship needs a figurehead. Executor [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") presented us with sweet Gyra on the day of our departure. She spoke with her movement. Her dancing was meant to bring joy, but when we entered the Void... such madness, and one so gentle. The way you move now reminds me of her.

—[Quinn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quinn "Quinn")

After the events of Angels of the Zariman, when speaking to [Quinn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quinn "Quinn") as Gyre for first time in the [Chrysalith](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chrysalith "Chrysalith"), he reminisces about the day of the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero")'s departure for the [Tau System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau_System "Tau System").

A passenger by the name of **Gyra** was sent aboard by Orokin Executor [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") to act as a figurehead. She was renown for her graceful movements and was a gentle soul, yet misfortune befell her as the Zariman crossed the fold in the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"). She succumbed to madness and insanity same as the rest of the ship's occupants.
