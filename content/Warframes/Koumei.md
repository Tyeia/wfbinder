## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Test your luck with the dice-maiden. Koumei weaves fate to unleash damage and ensure her survival. To the virtuous, she is an oracle. To the wicked, a perilous game of chance.

---

**Release Date:** October 2, 2024

Chance thy mortal fate.  
Unravel these tangled threads.  
Dare now cast my dice.  

Weave the strands of destiny as its seamstress, prophet and marionette. Raise the stakes and gamble the future of Origin, take a risk or many by lady luck's hand. Defy fate's preordained decree, repeat and escape its spiral unscathed as the Dice-Maiden, **Koumei**. Protect thyself with serendipitous charms, then bestow misfortune onto thy foes, so too shall they dangle on  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) strings. Koumei cast her dice on [Update 37.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_37#Update_37.0 "Update 37") (2024-10-02).

**Koumei** is a luck-themed [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") who constantly tempts fate. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") has a randomly selected weapon to imbue random [Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect"), while all her abilities roll dice to enhance their effects with a perfect roll enhancing the ability.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Kumihimo.png/32px-Kumihimo.png?4fc18)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kumihimo) [Kumihimo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kumihimo) weaves threads in an area that apply random statuses,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Omikuji.png/32px-Omikuji.png?4f9eb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omikuji) [Omikuji](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omikuji) invokes a challenge that upon completion grants a random [Decree](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Decree "Decree"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Omamori.png/32px-Omamori.png?1a7cf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omamori) [Omamori](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omamori) summons protective charms that have a chance to negate damage while healing Koumei, and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Bunraku.png/32px-Bunraku.png?be105)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bunraku) [Bunraku](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bunraku) binds enemies on puppet strings as they receive random statuses.

## Abilities

### Passive
- Dare to tempt her fate, risk all in this game of chance, soon shall she succeed. Every **1** minute, fate selects one of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/KoumeiIcon272.png/31px-KoumeiIcon272.png?5abcc)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Koumei) [Koumei](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Koumei)'s equipped weapons to impart randomized [Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") when dealing [Damage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage "Damage") for **1** minute.
- Casting an ability rolls **The Five Fates**, up to **5** six-sided dice displayed above Koumei's ability icons. The dice of fate randomly roll to one of their six sides, combining the plum blossom petals into a total number; the total dice roll determines the effectiveness of some abilities from a minimum of **1** to a maximum of **30**. When rolling at least three dice in a triple **6** formation, known as **Shadow's Trinity**, the ability is amplified with a bonus effect. If all dice were fair (each of the faces had the same probability of landing facing up, ⅙ in this case), there would approximately a 3.55% chance of getting Shadow's Trinity. However, Koumei has an approximately **10%** chance to roll Shadow's Trinity.
	-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Kumihimo.png/32px-Kumihimo.png?4fc18)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kumihimo) [Kumihimo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kumihimo): Damage is multiplied by the dice roll. Triple sixes causes all threads of destiny to apply one stack of each primary and secondary elemental status effect.
	-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Omikuji.png/32px-Omikuji.png?4f9eb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omikuji) [Omikuji](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omikuji): Immediately awards Koumei a [Decree](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Decree "Decree") without needing to complete a challenge.
	-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Omamori.png/32px-Omamori.png?1a7cf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omamori) [Omamori](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omamori): Summons Omamori Charms whose number is determined by the total dice roll. Triple sixes grant [Invulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerability "Invulnerability") for as long as one charm remains.
	-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Bunraku.png/32px-Bunraku.png?be105)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bunraku) [Bunraku](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bunraku): Entraps enemies in strings while applying random status effects, the number of stacks is determined by the total dice roll. Triple sixes apply the maximum number of status effect stacks (30) and extends the ability's range behind Koumei.

### **[Kumihimo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kumihimo)**
Weave the threads of destiny. Enemies who touch the threads suffer a random Elemental Status Effect. A roll of triple sixes creates threads that inflict one of every Elemental Status Effect.
> [!note]
> Threads form perpendicular to you in front of you. If enemies touch them, they deal Status Effects

### **[Omikuji](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omikuji)**
Koumei glimpses a favorable future and the precise steps needed to reach it. Complete the challenge to earn a Decree. A roll of triple sixes grants a Decree without a challenge.
> [!note]
> Complete a challenge, get a buff

### **[Omamori](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omamori)**
Surround yourself with Omamori Charms, each with a chance to have enemy attacks heal you instead of damage you. The number of charms is determined by Koumei’s dice roll. A roll of triple sixes grants invulnerability for the duration of your Omamori Charms.
### **[Bunraku](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bunraku)**
Wield your foe’s fate threads like the strings of marionettes. Koumei’s dice determine how many Status Effects foes in front of Koumei will suffer. A roll of triple sixes maximizes Status Effect stacks and extends Bunraku to enemies behind Koumei.
> [!note]
> Enemies get stunned

## Lore

>Koumei, what shall be my fate? Adrift in the river of destiny, I will flow with you. Gamble with my life, for I choose to roll the dice. Cast out evil, though I see it is a part of me. To make this choice is my only choice. Koumei, what shall be my fate?

—Ostron Worshipper

When the sea-side village of [Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus") was still known as Karifamil, **[Mer-Sah](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mer-Sah "Mer-Sah")**, one the village founders, would cast himself into the sea. Due to his absence, all order began to break down; all the wild animals of the [Plains](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon"), even the [Eidolons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon"), returned to their feral nature. It then said that Koumei herself arrived, rising from sea beyond Cetus.

Unlike  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TheQuillsSigil.png/32px-TheQuillsSigil.png?954cb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Quills) [The Quills](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Quills), who keep their visions of fate to themselves, Koumei would openly provide knowledge on potential futures, becoming an oracle for the common folk of Cetus. Now worshipped by the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/OstronSigil.png/32px-OstronSigil.png?ba21e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron) [Ostrons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron), Koumei would divine the fate of others with her fortune-telling dice. She would also deliver prophetic dreams, called **Fate Dreams**, as warnings of possible disasters, and allowed people to combat these dreams to seek for a better ending.
### The Five Fates
The Five Fates are Koumei's set of five dice, which she uses to predict and divine the future. When visiting her shrine, the Ostrons may cast the Fates and read their patterns to determine their futures. If the future is unfavorable, they may also present Koumei with offerings at her shrine in an attempt to re-spin their fates.
