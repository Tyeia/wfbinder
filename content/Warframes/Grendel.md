**Release Date:** October 31st, 2019

Indulge amid the stench of battle as **Grendel** the voracious gourmand, greedily swallowing armies whole into his fathomless maw. Corpulent and courageous, he consumes prey to invigorate kindred warriors, or disgorges the eaten out drenched in putrid acidic bile. Stampede through strongholds as a spherical menace, leaving cadavers in the wake crushed by his thunderous quake. Grendel's feeding frenzy began in [Update 26](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_26 "Update 26"): The Old Blood.

**Grendel** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that devours his foes to increase his survivability. into his mass and can hurl them out ## Abilities

### Passive
Devouring his prey whole,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GrendelIcon272.png/31px-GrendelIcon272.png?a4344)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grendel) [Grendel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grendel) slowly digests the enemies he swallows via  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Feast130xWhite.png/32px-Feast130xWhite.png?d2079)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Feast) [Feast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Feast). Each enemy still alive in Grendel's belly passively grants him **250** [Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor"), to a maximum of **1,250** bonus armor at the **5** enemy cap.
- Passive bonus value is displayed above Grendel's ability icons.
### **[Feast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Feast)**
Swallow an enemy whole and store it in Grendel’s gut. Hold to vomit out stored enemies covering them in toxic bile.
### **[Nourish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish)**
Regenerate health as Grendel absorbs nourishment from enemies in his gut. While he digests, enemies that attack or are attacked by Grendel suffer [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ViralSymbol.png/14px-ViralSymbol.png?bdf42)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:ViralSymbol.png)Viral Damage and energy sources provide more energy. These buffs extend to squad mates.
### **[Pulverize](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pulverize)**
Grendel curls into a ball. He heals over time as he rolls, knocking over anyone in his path. Jumping slams Grendel into the ground and generates a damaging shockwave.
### **[Regurgitate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Regurgitate)**
Violently puke out a bile soaked enemy from Grendel’s gut, turning the consumed into a toxic projectile.

## Lore

After the [Collapse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Story_and_History "Story and History") of the Orokin Empire, Karishh found refuge within the frozen, famine-struck city of **[Riddha](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riddha?action=edit&redlink=1 "Riddha (page does not exist)")**, located on **[Europa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Europa "Europa")**. There he would live a lavish life, consuming feast after feast while the emaciated citizens looked on.

The [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes")  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GrendelIcon272.png/31px-GrendelIcon272.png?a4344)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grendel) Grendel and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GaussIcon272.png/31px-GaussIcon272.png?46f33)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gauss) [Gauss](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gauss) would then appear to liberate Riddha from Karishh's control. Gauss would remain near the entrance of the city, presumably intercepting any returning patrols, while Grendel would go on to plow straight into the city, consuming all of Karishh's sycophants and [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") soldiers. With his gut full, Grendel would then  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Nourish130xWhite.png/32px-Nourish130xWhite.png?d2079)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish) [Nourish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish) all the afflicted masses of Riddha with the power he consumed, leading them to join him in storming the Karishh's manse.

Smashing open the gates with his  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Masseter.png/32px-Masseter.png?8e624)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Masseter) [Masseter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Masseter), Grendel would lead the newly reinvigorated citizens, interrupting yet another of Karishh's feasts. The Executor would then flee to the far-off mountains of Riddha, pursued by Grendel. As the citizens sat feasting, Grendel would have a feast of his own, consuming Karishh in one bite.
