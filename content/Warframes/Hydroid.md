**Release Date:** April 10th, 2014

Wash ashore with oceanic momentum to turn the tides of war. Below unfathomable depths lurking in an abyssal trench, the Kraken awaits his call. Drench the surface dwellers in torrential downpour and deluge as the Maritime Marauder, **Hydroid**, sails across strange seas to pillage and plunder. Command elemental water to rend foes asunder, as horrid tendrils grasp flailing prey and relentless waves erode away the drowned.

**Hydroid** is a pirate [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that commands  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) water to control the battlefield. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") provides Corrosive status additional [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") reduction.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TempestBarrage130xWhite.png/32px-TempestBarrage130xWhite.png?94a53)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tempest_Barrage) [Tempest Barrage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tempest_Barrage) calls down a bombardment of Corrosive water.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TidalSurge130xWhite.png/32px-TidalSurge130xWhite.png?13465)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tidal_Surge) [Tidal Surge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tidal_Surge) transforms him into an [Invulnerable](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable") wave of water as he surges forward.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Plunder130xWhite.png/32px-Plunder130xWhite.png?1abf0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plunder) [Plunder](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plunder) has Hydroid seize Corrosive status to permanently degrade armor while strengthening his own armor and imbues his weapons with Corrosive.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TentacleSwarm130xWhite.png/32px-TentacleSwarm130xWhite.png?06d97)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tentacle_Swarm) [Tentacle Swarm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tentacle_Swarm) summons a deep sea creature that captures and suspends enemies.

## Abilities

### Passive
Enemies damaged by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HydroidIcon272.png/31px-HydroidIcon272.png?6eb95)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hydroid) [Hydroid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hydroid) are **permanently** more vulnerable to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) Status, with initial status reducing [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") by **50%** rather than 26%, allowing  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) Status to reach **100%** armor reduction at full stacks. As long as Hydroid has damaged the enemy, the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) Status Effects can be applied from any source, not just from Hydroid's weapons or abilities, and will receive the benefit.

### **[Tempest Barrage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tempest_Barrage)**
Summon a tempest to rain down upon a target area.
> [!note]
> Orbital bombardment in a selected area


### **[Tidal Surge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tidal_Surge)**
Crash through enemies in a ferocious wall of water.
### **[Plunder](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plunder)**
Plunder Armor from nearby enemies and increase Corrosive Damage on your Abilities and weapons. Enemies affected by Corrosion Status offer a greater increase.

### **[Tentacle Swarm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tentacle_Swarm)**
Summon a creature from the depths. Its watery tentacles emergy from nearby surfaces to wreak havoc.
> [!note]
> Summon tentacles in an area. They will grab enemies and slam them around
