**Excalibur Umbra** is the Umbra variant of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExcaliburIcon272.png/31px-ExcaliburIcon272.png?3bcf7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) [Excalibur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur), sporting higher [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") and [energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy"), as well as three Umbra polarities while losing a Vazarin polarity. Unique to Umbra is his display of sentience, allowing him to fight on his own when not piloted by the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator").

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExcaliburIcon272.png/31px-ExcaliburIcon272.png?3bcf7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) [Excalibur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) has improved mastery in the Tenno [art of the blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee "Melee"), receiving **+10%** [Attack Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Attack_Speed "Attack Speed") and **+10%** [damage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage "Damage") bonuses for melee attacks while wielding the following weapons:

- [Swords](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Sword "Category:Sword"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Skana.png/32px-Skana.png?67dfa)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Skana) [Skana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Skana).
- [Dual swords](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Dual_Swords "Category:Dual Swords"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DualSkana.png/32px-DualSkana.png?af2c5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Skana) [Dual Skana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Skana).
- [Nikanas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Nikana "Category:Nikana"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DragonNikana.png/32px-DragonNikana.png?20efa)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dragon_Nikana) [Dragon Nikana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dragon_Nikana).
- [Rapiers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Rapier "Category:Rapier"), such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Destreza.png/32px-Destreza.png?52b66)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Destreza) [Destreza](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Destreza).
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedBladeWeapon.png/32px-ExaltedBladeWeapon.png?654f4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade_\(Weapon\)) [Exalted Blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade_\(Weapon\))
- Bonuses are additive to mods such as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pressure_Point) [Pressure Point](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pressure_Point) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fury) [Fury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fury).

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Excalibur_UmbraIcon272.png/31px-Excalibur_UmbraIcon272.png?74cc1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Umbra) [Excalibur Umbra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Umbra) exhibits sentience, allowing him to remain active in combat even when a player uses [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") form, acting similarly to a [Specter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Specter_\(Tenno\) "Specter (Tenno)").

- Umbra can receive damage and healing in his specter form. Health and shields lost in his specter form will **not** be restored upon returning to Umbra unless all of Umbra's health is depleted.
- Once all of Umbra's health is depleted, he will stop moving or attacking.
    - The player does **not** die or lose a revive, however, if Umbra's health is depleted.
- Umbra can use any equipped weapon and most abilities, which will take into account any mods installed.
- When enemies come within **3** meters of Umbra, he will automatically switch to the equipped melee weapon.
    - If no melee weapon is equipped, Umbra will not attack enemies who approach him, but will still cast abilities.
- Umbra's weapons will still expend [ammo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ammo "Ammo") reserves normally. Umbra is capable of looting ammo pickups, and will actively search for them should his ammo reserves be depleted.
    - Depleting all ammo reserves displays a red ammunition icon above Umbra's head.
    - [Squad Ammo Restores](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Squad_Ammo_Restore "Squad Ammo Restore") will replenish ammo but **only** if the player is in range, even if Umbra himself is in range.
- If Umbra falls down a bottomless pit while in Specter mode, he will **not** be teleported back and will remain missing until players return to Warframe mode.

### **[Slash Dash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slash_Dash)**
Dash between enemies while slashing with the Exalted Blade.

### **[Radial Howl](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Howl)**
Let out a ferocious howl that stuns nearby enemies and causes Sentients to shed any built up resistances.

### **[Radial Javelin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Javelin)**
Launches javelins towards enemies, dealing high damage and impaling them to walls.

### **[Exalted Blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Blade)**
Summon a sword of pure light and immense power.
## Lore

>But you won't have to imagine. A lovingly cultured [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested") swarms within your blood. Your transformation has begun, reshaping you into a sacred surrogate of the unholy [[Tenno]]m/w/Warframe "Warframe") with but a Single. Burning. Memory. It is... a miracle! But all miracles require sacrifice.

—[[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]]

Excalibur Umbra was originally an [[Orokin]]") soldier who was punished by [[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]] for attempting to interfere with his betrayal. Similar to the description of the Warframe creation process previously recorded by Ballas in the [Vitruvian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vitruvian "Vitruvian"), he was shown to be exposed to the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested") to become an anti-[[Sentient]]e" that required the "sacrifice" of Umbra's son [Isaah](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Isaah "Isaah"), at his own hands, forced by Ballas through a unique Transference Bolt. This memory of filicide was permanently stored in Umbra to be replayed endlessly as punishment for spying on Ballas.

As a result of Umbra's torment, he retains most of his sentience and is capable of acting on his own without the [[Tenno]]'s control, even initially rejecting the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator")'s Transference and going rogue. Umbra also carries the memories of his final moments before transforming into a Warframe, which the Operator discovers to contain codes that unlock Ballas's Vitruvian recordings of the Sentient's betrayal, the creation of Warframes, and the Tenno.

Umbra is first seen in a courtyard on [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth") battling his creator and his allied Sentients, but due to Transference influence he is unable to disobey Ballas, leading him to be easily destroyed by [[Natah]]. The Operator discovers Umbra's remains on Earth, which leads them to a containment cell on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua") housing Ballas's Vitruvian, allowing the Operator to rebuild Umbra from the contained data. However, Umbra immediately goes rogue and flees across the solar system, but not before the Operator taps into his memories.

>--[We](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") had created [monsters](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") we couldn't control. We drugged them, tortured them, eviscerated them... We brutalized their minds... but it did not work. Until [they](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") came. And it was not their force of will - not their [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") devilry - not their alien darkness... It was something else. It was that somehow, from within the derelict-horror, they had learned a way to see inside an ugly, broken thing-- And take away its pain.

—[[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]]

After seeing all of Umbra's memories, the Operator reassures Umbra that they will fight Ballas together. In a strange and surreal sequence in his mind, Umbra calms down and finally accepts the Operator's Transference. Together they battle Ballas on Earth, whose attempts to subdue Umbra prove futile when the Operator's Transference overpowers Ballas's control, allowing Umbra to significantly wound his creator.
