Styanax never wonders how many enemies he will face, only where he can find them. The hoplite deals high damage. Enemies tremble before his spear and shield.

**Release Date:** September 7th, 2022

Embody the valiant pride of ancient champions past, brandishing shield and spear zealously honed by ageless strife. Relive the legendary mythos of **Styanax**, the indomitable peltast. Impale the enemy upon **Axios**, his unstoppable javelin thrown with Olympian might. Repel their advance and sunder their defenses, through a swing of his unbreakable shield, **Tharros**. Bellow true a call to arms and rally war-torn souls, our spirits and power thus renewed. Ascend, steadfast and heroic, to spearhead the wicked's passage onto the River Styx. Styanax and his army marched into Origin in [Update 32.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_32#Update_32.0 "Update 32") (2022-09-07).

**Styanax** is the hoplite [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that brandishes his shield and spear to deal damage. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") provides [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") while in possession of [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield"), which is strengthened for [Spearguns](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Speargun "Speargun").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AxiosJavelin130xWhite.png/32px-AxiosJavelin130xWhite.png?31321)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Axios_Javelin) [Axios Javelin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Axios_Javelin) throws a single javelin that, upon striking an enemy, imaples them to a surface while creating a vortex that pulls in other enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TharrosStrike130xWhite.png/32px-TharrosStrike130xWhite.png?31321)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tharros_Strike) [Tharros Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tharros_Strike) hurls a wall of shields that removes enemy Shields and [Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor"), while recovering [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RallyPoint130xWhite.png/32px-RallyPoint130xWhite.png?0ad21)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rally_Point) [Rally Point](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rally_Point) creates an uplifting resolve for allies that recovers [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") over time while restoring Shields on kill, but increases his [Threat Level](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Threat_Level "Threat Level").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FinalStand130xWhite.png/32px-FinalStand130xWhite.png?95f9f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Stand) [Final Stand](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Stand) rises into the air with an army of spectral warriors as they throw a barrage of javelins.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/StyanaxIcon272.png/31px-StyanaxIcon272.png?043ed)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Styanax) [Styanax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Styanax)'s weapon [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") bonus increases by **1%** per **40** [Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") points, based on his current total including Overshields. Passive bonus is doubled on [Speargun](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Speargun "Category:Speargun") primary [Weapons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Weapons "Weapons")

### **[Axios Javelin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Axios_Javelin)**
Throw an Axios Javelin. When the javelin impales an enemy against a wall, surrounding enemies are pulled into the area and suffer a burst of damage.

### **[Tharros Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tharros_Strike)**
Summon Tharros, the shield of Styanax. Swing Tharros to repel enemies and reduce their shields and armor. Styanax regenerates health for every enemy struck.

### **[Rally Point](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rally_Point)**
Draw enemy attention to Styanax. His resolve uplifts nearby allies, regenerating the squad’s energy over time. Styanax and his allies also regenerate shields for every kill assist they contribute.

### **[Final Stand](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Stand)**
Exude might and valor. Rise into the air and throw a barrage of Axios Javelins. The javelins deal damage to nearby enemies wherever they land. Direct hits to enemies deal greater damage.

## Lore

A surprisingly recent tale, this tale of Styanax takes place on [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"), located within a cult lead by a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) renegade known only as **'[The Philanthropist](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Philanthropist "The Philanthropist")**'.

Two war orphans, **Aria** and her older brother **Darro**, were saved by the Philanthropist from an  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) Corpus ship, taking them to live in his cult. They would both live within this paradise, until they would come of age and join the Philanthropist's Chosen for a ceremony he called '**Ascension Day**'. Between the siblings, Darro was chosen first, with Aria being chosen 5 years later.

During this time, Aria would then come into contact with Styanax through unclear means, presumably flattening a large field of crops into the shape of lotus which could only be seen from space. Whatever the reason, Styanax became Aria's unseen defender, and gifted her a rescue beacon.

When Aria's Ascension Day arrives, the Philanthropist brings her to his laboratory, revealing to be producing a hulking, monstrous Infested named **[Leviathan](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leviathan "Leviathan")**, built from the experimented bodies of the other Chosen, and was planning to use Aria as its driving force.

In defiance, Aria reveals her beacon and presses it, summoning Styanax. Faced with near defeat, the Philanthropist chose to pilot Leviathan himself, and implanted himself atop its head. As Infested flesh met Tenno shield, Aria saw the face of Darro among the Leviathan's mass. With a single desperate scream, Aria awakens her brother. Darro, now fully aware. He clamored to where the Philanthropist was piloting, and with a single remaining arm, held the tyrant by his throat.

Thanks to the distraction, Styanax  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RallyPoint130xWhite.png/32px-RallyPoint130xWhite.png?0ad21)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rally_Point) [Rallies](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rally_Point) the rest of the afflicted Chosen, causing them all to claw their way up the Philanthropist, drowning him in a tide of flesh. Styanax then strikes the final blow, annihilating the Leviathan with a rain of spears from his  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FinalStand130xWhite.png/32px-FinalStand130xWhite.png?95f9f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Stand) [Final Stand](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Final_Stand).

In the aftermath, Aria offers to return the beacon, but Styanax simply closes her hand and departs.
