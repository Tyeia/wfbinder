The diminutive temptress **Titania** and her entourage of bladed butterflies scatter their dust to disorient, enchant and beckon foes to their doom. She fluttered in on August 19th, 2016 with [Update: The Silver Grove](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_19#Update:_The_Silver_Grove "Update 19") (2016-08-19).

## Abilities

### Passive
Frolicking and fluttering about,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TitaniaIcon272.png/31px-TitaniaIcon272.png?c9409)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Titania) [Titania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Titania) passively possesses **+25%** [Bullet Jump](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bullet_Jump "Bullet Jump") and [Rolling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Rolling "Maneuvers") distance. Casting her abilities rejuvenates Titania and her allies within **15** meters, granting them the **Upsurge** buff which regenerates **4** [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") points per second for **20** seconds.

### **[Spellbind](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spellbind)**
Enemies fumble their weapons as they tumble into the air. Nearby allies become immune to Status Effects.
> [!note]
> Enemies float, but allies get status immunity

### **[Tribute](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tribute)**
Cycle through and extract one of the four Buffs when cast on an enemy. Thorns reduces incoming damage. Dust degrades enemy accuracy. Full Moon increases companion damage. Entangle slows enemies within range.

### **[Lantern](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lantern)**
Create a swarm of razorflies that transform an enemy into an irresistible floating beacon, attracting witless comrades and finally exploding.

### **[Razorwing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Razorwing)**
Shrink down and take flight, while razorflies attack nearby enemies.
> [!note]
> This ability can end up being the fastest thing in the game


## Lore

> Titania. My shamed creation. I felt her beating wings through my branches. I felt her mortal wounds, her weeping burns as she fell beneath my canopy. And so it was here, within this grove, that she died to save me. Me, the one who had hated her most.

—[Silvana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Silvana "Silvana")

[Silvana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Silvana "Silvana") was an [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin")-era researcher from [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth") who wished to save her dying planet. She was recruited by [Margulis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Margulis "Margulis") to assist in Transference research, but the dream in working with her childhood hero turned into a nightmare as [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") warped the project into creating Warframes for the sole purpose of destruction. Thus, Titania was born, a killer made by a healer.

Hateful at her own creation, Silvana defected and stowed away on Earth-bound cargo, having stolen Apothics and slapdash Somatics with the intention to revive her planet. Seeding a mighty forest that would become the Silver Grove, Silvana then used her Transference knowledge to successfully and permanently bond with the forest itself.

When the Orokin found out of her defection, a battalion of Dax soldiers was dispatched to bring ruin to her forest. As the trees set ablaze, Silvana sensed a familiar presence: an unseen pixie that proceeded to slay the soldiers, Titania. The Warframe had given her own life to protect the woman who hated her the most.

In the present day, [New Loka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/New_Loka "New Loka") anoints the Silver Grove as their shrine. Desiring to protect the forest from [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer"), New Loka's leader Amaryn hires the Tenno to craft the Apothic given to them by the forest itself to awaken its ancient defenses. The Apothic blueprints also contain voice imprints of Silvana which details her logs, which horrifies Amaryn as she learns that the Silver Grove is not a pure sanctuary but an "Orokin poison". While anointing the Apothic, Silvana's disembodied voice awakens and initially mistakes the Tenno for an intruder until she gains enough clarity and sees them as a protector like Titania was. Amaryn, realizing that New Loka shares Silvana's ideals in spite of the Grove's artificial nature, sees that her syndicate change their dogmatic beliefs and swears that Titania will fly again.
