## Abilities

### Passive
Where sacred light shines the song of life begins, as the shadow of time marches evermore toward deathly silence. As The Anointed, Jade possesses two passives:
- Access to **2** [Aura](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aura "Aura") mod slots in her [Arsenal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arsenal "Arsenal") loadouts to assist herself and her squadmates
- Enemies that enter  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Light%27sJudgment130xWhite.png/32px-Light%27sJudgment130xWhite.png?3a7e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Light%27s_Judgment) [Light's Judgment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Light%27s_Judgment) well and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GloryonHigh130xWhite.png/32px-GloryonHigh130xWhite.png?51d4e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Glory_on_High) [Glory on High](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Glory_on_High)'s primary fire at a **10%** chance afflict enemies with the **Judgements** debuff as an overhead icon visual, applying **50%** [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability") lasting for **10** seconds.

### **[Light's Judgment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Light%27s_Judgment)**
Create a well of light that heals allies and hurts enemies. Those who enter the well will be highlighted by Judgments.
> [!note]
> AoE damage area


### **[Symphony of Mercy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Symphony_of_Mercy)**
Cycle through three songs that strengthen allies. Power of The Seven increases Ability Strength. Deathbringer increases Weapon Damage. Spirit of Resilience increases Shield Effectiveness. Extend the duration of each song by killing enemies surrounded by Judgments.

### **[Ophanim Eyes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ophanim_Eyes)**
Jade summons an accusatory gaze that slows nearby enemies and dissolves their Shields and Armor. When the gaze falls upon allies, they can be revived from a distance.
> [!note]
> It is not kidding when it says gaze. the affected area is where you are looking

### **[Glory on High](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Glory_on_High)**
Soar with destructive power. Use alternate-fire to detonate Judgments, causing an explosion of Jade Light. Enemies inside the Light's Judgment radius empower the explosion.
> [!note]
> Take flight and shoot explosive projectiles


## Lore

### Forbidden Love
During the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [[Orokin]] Era, Jade was once human, engaged in a secret relationship with a Low Guardian named [Sorren](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stalker "Stalker"). Sorren's desire for a child, which was considered a violation of the Legems, worried him. Jade dismissed his worries in the belief that Sorren would always protect them.

Jade and Sorren were eventually caught by Executor [[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]]s punishment, Jade was forcibly transformed into a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes") while pregnant, where Ballas taunted her failure to birth the child. Her source of power, the [Jade Light](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Light "Jade Light"), resulted in the Orokin's need to contain her in a hibernation pod within the execution chamber on Lua. Her only moments of freedom being to carry out the Seven's executions. Believing her to be in a dreamless sleep within her pod, unbeknownst to them, Jade would instead dream of seeing her child's face, and the time they would have spent together.

Ballas would continue to taunt Jade, stating that Sorren was still alive, having been transformed into a Warframe just like herself. Sorren only blamed himself, and, seeking to repent, chose to loyally serve the Orokin in hope of seeing his wife again.

Jade was eventually given an [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"), and sent on an assignment to [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"). Horrified at what the Orokin did to the child, Jade lulled her Operator to sleep and fought of her own free will to spare the child, believing that the Orokin's reckoning would one day come.

When the Naga drums echoed the night of the Orokin Fall, Jade finally encountered Sorren, who had become the being known as [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]], but he did not recognize her. As the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [[Tenno]] began to massacre the Orokin at the Terminus, Jade fought her way to Sorren and flew him away, ultimately arriving at what would later become [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]]'s tomb on [Uranus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Uranus "Uranus"). Recalling the taunts of Ballas that her unborn child may stir if she laid still for a long time, Jade spent the remainder of her life channeling all energy into her pregnancy.
### Jade's Passing
In the present, Jade had fallen critically ill. The [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]] attempted to nurse her back to health as thanks for saving his life. Despite his attempts at stealing [Bioplasma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bioplasma "Bioplasma") from the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) and [Cortichrome](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cortichrome "Cortichrome") from the [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff|50)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [[Infested]], Jade's life continued to fade. [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]] urged Stalker to move past his hate, finally forcing him to turn to the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") for help. Via [Transference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Transference_\(Lore\) "Transference (Lore)"), the Operator discovered that Jade is not fighting an illness, but is instead gestating a child. Despite the Stalker's help, the Operator can only help Jade with her birth, and she ultimately dies soon after, dissolving into motes of green light while comforting Stalker one last time.

While attempting to heal Jade, Stalker would catch the attention of the Corpus Captain [Xeto](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xeto "Xeto"), who raids his lair soon after Jade dies. Stalker, now protecting his newborn son, fights past the invading Corpus to reach the surface. Upon reaching his ship, Xeto corners Stalker with a barrage of laser fire. However, upon hearing the cries of the child, Xeto orders her forces to stand down and allows the Stalker to escape.

After reminiscing his promise to protect his family, Stalker names the child either **Sirius** or **Orion**.

Finally, Hunhow then contacts the Tenno. Despite Stalker's hatred, he entrusts Jade's blueprint to them, believing it's what Jade would have wanted.
### Granum's Desecration
When Jade died, she disintegrated into motes of Jade Light which have now permeated the [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]]'s hideout. Sensing opportunity, Corpus Founder [Parvos Granum](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parvos_Granum "Parvos Granum") sends his Corpus to collect the motes to further his Jade [Eximus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eximus "Eximus") project, intent on using her power as a tool of his desire. However, Parvos's current Jade Eximus are unfinished, requiring Jade's  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VolatileMotes.png/32px-VolatileMotes.png?8b0ee)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Belly_of_the_Beast) [Volatile Motes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Belly_of_the_Beast) to perfect them. The Tenno fight to collect these motes first, consigning them to be burned in the sun so Jade can finally be at rest.

Despite his remaining hatred for the Tenno, the Stalker would provide them aid, and could be seen fighting alongside them.

The Tenno are ultimately victorious, with Parvos now attempting to harvest Jade's lesser  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VestigialMotes.png/32px-VestigialMotes.png?42b79)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vestigial_Motes) [Vestigial Motes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vestigial_Motes) to complete his project. However, due to their lack of sheer power compared the Volatile Motes, his progress is virtually halted.

### Memory Feathers
After completing the [Jade Shadows](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Shadows "Jade Shadows") quest, an assembly of seven of Jade's feathers will appear behind [[1 - Awakening/Important Characters/Teshin|Teshin]] in any [Relay](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Relay "Relay"), in front of some tombstones.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 01 -

BEDSIDE. LAIR. URANUS.

I will live, little one. We will live.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 02 -

CREVASSE. LUA.

The Executor smirked down at me. "What is a life but a mass of unfulfilled potential? Just like the life that would have grown within you. But you chose to spite Us. And for that sin, you will mother only ashes and oblivion. Jade."

I screamed in defiance in his face, but my voice was razors and steel.

Did you hear me, my beloved child? Did you feel my promise? Whatever they have done to us, I SHALL bring you to into the world. I WILL see your face shining down on me.

I will raise you to the skies over Lua as my mother lifted me and show you the first mother they desecrated, radiant in blue. When you cry, I will cry too.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 03 -

HIBERNATION POD. JADE LIGHT EXECUTION CHAMBER. LUA.

They keep me in what they imagine to be sleep in between the executions. I pass the time dreaming of us. The games we can still play.

I close my mask.

Mummy is gone!

I open my mask.

Here she is!

Over and over.

The Dax murmur behind their hands.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 04 -

RUINS. OROKIN MANSION. VENUS.

Your father blamed himself. As if love could ever be a crime! As if I had no say in the matter!

So now he repents. In his changed skin, he serves them, "like a devoted dog." Ballas gloats to me of how they dangle the promise of me - of us - over his head.

"He lives in hope of seeing you again, one day. I have promised him this. You should be proud."

He wants me to hate. I shall not. I own myself.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 05 -

BATTLEFIELD. THARSIS.

There is a child in my head. Not you, my love. Some battle-shattered thing, a ghost, a dream. She calls me by a name I do not choose.

What are you? I ask.

"Your Operator," the child says. "C'mon."

What did they DO to you?

"Let's go! We're being called up! Time to fight!"

My hand raises of its own volition. It is trembling. I hear the unvoiced tears in her thoughts.

"I can do this. I'm Tenno. I can do this."

I repeat, softly, firmly: What did they do to you?

I feel the child curl up, fetal. She whimpers.

"I... I want my mom."

With my mind, I embrace her. I sing her the old songs of Cathal-Rah and the riddle-verses of Lyrim of Haq. I tell her the winding, meandering story of Lankity Hopkin. I am a thing all clouds and feathers. I rock her to sleep.

And so I go to war, and none suspect. I am as much the warrior as I ever was. With bow and scythe, I fight for the people I despise against a foe that deserves their victory. Because I know the reckoning will come.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 06 -

WRECKAGE. PLUTO TERMINUS.

When the drums struck, I saw him. Changed, just as I was changed. But the medal was his. There is no other like it.

My Sorren. And he did not know me.

I took him. I flew. Through blood and storm and insanity, I flew.

#### - MEMORY FEATHER 07 -

URANUS. TOMB. THE SENTIENT.

He could not have known it, but in taunting me, Ballas gave me hope.

"There is plentiful strength within you - but only to keep you preserved as you are now. Your child, alas, will never grow. Even if you fed it with all the strength in your body! Perhaps if you lay lifeless for a year, the child would twitch a little. A century and its heart might begin to beat. A thousand years, and who knows? Perhaps you might feel a kick."

And so, I lie here. Stubborn as the day is long, as my father used to say.

I promised that you would live, little one. I will keep that promise.

With your father watching over us, I will give you the gift of myself.

And one day, these metal fingers of mine will dip in slippery paint and draw a smiley sun and a house and a dog, and all of us together, and I will hear you laugh.
