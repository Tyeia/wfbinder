**Release Date:** October 18th, 2023

By twilight crepuscule on Naberus Eve, when the damned revel in ritual while the bereaved beseech for seance, an autumn draught rattles these wretched bones. Unseal the haunted crypt of **Dagath** at your peril, lest her night mares plague the living. Awaken, oh mighty Hollow Rider, to bestride ye ghastly steeds. Ne'er do they dare forget the whirl of splintered spines, the vendetta of her untaken soul, and the vestige of **Rakhali's** spectral stampede. Dagath and her loyal steed rode into service in [Update 34.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_34#Update_34.0 "Update 34") (2023-10-18): Abyss of Dagath.

**Dagath**, the [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") who had lost her face, roams to reap the souls of the living. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") has a chance to strengthen the recovery of [Health Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") and [Energy ## Abilities

### Passive

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DagathIcon272.png/31px-DagathIcon272.png?4af62)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dagath) [Dagath](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dagath) feeds from the souls of the deceased, granting her a **35%** chance to activate **Abundant Abyss** when picking up [Health Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") or [Energy Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Orb "Energy Orb") to increase their effective yield by **+300%**. The passive has no cooldown and can activate on each orb collected.

- This applies to:
    - Health Orbs — 50 health becomes 200 health
    -  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Dispensary130xWhite.png/32px-Dispensary130xWhite.png?b3988)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary) [Dispensary](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary)'s Empowered Health Orbs — 100 health becomes 400 health
    - Small Energy Orbs — 25 energy becomes 100 energy
    - Large Energy Orbs — 50 energy becomes 200 energy
    - [Universal Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Universal_Orb "Universal Orb") — 25 health and 25 energy becomes 100 health and 100 energy
        - Note that when picking up a Universal Orb, Abundant Abyss performs two separate rolls; the first roll determines whether the health is increased, and the second roll determines whether the energy is increased. It is possible for one to increase and not the other.
- Does **not** affect  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equilibrium) [Equilibrium](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equilibrium) conversion.
- Effect is multiplicative with Energy/Health Orb Effectiveness increases from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/AmberArchonShard.png/32px-AmberArchonShard.png?5eaa1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard) [Amber Archon Shard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energizing_Shot) [Energizing Shot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energizing_Shot),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mending_Shot) [Mending Shot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mending_Shot), and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Restorative_Bond) [Restorative Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Restorative_Bond)
    - For example, with two normal Amber Archon Shards slotted for Energy Orb Effectiveness (+100%), a small Energy Orb will normally provide **50** energy, and Abundant Abyss's activation will increase the amount to **200** energy.
- When her passive activates, swirling Void and mist particle effects surround Dagath for a moment.

### **[Wyrd Scythes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wyrd_Scythes)**
Wyrd Scythes surround Dagath and seek out nearby enemies. Those struck are slowed and suffer Viral Damage with a guaranteed Status Effect. The scythes also spread Doom and extend its duration.

### **[Doom](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Doom)**
Condemn nearby enemies to their doom. A portion of the damage Dagath deals is revisited upon them by a Wyrd Scythe. They also suffer Viral damage.

### **[Grave Spirit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grave_Spirit)**
Supercharge Dagath's weapons with extra Critical Damage. The effects are doubled on Doomed enemies. Escape fatal blows by briefly assuming a spectral form.

### **[Rakhali's Cavalry](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rakhali%27s_Cavalry)**
Phantom Kaithes charge forth, inflicting Viral Damage upon all in their path. Their attack strips the defenses of Doomed enemies.
> [!note]
> Kaithe = Horse


## Lore
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DagathIcon272.png/31px-DagathIcon272.png?4af62)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dagath) Dagath's story is told in a [Naberus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Naberus "Naberus") tale narrated by [Grandmother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grandmother "Grandmother"). The story cutscene can be accessed in [Dagath's Hollow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Research/Dagath%27s_Hollow "Research/Dagath's Hollow") at the shrine located near her resting place.

The Warframe known as Dagath was originally a [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") cavalrywoman, who served an  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) couple named **Corphel** and **Irilia**, and was secretly their lover. The couple never used the cavalrywoman's original name, instead calling her by pet names, for names have great power. Among other gifts, the couple gave her a horse, on the one condition that she never named it, for she would love the horse more than the couple. Unable to bear the sadness of not giving it a name, the Dax named the horse after all, giving it the name **Rakhali**, meaning "**freshness-after-rain**". As the couple's fears came to pass, they arranged for the horse's 'accidental' death. However, the rider was accidentally mortally injured as well. As she coughed up blood, the couple pleaded with [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") to allow her to live forever. He complied, and after some time, returned the rider to the couple, now a Warframe of living steel, with a smiling face that would never fade.

However, the rider was not the same. Vacant of emotion, she would simply stand idly by, like a nonliving doll, staring at them with her perpetual smile. The couple quickly tired of their lover, and since she was no longer a Dax, their taboo relationship was abandoned. They made a deal with an unnamed master of industry, and took the rider to his facility. There, she was secured with restraints, and killed via a disintegration beam to the face, which burned a hole clean through her head.

However, the Warframe survived, and clambered out of the facility's trash heap. Still believing herself to be a Dax, the rider sought for her [Nikana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nikana "Nikana"), but only found the [whip-blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dorrclave "Dorrclave") of the facility's overseer. She would remember what her masters had done to her, and with a head full of despair and betrayal, the renegade summoned a ghostly Rakhali to her side. Before the following sunrise, she took revenge on Corphel and Irilia, leaving them as faceless as she was.

On a lonesome Naberus night, wandering without purpose, she later encountered **[Grandmother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grandmother "Grandmother")**, and as per Naberus tradition, demanded a gift. But the old woman had nothing to give but her name. So the Warframe rode away, now named **Dagath**, meaning "**the mirror that accuses**". And Grandmother was then only known as Grandmother.

With Dagath's name came her new purpose: she would ride throughout the land, her empty face acting as a mirror to show others their sins – and always ready to deliver the sentence thereafter.


> There once was a [Dax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dax "Dax") cavalarywoman, the lover of an [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") couple named Corphel and Irilia. What was her name? We do not know, for they never used it. They called her by many pet names, though, for an exotic pet she was to them. Little Sweetness. Tender Petal. Wild Rider. Had a mere Dax been known to be engaging in relations with her betters, she would have been executed on the spot. But the couple's power and influence were such that the affair would never be publicly addressed.
> 
> Still they feared to use her name, for names have power. Many gifts they gave her. Fine armor. [Rubedo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rubedo "Rubedo") rings. And a horse of her own, on the condition that she never, ever named it. Names were for people, not possessions. So long as it remained a mere nameless thing, she would never love it more than them. But the rider could not bear to deny her friend the simple kindness of a name. So she whispered into its ear: you are Rakhali, freshness-after-rain.
> 
> And just as the couple had feared, she came to love the horse so dearly. This was intolerable to them. The horse had to go. An accidental impalement was arranged. But fate played a cruel trick, and both rider and horse were impaled. Corphel and Irilia hastened to her side. Ignoring the dying horse, they fretted over their lover as she coughed blood. [Continuity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Continuity_\(Lore\) "Continuity (Lore)") was out of the question. In desperation, they turned to a certain... [expert](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas"), and pleaded with him to let her live forever. He returned the rider to them, transformed to a warrior of living steel. Her sweet and pretty face looked upon them with a smile that would never fade.
> 
> For all their talk of 'forever', they wearied of her immediately. Now that the relationship was no longer taboo, they abandoned it. Parties became awkward when the rider stood vacantly by, useless as an abandoned doll. She had to go, they agreed. So arrangements were made with a master of industry, and the rider - all unknowing - accompanied the couple to a facility. She lay down obediently when instructed, showed no emotion when the restraints clamped her limbs, and made not a sound when the full force of the dissolution beam blazed through her head and out of the back of her steel skull. The couple were satisfied. Their sentimental mistake was dead, and furthermore, the smiling doll's face was erased. Now they could erase it from memory.
> 
> I doubt I need to tell you what faceless thing clambered jerkily from the facility's waste disposal heap, in the still of a [Naberus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Naberus "Naberus") night. How she sought for her Dax [Nikana](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nikana "Nikana"), and found only the overseer's [whip-blade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dorrclave "Dorrclave"). Confused, vacant, she wandered. Why was she alone? Where was her loyal Rakhali? She reached deep into despair and memory, and summoned up a ghost-horse to run alongside her on the night winds. Now, at least, she was no longer alone. She groped for other memories, came up with hands overflowing with betrayal. When the morning sun rose on Corphel and Irilia, they were faceless too, and quite still.
> 
> After that, she wandered without purpose, and the legends of the hollow rider grew. And so we come to my part in her story. You must understand, dear, that it is terribly bad luck to be out on Naberus with no gift to give a reveler. And yet that was my plight as I stood, my old bones aching, before the dreadful figure on the road, many years ago. 'I have no face,' the figure before me seemed to say, in a whispering voice that spoke in my mind. 'I have no name. Only a need. And you come before me empty-handed this Naberus night?'
> 
> 'I do have one thing to give, if it pleases, you' I said to her. 'My name. When you ride away, you shall be Dagath. And I shall be only Grandmother.' And that is the story of how I lost my name, and kept my head, though many others have since lost theirs. For with her name came a new purpose. Dagath, you see, means 'the mirror that accuses'. They say that none now can look into the gulf where once her face was, without their own sins rising to accuse them, and a swift, sharp sentence following.
> 
> A happy Naberus to you all!

—[Grandmother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grandmother "Grandmother")
