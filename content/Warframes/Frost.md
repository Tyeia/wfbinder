## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

When Frost steps onto the battlefield, enemies experience a chill. Frost's attacks control crowds, protect objectives, and increase survivability. Wield the biting cold.

---

> This is Frost, colder and deadlier than space itself.
> 
> Frost can protect or punish, the decision is yours.

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:** January 29th, 2013

Seek solace and serenity in snowfall, as furious blizzards pummel the exposed upon permafrost. Tread across the frozen tundras of Origin's outer worlds, summoning forth glacial force and subzero extremes. Purify the battleground into a pristine expanse and enforce ceasefire through merciful cryo-sleep, by sculpting elemental ice itself as Father Winter, **Frost**.

**Frost** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that invokes the defensive power of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) while freezing enemies ice-solid. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases Cold [Status Duration](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Duration "Status Duration") and gains fortified [Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") from nearby enemies with Cold status.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Freeze130xWhite.png/32px-Freeze130xWhite.png?59db2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Freeze) [Freeze](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Freeze) releases a projectile that encases enemies in ice, while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IceWave130xWhite.png/32px-IceWave130xWhite.png?292e9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ice_Wave) [Ice Wave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ice_Wave) throws a ground trail of ice.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SnowGlobe130xWhite.png/32px-SnowGlobe130xWhite.png?b3c9b)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Snow_Globe) [Snow Globe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Snow_Globe) creates a protective dome while pushing freezing and enemies away on its formation and applying Cold status to enemies that wander inside.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Avalanche130xWhite.png/32px-Avalanche130xWhite.png?7a9d6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Avalanche) [Avalanche](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Avalanche) surrounds Frost with chilling winter to freeze enemies solid and removing their armor.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FrostIcon272.png/31px-FrostIcon272.png?6de31)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Frost) [Frost](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Frost)'s abilities have their  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) status effect duration increased by **100%**. Frost's **Fortifying Freeze** passive increases his [Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") by **50** per enemy afflicted by a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) status effect within [Affinity Range](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Affinity_Range "Affinity Range").

### **[Freeze](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Freeze)**
A frigid energy blast that freezes targets in their tracks.
> [!note]
> Its a snowball

### **[Ice Wave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ice_Wave)**
Sends a wave of razor sharp, crystallized ice toward an enemy, dealing heavy damage.
> [!note]
> 90% of Todoroki's Ice attacks

### **[Snow Globe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Snow_Globe)**
Frost deep freezes any vapor and moisture in the area, creating a protective sphere with brief invulnerability to boost its strength.
> [!note]
> Creates a big ice ball that blocks anything from outside from shooting in

### **[Avalanche](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Avalanche)**
Summons a treacherous landslide of ice that instantly freezes and shatters all enemies in its radius.
