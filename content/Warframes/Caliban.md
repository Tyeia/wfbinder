## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

The heir of twin kingdoms ascends. Biological and Sentient life meet in Caliban. An adept monarch, he offers crowd control and survivability. His creator had more than one world in mind.

---


> A twisted hybrid of Warframe and Sentient, he is raised from the wreckage of the Old War. This is Caliban, the survivor, the adaptive.
>
>Once a weapon consigned by Erra after the Old War, Caliban is yours to sow vengeance in the New.

—[[Natah]]

**Release Date:** December 15th, 2021

Amalgamated with our ancient nemesis, his vessel of Helminth morph suffused by adaptive bionics. Hybridize the powers of [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes") and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [[Sentient]]al prototype. Splinter forth his triad of familial fragments, to sweep death from Tau across Origin, through cyclonic devastation and destructive energy infusion. Caliban returned to wage war in [Update 31.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_31#Update_31.0 "Update 31") (2021-12-15): The New War.

As a Sentient-themed [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe"), **Caliban** boasts impressive adaptability. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") builds [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") from incoming attacks, and all his abilities apply  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgTauSmall64.png/32px-DmgTauSmall64.png?f7727)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Tau_Damage) [Tau](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Tau_Damage) Status for [Status Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Chance "Status Chance") Vulnerability.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RazorGyre130xWhite.png/37px-RazorGyre130xWhite.png?c68b8)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Razor_Gyre) [Razor Gyre](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Razor_Gyre) spins towards enemies to deal damage while recovering [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health"), [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield"), and [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy"), while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientWrath130xWhite.png/37px-SentientWrath130xWhite.png?dd27d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient_Wrath) [Sentient Wrath](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient_Wrath) has him stomp to release a wave of energy to apply aerial stasis and [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/LethalProgeny130xWhite.png/37px-LethalProgeny130xWhite.png?cb3cb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lethal_Progeny) [Lethal Progeny](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lethal_Progeny) summons three types of Sentients that provide shield restore and support: [Conculyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conculyst "Conculyst") melee fighters with high damage, [Ortholyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ortholyst "Ortholyst") ranged fighters that rapidly apply Tau status, and [Summulysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Summulyst "Summulyst") that spawn [Choralysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Choralyst "Choralyst") to distract three streams of energy to a single point while leaving behind a fallout field, removing enemy shields and [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor").

## Abilities

### Passive

Versatile and resilient,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CalibanIcon.png/31px-CalibanIcon.png?2dd39)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Caliban) [Caliban](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Caliban) and allies within his [Affinity Range](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Affinity_Range "Affinity Range") gain **Adaptive Armor** that builds damage resistance against the highest damage type received from enemy attacks, stacking **5%** per hit up to a cap of **50%**. If Caliban or his allies do not receive damage for **5** seconds, their Adaptive Armor loses **2%** per second until fully removed.

- Passive bonus value is displayed as a [Buff](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Buff "Buff") icon beside Caliban's hitpoint indicators.

- Damage over time from procs (such as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgSlashSmall64.png/32px-DmgSlashSmall64.png?bab47)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) [Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) or  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgToxinSmall64.png/32px-DmgToxinSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) [Toxin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) for example) will **not** contribute nor maintain damage resistance.
- Adaptive Armor does **not** stack with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Adaptation) [Adaptation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Adaptation). The damage reduction provided to Caliban (or his allies) equipped with Adaptation will be the higher of the two values.

### **[Razor Gyre](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Razor_Gyre)**

Dash forward in a spinning vortex of death. Struck enemies are inflicted with [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientSymbol.png/14px-SentientSymbol.png?07eef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:SentientSymbol.png)Tau Status Effect, making them more vulnerable to all other status effects. For each enemy hit, recover a portion of the energy cost, as well as health and shields. Enemies lifted by Sentient Wrath take increased damage.
> [!note]
> Spin. Any enemies you touch get hurt. You heal for hitting enemies

### **[Sentient Wrath](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient_Wrath)**

Smash the ground sending out a radial wave of destruction. Those not killed by the initial blast are helplessly raised into the air, where they take amplified damage for a short time.
### **[Lethal Progeny](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lethal_Progeny)**

Cycle through Sentient unit types, then summon them to Caliban's side. Conculysts focus on raw melee damage. Ortholysts specialize in ranged attacks and inflict [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientSymbol.png/14px-SentientSymbol.png?07eef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:SentientSymbol.png)Tau Status Effect. Summulysts summon their own Choralysts and provide shield protection for the squad.
### **[Fusion Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fusion_Strike)**
Converge three streams of raw energy upon a single point, causing a massive explosion. The streams strip armor and shields, and each enemy struck detonates on stream convergence. Summoned Conculysts can wield their own mini Fusion Strikes.
