**Release Date:** October 25th, 2012

Resuscitate the dying wounded and protect the weak, soon miraculously revitalized to their optimal state. Brave the crossfire to commence emergency triage, then regenerate and energize her patients as **Trinity**, the Combat Medic. Dispense her gifts aplenty at her foes' expense, and bestow the blessing of youthful vigor to escape the curse of mortality.

**Trinity** is a combat medic [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") who provides healing for her allies. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases herself and her ally's [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") based on her [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/WellOfLife130xWhite.png/32px-WellOfLife130xWhite.png?5b0d2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Well_of_Life) [Well of Life](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Well_of_Life) suspends an enemy in aerial stasis as allies near them receive healing and [Life Steal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Life_Steal "Life Steal"), [Status Effect](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") immunity, and [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death") prevention.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EnergyVampire130xWhite.png/32px-EnergyVampire130xWhite.png?90812)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Vampire) [Energy Vampire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Vampire) staggers an enemy to emit waves that restore energy which is released in full upon their death.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Link130xWhite.png/32px-Link130xWhite.png?bb364)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Link) [Link](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Link) tethers nearby enemies to Trinity to provide [Damage Redirection](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Redirection "Damage Redirection").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Blessing130xWhite.png/32px-Blessing130xWhite.png?5d91e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blessing) [Blessing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blessing) releases a healing wave that restores Health and [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") and provides [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction").

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TrinityIcon272.png/31px-TrinityIcon272.png?e9967)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Trinity) [Trinity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Trinity) and allies in her [Affinity Range](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Affinity_Range "Affinity Range") gain **Lifegiver**, which increases their [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") equal to **50%** (**25%** in [Conclave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conclave "Conclave")) of Trinity's total [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") pool.

### **[Well of Life](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Well_of_Life)**
Create a well of life on an enemy who will absorb Status Effect damage intended for nearby allies. Allies gain additional Health when they attack the target. If allies die, enemies in the well of life die in their stead.
> [!note]
> Enemy gets lifted up, anyone who attacks it gets life steal


### **[Energy Vampire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Vampire)**
Allies will gain energy over time when enemies are marked with Energy Vampire.
> [!note]
> Enemy pulses out energy.

### **[Link](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Link)**
Any damage taken while Link is active will be channeled to a nearby enemy.

### **[Blessing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blessing)**
Restore the health and shields of allies within Affinity Range while reducing the damage they take from enemies.
