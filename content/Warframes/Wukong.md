**Release Date:** November 25th, 2015

Descending from the empyrean eternal, the unruly primal saint **Wukong** wreaks havoc in the System with his immortal trickery and savage beatings. Divide and conquer with twice yourselves, then journey unseen under a cloak of clouds. Endure through any assault with arrogant poise, donning the legendary staff to tip the balance in his favor, and his opponents off their feet. Wukong embarked on a new journey in [Update 17.12](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_17#Update_17.12 "Update 17") (2015-11-25) and was joined by his twin in [Update 25.2](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_25#Update_25.2 "Update 25") (2019-06-19).

**Wukong** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with mythical powers that grant him damage and survivability. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") lets him cheat [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death") up to three times, with each prevention temporarily granting him increased elemental damage, [Invisible](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisible "Invisible"), [Invulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerability "Invulnerability"), extra resource drop chance, or increased [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") and [Energy Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Orb "Energy Orb") effectiveness.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CelestialTwin130xWhite.png/32px-CelestialTwin130xWhite.png?40cb1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Celestial_Twin) [Celestial Twin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Celestial_Twin) sheds a clone that fights alongside Wukong.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CloudWalker130xWhite.png/32px-CloudWalker130xWhite.png?ac7d1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cloud_Walker) [Cloud Walker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cloud_Walker) transforms into a mobile cloud that grants [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") restore, invisibility, and invulnerability, while  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Defy130xWhite.png/32px-Defy130xWhite.png?ac7d1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Defy) [Defy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Defy) engages an invulnerable stance that absorbs and reflects damage while granting increased [Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/PrimalFury130xWhite.png/32px-PrimalFury130xWhite.png?33c1c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primal_Fury) [Primal Fury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primal_Fury) summons the legendary  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IronStaff.png/32px-IronStaff.png?a65ee)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Iron_Staff) [Iron Staff](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Iron_Staff) melee.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/WukongIcon272.png/31px-WukongIcon272.png?531af)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wukong) [Wukong](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wukong) has learned five techniques to avoid [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death") whenever he takes fatal damage. Wukong will be randomly allowed **3** of these techniques per mission, each will make Wukong avoid entering bleedout upon taking fatal damage, [Invulnerable](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable") for **2** seconds, restore **50%** [health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health"), and provide a unique buff:
 - **Primal Forces:** **300%** elemental damage for **60** seconds.
 - **Heavenly Cloak:** [Invisibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisible "Invisible") for **30** seconds.
 - **Cosmic Armour:** Invulnerable for **30** seconds.
 - **Monkey Luck:** Enemies killed yield extra loot drops for **60** seconds.
 - **Sly Alchemy:** [Orbs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pickups#Orbs "Pickups") are **4x** more effective for **60** seconds.

### **[Celestial Twin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Celestial_Twin)**
Shedding part of himself, Wukong creates a twin to fight by his side. Attack at range and the twin will melee, pull a blade and the twin will lay down covering fire. Use again to command the twin to attack a target with increased damage.

### **[Cloud Walker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cloud_Walker)**
Evaporate into a cloud of mist and float through the battlefield, dazing any enemy encountered, while healing Wukong and his twin.

### **[Defy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Defy)**
Wukong and his twin become invulnerable and defy enemies to attack. All damage is captured, stored, and dealt back in a single furious strike of Wukong's staff. Bonus Armor is then granted relative to the damage captured.

### **[Primal Fury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primal_Fury)**
Summon the Iron Staff and unleash fury.
