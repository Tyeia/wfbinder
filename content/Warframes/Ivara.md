**Release Date:** December 3rd, 2015

Equipped with a diverse selection of arrows, deft hands, shroud of stealth and an exalted bow, the crafty **Ivara** infiltrates hostile territory with deception and diversion, and eliminates threats with a shot from beyond. Ivara emerged in [Update 18.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_18#Update_18.0 "Update 18") (2015-12-03).

**Ivara** is a hunter [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that utilizes stealth. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") extends the range of her enemy [Radar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radar "Radar").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Quiver130xWhite.png/32px-Quiver130xWhite.png?ab2a6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver) [Quiver](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver) cycles through four arrows: Cloak Arrow creates a bubble that provides [Invisibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisibility "Invisibility"), Dashwire Arrow forms a [Zipline](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zipline "Zipline"), Noise Arrow emits noise to attract enemies to it, and Sleep Arrow puts enemies to sleep.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Navigator130xWhite.png/32px-Navigator130xWhite.png?85052)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Navigator) [Navigator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Navigator) projects her consciousness into her projectiles to manually control and direct it.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Prowl130xWhite.png/32px-Prowl130xWhite.png?fe021)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prowl) [Prowl](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prowl) turns her invisible, increases her [headshot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts") damage, and allows her to automatically spawn loot from nearby enemies, at the cost of reduced [Movement Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed") and limited maneuvers that would otherwise break the cloak.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ArtemisBow130xWhite.png/32px-ArtemisBow130xWhite.png?a8bea)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow) [Artemis Bow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow) draws her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ArtemisBowWeapon.png/32px-ArtemisBowWeapon.png?a2243)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow_\(Weapon\)) [Artemis Bow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow_\(Weapon\)) to release fanning arrows.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IvaraIcon272.png/31px-IvaraIcon272.png?8c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ivara) [Ivara](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ivara)'s innate enemy [Radar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radar "Radar") has a larger radius, detecting enemies and displaying their position on the [minimap](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/HUD "HUD") within **50** meters rather than the usual 30 meters.

### **[Quiver](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver)**
Cycle through and shoot one of four tactical arrows: Cloak, Dashwire, Noise and Sleep. In the Conclave, use the Null-Shield and Slow Arrows.
### **[Navigator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Navigator)**
Assume control of a projectile and guide it to the target.
> [!note]
> The camera follows the projectile. This may be disorienting

### **[Prowl](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prowl)**
Become invisible and steal loot from unsuspecting enemies or take out prey with deadly headshots.
### **[Artemis Bow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow)**
Summon a mighty bow and unleash a volley of devastating arrows.
## Lore

Quite early in her history, Ivara would encounter a slayer of Warframes, a beast-thing known as the [Myrmidon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Myrmidon "Myrmidon"). Those that it killed were deemed unworthy to be remembered, and were erased from the records of history.

A Dax emergency call led Ivara and two unknown Warframes to a complex cave system. The discovered the dead bodies of the Dax who sent the message, along with the Myrmidon. The creature made short work of Ivara's companions, first by nullifying their abilities, and vaporizing them with [an emerald light](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Light "Jade Light"), converting them both into clouds of dust.

Ivara attempted to sneak away, but the Myrmidon rendered both her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Prowl130xWhite.png/32px-Prowl130xWhite.png?fe021)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prowl) [Prowl](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prowl) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Quiver130xWhite.png/32px-Quiver130xWhite.png?ab2a6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver) [Quiver](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver) useless. Ivara retreated and readied her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Aksomati.png/32px-Aksomati.png?d8360)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aksomati) [Aksomati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aksomati),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Rubico.png/32px-Rubico.png?b8a35)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rubico) [Rubico](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rubico), and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ArtemisBowWeapon.png/32px-ArtemisBowWeapon.png?a2243)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow_\(Weapon\)) [Artemis Bow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Artemis_Bow_\(Weapon\)). Guided by her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Navigator130xWhite.png/32px-Navigator130xWhite.png?85052)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Navigator) [Navigator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Navigator), she managed to outwit the creature. Stripping him of his armor, the Myrmidon lay naked in front of the huntress, but was revealed to have a face similar to an [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin"), a race Ivara was sworn to never kill. The Myrmidon knew it, and smirked at Ivara.

After the duel, Ivara brought the defeated Myrmidon to the Council of the Seven. For slaying her comrades, she expected him to meet justice at that hands of his own people, the people he mocked with his pallid visage. One of the Seven removed a small device, "a lattice of delicate silver" from their head and placed it on the podium between the Council and Ivara, causing the Myrmidon to instantly collapse lifeless to the floor. Executor [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") then told Ivara how her combat abilities and loyalty were tested, and because of her success, she will be remembered, while her defeated comrades would be long forgotten.
