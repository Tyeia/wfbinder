**Release Date:** November 27th, 2014

The gunslinger **Mesa** wields both style and power to overcome her foes in a rapid shower of ballistic ordnance. **Mesa** shot first in [Update 15.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_15#Update_15.5 "Update 15") (2014-11-27).

**Mesa** is a gunslinger [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with rapid damage. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") grants her increased [Fire Rate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fire_Rate "Fire Rate") when wielding [Dual Sidearms](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Sidearms "Dual Sidearms") or [Reload Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reload_Speed "Reload Speed") for [Single Sidearm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Single_Sidearm "Single Sidearm"), and increased [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") when not equipped with a [melee](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee "Melee") weapon.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/BallisticBattery130xWhite.png/32px-BallisticBattery130xWhite.png?f0d48)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballistic_Battery) [Ballistic Battery](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballistic_Battery) stores damage dealt to be then released on the next damage instance.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ShootingGallery130xWhite.png/32px-ShootingGallery130xWhite.png?b78b2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shooting_Gallery) [Shooting Gallery](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shooting_Gallery) creates a swirling lasso that enhances weapon damage while lashing nearby enemies to [jam](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Disarm#Jam "Disarm") their guns or stun melee fighters.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ShatterShield130xWhite.png/32px-ShatterShield130xWhite.png?16869)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shatter_Shield) [Shatter Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shatter_Shield) provides a barrier that grants [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") against gunfire, while reflecting hitscan shots or deflecting projectiles.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Peacemaker130xWhite.png/32px-Peacemaker130xWhite.png?4f295)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Peacemaker) [Peacemaker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Peacemaker) has Mesa draw her  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Regulators.png/32px-Regulators.png?49909)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Regulators) [Regulators](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Regulators), becoming stationary as she delivers rapid fire mayhem.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MesaIcon272.png/31px-MesaIcon272.png?23126)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mesa) [Mesa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mesa) has improved proficiency with secondary weapons, and gains a [health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") bonus at the expense of not having a melee weapon equipped:

### **[Ballistic Battery](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballistic_Battery)**
When activated, this power stores damage caused by guns. When triggered again, that damage is channeled through the next gunshot.

### **[Shooting Gallery](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shooting_Gallery)**
Gives an ally extra damage while jamming the guns of nearby enemies. This power shifts between team members.

### **[Shatter Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shatter_Shield)**
Envelops Mesa in a barrier of energy, reflecting back incoming bullet damage.

### **[Peacemaker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Peacemaker)**
With intense focus, Mesa draws her Regulator pistols, shooting down her foes in rapid succession.
> [!note]
> Shoots any and all enemies in front of you.
