## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Albrecht Entrati designed Qorvex to protect a Chosen Operator from the unique hazards of his lab. A Crucible Core gives Qorvex high survivability as he provides crowd control.

---

**Release Date:** December 13th, 2023

Crucible reactor overload, containment breach imminent. Erect industrial pylons of radioactive blight to cement our  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) presence in the nuclear warzone, then eradicate all hostile forces entrenched by collapsed concrete. Disintegrate the irradiated lifeforms via concentrated emission from the Brutalist Golem, **Qorvex**. Perfect this unstable machine of Master  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EntratiIcon.png/32px-EntratiIcon.png?cafef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati) [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati)'s design, in defense against unspeakable eldritch horrors festering from his labyrinthine laboratories below... Qorvex was reconstructed in [Update 35.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_35#Update_35.0 "Update 35") (2023-12-13): Whispers in the Walls.

**Qorvex** is a radioactive [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with immense [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") and debuffs enemies with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgRadiationSmall64.png/32px-DmgRadiationSmall64.png?91a72)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Radiation_Damage) [Radiation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Radiation_Damage). His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") grants increased [Punch Through](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Punch_Through "Punch Through"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ChyrinkaPillar130xWhite.png/32px-ChyrinkaPillar130xWhite.png?e7ed4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chyrinka_Pillar) [Chyrinka Pillar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chyrinka_Pillar) summons a pillar that pulses Radiation, and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ContainmentWall130xWhite.png/32px-ContainmentWall130xWhite.png?e368d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Containment_Wall) [Containment Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Containment_Wall) constructs a pair of walls in a line that are slammed together to group enemies and debuff them with [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DisometricGuard130xWhite.png/32px-DisometricGuard130xWhite.png?2588b)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Disometric_Guard) [Disometric Guard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Disometric_Guard) summons a ring of concrete plates that negate [Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") and can recover plates on killing enemies with Radiation status.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CrucibleBlast130xWhite.png/32px-CrucibleBlast130xWhite.png?b2e56)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Crucible_Blast) [Crucible Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Crucible_Blast) has Qorvex hunker down while releasing a deadly Radiation beam from his chest while causing enemies with Radiation status to explode.

## Abilities

### Passive
All primary, secondary, and selective melee weapons ([Glaives](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Glaive "Glaive") and [Gunblades](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gunblade "Gunblade")) wielded by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/QorvexIcon272.png/31px-QorvexIcon272.png?9d1dd)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Qorvex) [Qorvex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Qorvex) gain **Core Exposure**, granting them a **+3** [Punch Through](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Punch_Through "Punch Through") bonus. This also applies to some subsumed abilities' projectiles, including:

### **[Chyrinka Pillar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chyrinka_Pillar)**
Summon a Chyrinka Pillar that slows enemies. It pulses Radiation Damage with a guaranteed Status Effect.

### **[Containment Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Containment_Wall)**
Contain the threat. Qorvex summons walls that slam together, damaging all enemies caught between them and inflicting Radiation Status.

### **[Disometric Guard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Disometric_Guard)**
Guard yourself and nearby allies against Status Effects. Each time Qorvex kills or assists in killing an enemy affected by Radiation Status, the number of Status Effects Disometric Guard can prevent increases.

### **[Crucible Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Crucible_Blast)**
Release a beam from Qorvex's Crucible Core. Each enemy struck suffers Radiation Damage with a guaranteed Status Effect. Enemies affected by Radiation Status explode in a chain reaction.

## Lore

> Tenno, the Warframe Qorvex was designed by my Albrecht's own hand, to protect the chosen Operator from the hazards of his laboratory. This design is yours by right.

—[Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid "Loid")

Qorvex is a hulking mass of concrete arranged around a radioactive Crucible Core. Designed and created by [Albrecht Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati") himself, he serves as a bulwark against the terrifying and eldritch forces that now invade his labs. Qorvex now serves as a new guardian to the Chosen Operator.
