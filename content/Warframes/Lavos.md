**Release Date:** December 18, 2020

Concoct volatile brews of recombinant elements to dissolve, evaporate, and transmute hostile forces. Equalize the System through alchemical warfare as the eccentric scientist, **Lavos**, to dose his foes with serpentine venom and alter the state of combat conditions through his unorthodox experiments. Lavos's transmutation began in [Update 29.6](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_29#Update_29.6 "Update 29") (2020-12-18): Orphix Venom.

## Lore

Lavos served as a warden to the prison of **[Dabaoth-Kra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dabaoth-Kra?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dabaoth-Kra (page does not exist)")** on [Venus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venus "Venus"), and was assigned to one special prisoner, an [Archimedean](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archimedean "Archimedean") named **[Javi](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Javi "Javi")**, who possessed skills and knowledge in alchemy and transmutation. The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [[Orokin]] feared and hated transmutation, as they believed it would allow the lower caste to rise up against them. They even feared that Javi would somehow survive being vaporized by the [Jade Light](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Light "Jade Light"), and instead chose to imprison, hoping that his presumed evil would be contained.

During his imprisonment, Javi was reported to whisper to Lavos during his beatings, and would draw seemingly demonic symbols and snakes on the walls of his cell. Overtime, Javi's behavior became more bizzare, causing his sentence to be overturned. Instead, Javi was to be executed by cellular reversal, a process that would reduce him to biological pulp. After Lavos received the ceremonial garb to carry out the execution, he no longer beat the other prisoners. Due to Javi's teachings, Lavos realized that his writings were not of demonic origin but of evolution and healing.

When Execution Day came, Lavos dressed his prisoner accordingly, fitting a ceremonial hood on his head is honor of his Archimedean status. As Lavos turned to face the Orokin Warden, his  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Cedo.png/32px-Cedo.png?0aaf5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cedo) [Cedo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cedo) shotgun trembling in his hands, Javi whispered. In response, Lavos lowered his weapon and bowed before activating his power, liquidizing Javi's flesh and turning him into a soupy pulp. The other jailers cheered before realizing in horror that Lavos was not executing the alchemist, but transmuting the remains; Javi would be reborn as a serpent on Lavos's left arm, while a second serpent emerged from Lavos's own flesh as a symbol of change. The new student of alchemy proceeded to massacre the Orokin jailers with a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VialRush130xWhite.png/32px-VialRush130xWhite.png?58aef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vial_Rush) [Vial Rush](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vial_Rush).

Not one survived the fall of Dabaoth-Kra save for a floor-washer named **[Bekran Zaft](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bekran_Zaft "Bekran Zaft")**, who lived to tell the tale.
