Four beasts, one heart. Voruna's loyal wolf pack makes for a deadly combination of strength and stealth. Beware the monstrous howl that heralds fangs of vengeance.

**Release Date:** November 30th, 2022

Beware the telltale bloodcurdling howl when the primeval hunt begins anew. Nightfall engulfs the hallowed moonlit shrine, where sacred beasts of claw and fang prowl. Heed the wolven queen, **Voruna**, here she decrees her midnight feast. Reveling in feral carnage, her loyal lupine fiends, the silent **Dynar**, chaotic **Raksh**, ravenous **Lycath**, and ferocious **Ulfrun** ravage their prey once unleashed. Beneath lunar glow upon scarlet soil, tremble before her monstrous grin. Voruna and her wolves' hunt resumed in [Update 32.2](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_32#Update_32.2 "Update 32") (2022-11-30): Lua's Prey.

## Abilities

### Passive
Like a blood moon striding across the starlit night,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VorunaIcon272.png/31px-VorunaIcon272.png?f024e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna) [Voruna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna) never hunts alone. Safeguarded by her four loyal wolves, she may **freely** invoke their protective passives anytime during a mission.

Hold down the ability button (default 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 ) to invoke **1** passive into the UI indicator above the ability icons. Wolf passives persist indefinitely until switched, undergoing a cooldown (Ulfrun's Descent), or revoked by holding down the ability key again; while a passive is selected, the corresponding wolf skull on Voruna's body glows with energy, as a flying wolf skull orbits around her.

-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ShroudofDynarIcon.png/32px-ShroudofDynarIcon.png?8bef1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shroud_of_Dynar) [Shroud of Dynar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shroud_of_Dynar) increases [Parkour](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers "Maneuvers") velocity by **50%**.
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/FangsofRakshIcon.png/32px-FangsofRakshIcon.png?38d8a)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fangs_of_Raksh) [Fangs of Raksh](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fangs_of_Raksh) grants complete resistance to new [Status Effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect").
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Lycath%27sHuntIcon.png/32px-Lycath%27sHuntIcon.png?662b8)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lycath%27s_Hunt) [Lycath's Hunt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lycath%27s_Hunt) imbues Voruna's melee weapon with an additive **100%** [Heavy Attack Efficiency](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee#Heavy_Attack "Melee").
    - Since heavy attack efficiency is hard-capped to **90%**, the extra 10% is only visible and viable when equipped with a [Riven Mod](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riven_Mod "Riven Mod") that penalizes it.
-  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Ulfrun%27sDescentIcon.png/32px-Ulfrun%27sDescentIcon.png?57112)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ulfrun%27s_Descent) [Ulfrun's Descent](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ulfrun%27s_Descent) has Ulfrun die in Voruna's place when she receives lethal damage, preventing [bleedout](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bleedout "Bleedout"), rendering her invulnerable for **3** seconds and restoring **100%** of her [shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") and [health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health"). Ulfrun's passive undergoes a **60** second cooldown after his sacrifice or when swapped out.

### **[Shroud of Dynar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shroud_of_Dynar)**
(TAP) Dynar shrouds Voruna with invisibility and accelerates her speed. Invisibility ends when Voruna attacks. For a short time, melee attacks have increased Critical Damage and Status Chance and inflict Bleed. Melee kill an enemy during this time to extend the buff.

(HOLD) Dynar guards Voruna, increasing her Parkour Velocity.

### **[Fangs of Raksh](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fangs_of_Raksh)**
(TAP) Raksh's fangs tear into your enemies. 5 random Status Effects are applied at 10 Stacks each on an enemy. On target's death, spread the Status Effects to other nearby enemies.

(HOLD) Raksh guards Voruna with Status Effect resistance.

### **[Lycath's Hunt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lycath%27s_Hunt)**
(TAP) Lycath hunts to sustain the pack. There is a chance for enemies killed by melee attacks to drop health orbs and enemies killed by headshots to supply energy orbs. Increase the duration of Lycath's hunt by killing enemies affected by 5 or more Status Effects.

(HOLD) Lycath guards Voruna with increased Heavy Attack efficiency.

### **[Ulfrun's Descent](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ulfrun%27s_Descent)**
(TAP) Voruna drops to all fours and prepares 5 brutal charges that lock onto enemies. Ulfrun, the most powerful wolf, leads the attack as Voruna dashes toward her target. The pack deals increased damage to targets and nearby enemies that are inflicted by Status Effects. Lethal attacks double the damage of Voruna’s remaining charges.

(HOLD) Ulfrun guards Voruna. If Voruna falls during this time, Ulfrun dies in her place.

## Lore

### General
Voruna was once the warden and guardian of the **Circulus**, the holiest of the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin's](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) Yuvan temples on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua") where numerous rituals of [Continuity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Continuity_\(Lore\) "Continuity (Lore)") were held. Conducted by the [Yuvan Clerisy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvan_Clerisy "Yuvan Clerisy"), these perverse rituals allowed the Orokin to transfer their consciousness into new, younger bodies to preserve their lives.

Due to the unique nature of Void Conjuctions that would appear on Lua, Continuity rituals held here would have an increased success rate. However the Void would often manifest latent thoughts and emotions as violent manifestations, which is the purpose of Voruna's duty. She would stalk the Circulus's halls, slaying any wayward manifestations with her pack of wolves by her side.

#### Voruna's Pack

When performing her duty as the Circulus's defender, Voruna had a pack of four wolves who would accompany her when protecting the rituals.

- **Dynar**, the shadowed and stealthy. He provides Voruna with the vigilant gift of stealth that she may only take her enemies by surprise.
- **Raksh**, the invigorating defender. Voruna's own loyal guardian, he ferociously guards his mistress and other pack members.
- **Lycath**, the stalwart brother. With his hunt, he provides, becoming the soul of the pack.
- **Ulfrun**, the Fierce and fanged. The most formidable of the pack, his strength is legendary.
### Voruna's Continuity
During [The Collapse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Story_and_History#Collapse_era "Story and History") where the Orokin Empire fell and the Tenno had turned on their masters, Voruna and her pack pursued the head of the Yuvan Clerisy, [Executor Tuvul](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Executor_Tuvul "Executor Tuvul"), to the Circulus, who was attempting to perform Continuity on himself to escape his demise.

As Tuvul reached the temple, he brought forth a profaned tome that belonged to [Albrecht Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati"); the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Grimoire.png/32px-Grimoire.png?e8ce6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grimoire) [Grimoire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grimoire). Using the knowledge of the Void stored within, Tuvul started the ritual, attempting to transfer his mind into the body of a [child](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"); one who was stored in a peculiar cryopod. As the incantations started, a horde of manifestations were summoned, presumably drawn from Tuvul's vice.

Voruna and her pack tore into the abominable creatures, hacking and slashing them to pieces, but they were eventually overwhelmed; Raksh was the first to fall. Then Lycath, Dynar, and finally, the fiercest of them all, Ulfrun, was slain. As Tuvul's ritual neared completion, Voruna, howling with grief, took the severed heads of her fallen wolves and pushed him aside to hijack the Continuity, performing it on herself instead. Dousing herself and her pack-mates with the seething crimson, Voruna and her wolves were reborn, now fused together into a singular entity, who proceeded to mercilessly devour Tuvul. As Voruna left, she was last seen cradling the Operator in her arms.
