## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Beware the fury of the truly patient. Baruuk boasts survivability and crowd control. Pushed beyond restraint, he unleashes the storm within.

Doomed are the fools who attack the peace and exhaust the restraint of this reluctant warrior.

---


>This is Baruuk, the reluctant warrior.
>
>Following the path that winds between peace and hate, he maintains restraint before unleashing the storm within.

—[**Lotus**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus)

**Release Date:**  December 18th, 2018

Virtuously guided by his noble discipline, the pacifist monk **Baruuk** invokes sacred runes to evade conflict and bring blissful calm to endless strife. Reach beyond the brink of his patience, for foes to seek eternal rest within the storm unleashed.
## Abilities

### Passive

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/BaruukIcon272.png/31px-BaruukIcon272.png?73886)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Baruuk) [Baruuk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Baruuk)'s tolerance for violence wanes as his hand is forced by his foes. **Restraint** is a resource unique to Baruuk that provides up to **50%** [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") when the meter is completely depleted.

- Restraint meter is displayed in the lower-right on the HUD, above the ability icons.
- Restraint meter starts fully filled, and is depleted using Baruuk's abilities:
    - Each attack evaded through  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Elude130xWhite.png/32px-Elude130xWhite.png?3d8ce)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Elude) [Elude](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Elude) erodes **1.2%** from the meter.
    - Each enemy affected by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Lull130xWhite.png/32px-Lull130xWhite.png?9e1c6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lull) [Lull](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lull) erodes **0.8%** from the meter.
    - Each enemy disarmed by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DesolateHands130xWhite.png/32px-DesolateHands130xWhite.png?3d8ce)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desolate_Hands) [Desolate Hands](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desolate_Hands) erodes **1.6%** from the meter.
- Baruuk's fourth ability  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SereneStorm130xWhite.png/32px-SereneStorm130xWhite.png?f37a9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Serene_Storm) [Serene Storm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Serene_Storm) utilizes Restraint instead of [Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy "Energy") to cast and maintain. Using this ability refills Restraint meter by **4%** on activation and by **0.8%** per second until the ability is deactivated.
    - Restraint can still erode from using Baruuk's other abilities during this ability.

### **[Elude](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Elude)**

Dodge all incoming projectiles, but only while not attacking. Use again to deactivate this ability.
### **[Lull](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lull)**

A calming wave slows enemies until they fall into a slumber. Enemies woken by damage will be confused and disoriented. Short-term amnesia means all waking enemies forget anything that happened before the lull.
### **[Desolate Hands](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desolate_Hands)**

Summon a bevy of orbiting daggers to seek out enemy guns, destroying them with a small explosion. Combine with Elude to double the range.
### **[Serene Storm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Serene_Storm)**

With his Restraint eroded, Baruuk commands the Desert Wind to deliver powerful radial strikes with his fists and feet. Each moment commanding the storm restores his Restraint.
> [!note]
> Effectively, enter a martial arts stance to project your strikes further than your fists.
