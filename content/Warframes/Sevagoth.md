No longer adrift in the Void, Sevagoth has returned. He and his Shadow deal heavy damage and have high survivability. They are sustained by the reaping of wayward souls.

**Release Date:** April 13th, 2021

Lost in voyage adrift the Vast Untime, the dark helmsman **Sevagoth** harnesses inexorable mortality amidst the treacherous tempests of the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"). Return eternally from beyond the grave, as his **Shadow** eclipses the light, while harvesting wicked souls to sate his esurient darkness. Sevagoth was rediscovered in [Update 30.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_30 "Update 30") (2021-04-13).

**Sevagoth** is a phantom [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that commands his own shadow for damage and survival. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") has him retreat into a tombstone upon [death](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death"), emerging as his shadow to reap the souls of the living to revive himself.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Reap130xWhite.png/37px-Reap130xWhite.png?2bbe0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reap) [Reap](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reap) has his shadow fly outward to harass enemies with [Damage Vulnerability](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Vulnerability "Damage Vulnerability").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Sow130xWhite.png/37px-Sow130xWhite.png?9783c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sow) [Sow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sow) plants seeds of death that deal damage over time, which explode when hit by Reap.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Gloom130xWhite.png/37px-Gloom130xWhite.png?997cf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gloom) [Gloom](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gloom) summons a wave of darkness that inflicts  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/UnidentifiedItem.png/32px-UnidentifiedItem.png?6eed9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) [Slow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slow) while providing [Life Steal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Life_Steal "Life Steal").

All of Sevagoth's abilities build the Death Well to prepare  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExaltedShadow130xWhite.png/37px-ExaltedShadow130xWhite.png?3271c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Shadow) [Exalted Shadow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Shadow), granting control to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SevagothShadow-Arsenal.webp/48px-SevagothShadow-Arsenal.webp?c26ca)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sevagoth/Abilities) [Sevagoth's Shadow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sevagoth/Abilities) which is armed with its own abilities and the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ShadowClaws.png/32px-ShadowClaws.png?98400)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadow_Claws) [Shadow Claws](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadow_Claws), while control to Sevagoth is returned upon  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Reunite130xWhite.png/37px-Reunite130xWhite.png?fdd00)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reunite) [Reunite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reunite) or the Shadow's death.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Embrace130xWhite.png/37px-Embrace130xWhite.png?81d9e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Embrace) [Embrace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Embrace) pulls enemies toward the Shadow while inflicting  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Lifted_b.png/32px-Lifted_b.png?6fcdf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lifted) [Lifted](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lifted).  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Consume130xWhite.png/37px-Consume130xWhite.png?ce755)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Consume) [Consume](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Consume) has the Shadow dash forward, providing lifesteal when striking enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DeathsHarvest130xWhite.png/37px-DeathsHarvest130xWhite.png?bddad)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death%27s_Harvest) [Death's Harvest](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death%27s_Harvest) releases a harrowing wail to apply Damage Vulnerability.

## Abilities

### Passive
On fatal injury and entering [Bleedout](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bleedout "Bleedout"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SevagothIcon272.png/31px-SevagothIcon272.png?89397)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sevagoth) [Sevagoth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sevagoth)'s body petrifies into a broken tombstone as his Shadow manifests itself. Control the Shadow to collect the souls of **5** enemies, reviving Sevagoth upon a full harvest.

### **[Reap](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reap)**
Sevagoth’s Shadow flies outward ravaging enemies in his path. Survivors are damaged by Death’s Harvest over time. The souls of the dead fill the Death Well.

### **[Sow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sow)**
Plant a death seed in nearby targets to drain their lifeforce. Reap what has been sown to detonate afflicted enemies, dealing a percentage of their health as radial damage. The souls of the dead fill the Death Well.

### **[Gloom](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gloom)**
Summon a radial pulse wave that ensnares and slows enemies, siphoning their lifeforce for the Death Well. Allies within the wave steal health with each attack.

### **[Exalted Shadow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exalted_Shadow)**
When the Death Well fills to above 75%, [[Sevagoth’s Shadow]] form is ready to be released. Tear the enemy asunder with a collection of Melee-focused Abilities.

## Lore

> Sevagoth. A Warframe believed lost to the Void forever. His energy almost extinct. With his last spark of life, Sevagoth embraced his Shadow once again. And both were at peace.

—Cephalon Cy

Sevagoth was the captain of **[The Tempestarii](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tempestarii_\(Object\) "Tempestarii (Object)")**, an Old War [Railjack](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Railjack "Railjack") highly renown for its rescue services. However, one day Sevagoth was lost to the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") during a Void Storm, but commanded his Shadow to continue to pilot the Tempestarii to search for his body as the ship's final rescue mission, effectively becoming a ghost ship.

The Tempestarii made an enemy of [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") Captain [Vala Glarios](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vala_Glarios "Vala Glarios"), who was the sole survivor of the ship's attack on Lucretia Platform. Left adrift in space among the fragments of her dead crew, she sought revenge.

[Cephalon Cy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Cy "Cephalon Cy") and the Tenno come across the Tempestarii in the [Veil Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veil_Proxima "Veil Proxima"). After learning about the ship from Corpus logs in the [Pluto Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pluto_Proxima "Pluto Proxima"), Cy is able to appease the Tempestarii by pleading as a ship in distress. The Shadow entrusts the Tenno a corrupted navigation log, which they repair, but are ambushed by Vala. The Tempestarii counterattacks and banishes Vala to the [Granum Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Granum_Void "Granum Void") before making a jump to the Void to complete the ship's final rescue mission.

The Tenno retrieve a lifepod containing Sevagoth's corpse, reuniting him with his Shadow. Now at peace, the Tenno delivers him a ship burial by launching his body back into the Void.
