**Release Date:** July 18th, 2014

Mysterious and mischievous, **Mirage** manipulates the spectrum of light to dazzle her foes. With deceitful mirror images, colorful photokinetic displays and explosive pranks up her sleeves, every battlefield becomes the stage for her next performance. **Mirage** was unveiled in [Update 14.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_14#Update_14.0 "Update 14") (2014-07-18).

**Mirage** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with manipulative arts to deal damage. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases [Slide](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slide "Slide") duration and all other [Maneuvers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers "Maneuvers") are executed faster.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HallOfMirrors130xWhite.png/32px-HallOfMirrors130xWhite.png?1de08)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hall_of_Mirrors) [Hall of Mirrors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hall_of_Mirrors) creates holographic doppelgangers that distract enemies while dealing a portion of Mirage's damage.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SleightOfHand130xWhite.png/32px-SleightOfHand130xWhite.png?d814a)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sleight_of_Hand) [Sleight of Hand](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sleight_of_Hand) booby traps various objects while creating a jewel that charms enemies into approaching it before exploding.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Eclipse130xWhite.png/32px-Eclipse130xWhite.png?1fbf5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eclipse) [Eclipse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eclipse) wreathes her in darkness for [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") or in light for weapon damage.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Prism130xWhite.png/32px-Prism130xWhite.png?d4cdf)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prism) [Prism](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prism) throws a prism of light beams before exploding to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RadialBlind130xDark.png/32px-RadialBlind130xDark.png?2817c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blind) [Blind](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blind) enemies.

## Abilities

### Passive
 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MirageIcon272.png/31px-MirageIcon272.png?08564)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mirage) [Mirage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mirage) has an increased proficiency when performing several [Maneuvers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuver "Maneuver"):

- Sliding lasts **85%** longer
- **+50%** faster Maneuver speed

### **[Hall of Mirrors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hall_of_Mirrors)**
Mirage creates an entourage of doppelgangers to confuse and distract the enemy.
> [!note]
> Shadowclones that also shoot your gun


### **[Sleight of Hand](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sleight_of_Hand)**
Booby trap nearby objects while conjuring an irresistible jewel that bursts with radial blind when touched in darkness, or a radial explosion in light. Conjure multiple smaller jewels with the help of Hall of Mirrors.

### **[Eclipse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eclipse)**
(TAP) Temporarily reduce the damage Mirage takes from enemies.

(HOLD) Temporarily increase Mirage's weapon damage.

### **[Prism](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Prism)**
Fires an energy prism that shoots lasers in all directions. Activating again detonates the prism, blinding nearby foes.
