## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Move like a shadow, strike with force. The Orokin created a Warframe that marries damage and stealth to eliminate sensitive problems, but he disappeared in the chaos of their demise. All that remains is a batch name: Cyte-09.

---

**Release Date:** December 13, 2024

Locked and loaded with his bullseye on the prize, nothing evades his crosshairs. A flash of gunfire before quickly repositioning, empowering his weapons to quickly eliminate his next targets. Whatever his original name was is lost to history, but he goes by a new designation: **Cyte-09**. Proximity alert at the rendezvous... impromptu target practice, right on cue. Cyte-09 set his sights on [Update 38.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_38#Update_38.0 "Update 38") (2024-12-13): 1999.

**Cyte-09** is a marksman [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that marries long-ranged attacks and stealth. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") grants increased [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") on [Weak Point](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts") kills.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SeekIcon.png/32px-SeekIcon.png?05c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seek) [Seek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seek) plants an antennae that highlight enemies through walls while increasing their vulnerability to weak point damage and increases Cyte-09's weapon [Punch Through](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Punch_Through "Punch Through"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ResupplyIcon.png/32px-ResupplyIcon.png?1a0c3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resupply) [Resupply](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resupply) deploys ammo packs that increases elemental damage of a selected type, and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EvadeIcon.png/32px-EvadeIcon.png?b7244)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Evade) [Evade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Evade) renders Cyte-09 [Invisible](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisible "Invisible") which can be extended with weak point kills.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NeutralizeIcon.png/32px-NeutralizeIcon.png?e8556)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutralize) [Neutralize](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutralize) summons the exalted sniper rifle  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Neutralizer.png/32px-Neutralizer.png?e3ee2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutralizer) [Neutralizer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutralizer), whose weak point hits ricochet to adjacent enemies and has an [Alternate Fire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alternate_Fire "Alternate Fire") that launches a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) grenade.

## Abilities

### Passive

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Cyte-09Icon272.png/31px-Cyte-09Icon272.png?1a06f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cyte-09) [Cyte-09](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cyte-09) gains **Practiced Aim** when he kills an enemy by damaging their [Weak Points](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts"), increasing his [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") against weak points by **1%** up to a maximum of **300%**, lasting for the entirety of a mission.

- Critical chance bonus is additive to similar mods such as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Mod_TT_20px.png?dec74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Point_Strike) [Point Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Point_Strike), with some exceptions depending on the weapon. See [Multiplicative Crit (Mechanic)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Multiplicative_Crit_\(Mechanic\) "Multiplicative Crit (Mechanic)") for more information.
- Additional critical chance from Cyte-09's passive can raise the [Critical Tier](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit#Crit_Tiers "Critical Hit") of his equipped weapon.

### **[Seek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Seek)**
Plant an antenna that projects a forward wave scan. Detected enemies take increased Weak Point Damage, and become visible through walls. Weapons gain Punch through.

### **[Resupply](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resupply)**
Throw two Elemental Ammo Packs that instantly refill the active weapon's magazine, while granting the weapon an additional instance of the selected Elemental Damage and Status Effect. Reload clears the effect. Sniper Rifles gain extra damage.

Hold the ability to select the Elemental Damage type.

### **[Evade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Evade)**
Jump backwards and become invisible for a short duration. Killing enemies on their Weak Points extends the duration and heals Cyte-09.

### **[Neutralize](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutralize)**

Summon the Neutralizer, Cyte-09's exalted Sniper Rifle. Bullets ricochet off Weak Points to seek out other nearby Weak Points. Alt fire lobs a [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ColdSymbol.png/14px-ColdSymbol.png?6e6d1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:ColdSymbol.png)Cold grenade that completely freezes enemies.

## Lore

>I can offer one service, at least. I discovered a clandestine blueprint among my Albrecht's personal effects, for a Warframe so secret it was never named. It is yours.

—[Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid_\(Original\) "Loid (Original)")
Crafted by the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [[Orokin]] to carry out highly sensitive missions, this [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes") was never given an actual name, only addressed by its batch name: Cyte-09.

After the fall of the Orokin, Cyte-09 would disappear during the ensuing chaos.
