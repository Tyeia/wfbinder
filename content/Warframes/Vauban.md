**Release Date:** May 17th, 2013

Ingenious contraptions contrived with an innovative mind. Assert control over the battlefield as the tactician **Vauban**, executing his combat schematics with lethal precision. Mobilize his armada of electrocuting drones to patrol wherever he treads. Besiege the frontlines with his array of minefields designed for suppression and support. Devastate enemy forces with deadly disintegration from orbit, as a merciful release from his technological prison and fortress combined. Vauban deployed in [Update 7.11](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_7#Update_7.11 "Update 7") (2013-05-17) and reinvented his arsenal in [Update 26.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_26#Update_26.0 "Update 26") (2019-10-31): The Old Blood.

**Vauban** is an engineering [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with numerous tools for crowd control. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") increases his damage to enemies afflicted by his crowd controlling abilities.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TeslaNervos130xWhite.png/32px-TeslaNervos130xWhite.png?c8aca)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tesla_Nervos) [Tesla Nervos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tesla_Nervos) summons small rollers that latch onto enemies and stun them with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage).  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Minelayer130xWhite.png/32px-Minelayer130xWhite.png?c8aca)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Minelayer) [Minelayer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Minelayer) cycles through four deployable mines: Tether Coil extends wires to pull in enemies, Flechette Orb releases high velocity nails in a small area, Vector Pad forcefully moves all entities into a specified direction, and Overdriver attaches to allies to strengthen weapon damage.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/PhotonStrike130xWhite.png/32px-PhotonStrike130xWhite.png?16b88)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Photon_Strike) [Photon Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Photon_Strike) launches a beacon to deploy an orbital cannon.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Bastille130xWhite.png/32px-Bastille130xWhite.png?02f9f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bastille) [Bastille](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bastille) releases a containment field places enemies in aerial stasis while removing their [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") and strengthening the armor of Vauban and his allies, which collapses into Vortex to pull in enemies into is center.

## Abilities

### Passive
An inventor by nature, the process of creation necessitates demolition and deconstruction.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VaubanIcon272.png/31px-VaubanIcon272.png?8d020)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vauban) [Vauban](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vauban) inflicts **25%** extra damage with his weapons and abilities against enemies that are incapacitated by an attached  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TeslaNervos130xWhite.png/32px-TeslaNervos130xWhite.png?c8aca)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tesla_Nervos) [Tesla Nervos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tesla_Nervos), entangled by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Minelayer130xWhite.png/32px-Minelayer130xWhite.png?c8aca)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Minelayer) [Minelayer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Minelayer)'s Tether Coil, lifted by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Bastille130xWhite.png/32px-Bastille130xWhite.png?02f9f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bastille) [Bastille](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bastille)'s prison, or pulled by Bastille's vortex gravity.

### **[Tesla Nervos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tesla_Nervos)**
Deploy a roller drone that attaches itself to enemies and delivers bursts of arcing electricity to anything in the immediate area.

### **[Minelayer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Minelayer)**
Cycle through four deployable mines: Tether Coil, Flechette Orb, Vector Pad and Overdriver.
> [!note]
> Tether grabs enemies, Vector pad sends enemies and allies in the direction of the pad, overdriver increases damage, and Flechette shoots nails


### **[Photon Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Photon_Strike)**
Drop a targeting beacon that calls in a devastating laser artillery strike.

### **[Bastille](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bastille)**
Erect a containment field to capture enemies and suspended them in stasis, stripping their armor. Hold to collapse all Bastilles into a single damaging vortex.
