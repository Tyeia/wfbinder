## Overview

#### [**CODEX**](https://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Codex)

---

Macabre spectral power has risen. Infused with the essence of the Eidolon, Revenant has strong survivability and deals high damage. Enemies become his thralls.

**Release Date:** August 24th, 2018

Ravaged by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) energy, wreathed with eldritch [Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon") essence, the blighted sovereign **Revenant** subjugates foes through his affliction. Bestow his sacrificial legion haunted dreams, reap unseen, and spill their lifeblood upon the desolate scorched earth. Revenant was resurrected in [Update 23.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_23#Update_23.5 "Update 23") (2018-08-24).

The Eidolon essences bestows the **Revenant** [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with vampiric powers. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") releases a radial  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgBlastSmall64.png/32px-DmgBlastSmall64.png?7a683)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Blast_Damage) [Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Blast_Damage) upon losing all [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Enthrall130xWhite.png/32px-Enthrall130xWhite.png?dd3a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enthrall) [Enthrall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enthrall) converts enemies into thralls that fight alongside Revenant; upon their death they release damaging pillars that spawns projectiles to create additional thralls.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/MesmerSkin130xWhite.png/32px-MesmerSkin130xWhite.png?dd3a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mesmer_Skin) [Mesmer Skin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mesmer_Skin) renders him [Invulnerable](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable"), gaining a number of charges that stuns attackers.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Reave130xWhite.png/32px-Reave130xWhite.png?dd3a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reave) [Reave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reave) transforms him into a wall of energy that leeches enemy Shields and [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DanseMacabre130xWhite.png/32px-DanseMacabre130xWhite.png?dd3a4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Danse_Macabre) [Danse Macabre](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Danse_Macabre) has him release beams of energy while rotating in the dance of death.

## Abilities

### Passive
When  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RevenantIcon272.png/31px-RevenantIcon272.png?186c5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Revenant) [Revenant](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Revenant)'s [Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") is fully depleted, he emits a radial pulse over **7.5** meters inflicting **100**  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgBlastSmall64.png/32px-DmgBlastSmall64.png?7a683)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Blast_Damage) [Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Blast_Damage) damage with maximum **75%** [Damage Falloff](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Falloff "Damage Falloff").

Additionally, harnessing the essence of [Eidolons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RevenantIcon272.png/31px-RevenantIcon272.png?186c5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Revenant) [Revenant](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Revenant) is immune to the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgMagneticSmall64.png/32px-DmgMagneticSmall64.png?80be3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) [Magnetic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) night waters of the [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon").

### **[Enthrall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enthrall)**
Convert a target into a zealous thrall. Thralls turn on their allies and enthrall through damage. On death, they disintegrate into a damaging pillar of energy. The thrall horde remains under Revenant’s spell until this Ability runs out.

### **[Mesmer Skin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mesmer_Skin)**
Become enveloped in Sentient energy, which redirects damage and stuns all those who dare attack. Stunned enemies can be Enthralled at no energy cost

### **[Reave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reave)**
Dash through enemies as a wall of Sentient energy, leeching shields and health from any encountered, enhanced for thralls.

### **[Danse Macabre](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Danse_Macabre)**
Erupt with a multitude of Eidolon energy beams and sweep a circle of death around Revenant. The beams will modify their Damage Type to target select defenses, while incoming damage is redirected back into the beams. Hold fire to boost Status Effects and Damage, at the cost of increased energy consumption. Thralls killed by this leave overshield pickups.

## Lore

> Why do I remember this? The land here, killing hot, full of strangeness. This is where [Gara](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara "Gara") vanquished the [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient")! Where its essence fell into the ground, trapped in some unworldly between. It wanted out. At moon rise its ghostly tendrils would emerge, seeking form, seeking... return... And this... this was the warden... a Warframe. I can see it, like a dream repeating... The tendril emerging night after night, and the warden, night after night, cutting it down... until the warden himself was lost. How?

—[Nakak](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nakak "Nakak")

Following  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GaraIcon272.png/31px-GaraIcon272.png?3956f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara) [Gara](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara)'s sacrifice over the [Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon"), at the very site of her decisive battle which the [Ostrons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron "Ostron") now call Gara Toht Lake, a Warframe known solely as the **Warden** would arrive to drive away the [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") as it emerged night after night. This trial would continue until one fateful evening, where the Warden foolishly tried to make contact with the Sentient and was overwhelmed. Discovering that the Sentient was using him as a means to reform itself, the Warden cast himself into the lake to seal it. The time spent as an "anchor" to the Sentient would infuse the Warden with its eldritch energies, becoming "the **Lost One**".

In the present day, [Nakak](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nakak "Nakak") receives a strange mask, the Mask of the Lost One, from [Onkko](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Onkko "Onkko"). She sells it to the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno"), instructing them to wear it in the [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon") at nighttime. There, they see a [large spectral silhouette of an Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ropalolyst "Ropalolyst"); meanwhile, Nakak receives a [vision](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fragments/Revenant "Fragments/Revenant") from the [Unum](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Unum "Unum"), explaining the story of the Warden. After completing the story, the Warden, now transformed into the Revenant that serves as a "mask" for the Eidolon, emerges but is driven back, leaving behind its own blueprint. Having rebuilt the Lost One, Nakak claims the Warframe that had been infused with the powers of the Eidolon will be needed to combat a new threat imposed by the Sentient.
