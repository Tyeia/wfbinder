**Release Date:** October 15, 2025

Below the snowbound crust of [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis"), thrives a vast subterranean undergrowth. Dwellers of the [Deepmines](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deepmines "Deepmines") slumber upon the fungal bedrock, where impending trouble awakens an ancient youth. Nurture this mycelial paradise where wild roots and tiny Sprodlings roam free, then frolic amongst the fungus as **Nokko**, the Sporespreader. Spring forth as a shrunken munchkin, regrow and rebound, until death blooms for foes who threaten our sporadic peace. Nokko sprouted in [Update 40.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_40#Update_40.0 "Update 40") (2025-10-15): The Vallis Undermind.

## Abilities

### Passive
Upon receiving fatal damage, instead of entering [Bleedout](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bleedout "Bleedout"), Nokko enters **Vital Decay** as he reverts to his Sprodling form and will have **10** seconds to reach the location of a previously placed  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Stinkbrain.png/32px-Stinkbrain.png?15532)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stinkbrain) [Stinkbrain](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stinkbrain) or  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Brightbonnet.png/32px-Brightbonnet.png?fac9d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brightbonnet) [Brightbonnet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brightbonnet) to revive himself, recovering **100%** [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") and [Shields](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield") and becoming [Invulnerable](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable") for **5** seconds. While in Sprodling form, Nokko will also release [Pickups](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pickup "Pickup") that increase [Movement Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed"). If a mushroom is not reached in time, the Sprodling will fall into slumber, and Nokko will enter bleedout as per normal.

### **[Stinkbrain](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stinkbrain)**
Throw a mushroom that periodically sheds poisonous spores, inflicting [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ViralSymbol.png/14px-ViralSymbol.png?bdf42)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:ViralSymbol.png) Viral Damage and Status Effect upon nearby enemies while also lulling them to sleep.

### **[Brightbonnet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brightbonnet)**
Release a rejuvenating mushroom that emits a radial pulse, giving Nokko and his allies’ Energy and applying a buff to Ability Strength that lasts for a short time.

### **[Reroot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reroot)**
Revert to Sprodling form, spawning orbs that heal Nokko and grant a speed boost. While in this state, Nokko is untargetable and heals gradually.

### **[Sporespring](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sporespring)**
Unleash a chaotic ballistic mushroom that seeks enemies and bounces explosively off whatever it touches. Contact with any of Nokko’s mushrooms invigorates them, doubling their pulse rate.

## Lore

> No corporate. No co. Nokko. I'm gonna call you Nokko.

—[Nightcap](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nightcap "Nightcap")

Delivering  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Fergolyte.png/32px-Fergolyte.png?fcf51)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fergolyte) [Fergolyte](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fergolyte) to The Prince will cause it to grow, which unlocks a vision that reveals [Nightcap](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nightcap "Nightcap")'s history with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Nokko.png/32px-Nokko.png?34082)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nokko) Nokko. In this vision, players control a Sprodling guided by the voices and images of characters and growing mushrooms stalks.

20 Fergolyte unlocks the Purple Stem Path, 40 for the Blue Stem Path, and 60 for the Green Stem Path, for a total of 120 Fergolyte.

### Purple Stem Path

Nightcap is introduced willingly working for [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo"), clearing out the native fungi that grow beneath the Vallis surface to make room for  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) expansion. Nightcap and his fellow Solaris are led to believe that the fungi are an  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) experiment gone awry. After some time, they discover a "kid" deep in the mines who regularly sabotages their equipment.

### Blue Stem Path

Despite his antics, Nightcap grows attached to the "kid" and names him Nokko (short for "**No** **Co**rporate", or "No Co"). Nightcap learns that the Vallis fungi feed off waste products generated by Venus's [cooling towers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Weeping_Towers "Weeping Towers"): without them, Venus would quickly revert to its normal, scorching climate. Despite Nef Anyo's demands, Nightcap shuts down the elevator leading to the Deepmines.

### Green Stem Path

Nightcap discovers that Nokko _is_ part of the Vallis fungi, defending his home and by extension, the Vallis above. Disappointed by Nightcap's resistance, Nef Anyo sends Corpus troops to breach the elevator. Nightcap responds by blowing up the mineshaft, denying any access going to and from the Deepmines. Cut off from resources, Nightcap's condition worsens while Nokko chooses to remain by his side. Nightcap is last seen laying on a bed of fungi, bidding Nokko farewell.

Dialogue from Nightcap stating he is physically unable to leave and Nef Anyo being unable to find Nightcap's name in debt ledgers implies that Nightcap did die and was revived by Nokko to continue defending the Deepmines. However, Nightcap himself denies this, warning the Tenno that The Prince can play tricks with their mind.
