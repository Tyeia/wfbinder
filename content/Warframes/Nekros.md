**Release Date:** September 13th, 2013

With powers likened to that of necromancers and dark arts practitioners, **Nekros** exploits the vulnerabilities of the living and the corpses of the dead. Nekros was disinterred in [Update 10.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_10#Update_10.0 "Update 10") (2013-09-13).

**Nekros** is a necromancer [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") providing crowd control. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") restores [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") when enemies are slain near him.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SoulPunch130xWhite.png/32px-SoulPunch130xWhite.png?5b4b8)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Soul_Punch) [Soul Punch](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Soul_Punch) violently punches an enemy, instantly killing them when below a certain health and resurrecting them into a shadow that fights alongside Nekros.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Terrify130xWhite.png/32px-Terrify130xWhite.png?f7eae)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Terrify) [Terrify](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Terrify) projects a wave of fear that temporarily reduces [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") and causes enemies to run away.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Desecrate130xWhite.png/32px-Desecrate130xWhite.png?ca78f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desecrate) [Desecrate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desecrate) consumes corpses of fallen enemies to spawn [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") and potentially produce additional loot.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ShadowsOfTheDead130xWhite.png/32px-ShadowsOfTheDead130xWhite.png?faf4c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadows_of_the_Dead) [Shadows of the Dead](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadows_of_the_Dead) summons numerous shadows of slain enemies.

## Abilities

### Passive
A master of death,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/NekrosIcon272.png/31px-NekrosIcon272.png?67f74)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nekros) [Nekros](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nekros) and his [Companions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Companion "Companion") regenerates **5** [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health") when an enemy dies within **10** meters of him.

### **[Soul Punch](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Soul_Punch)**
A blow so powerful, it turns the enemy's very soul into a deadly projectile, damaging all in its path.

### **[Terrify](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Terrify)**
Casts fear into the hearts of nearby enemies, causing them to run away in terror.

### **[Desecrate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Desecrate)**
Forces fallen enemies around you to drop additional loot.

### **[Shadows of the Dead](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadows_of_the_Dead)**
Summons shadow versions of vanquished enemies to fight alongside you for a short period of time.
