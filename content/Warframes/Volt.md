Electricity flows through Volt. His attacks deal high damage. Enemies will be shocked.

**Release Date:** October 25th, 2012

Electrical currents surge wildly through his veins, generating lethal sparks and chain lightning in his wake. Ready to race against time with his fellow  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [[Tenno]], short-circuit robotics and overload organics as the Live Wire, **Volt**. Bend his electricity into an impenetrable barrier, or convert the shocked masses into deadly Tesla coils. Choose wisely, Tenno. Start your new journey with Volt as one of three beginner Warframes.

**Volt** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) powers to deal damage. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") builds electricity and discharges it in his next attack.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Shock130xWhite.png/32px-Shock130xWhite.png?38258)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shock) [Shock](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shock) releases an electric projectile.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Speed130xWhite.png/32px-Speed130xWhite.png?c22a3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Speed) [Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Speed) energizes his and his allies with enhanced [Movement Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed"), [Attack Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Attack_Speed "Attack Speed"), and [Reload Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reload_Speed "Reload Speed").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ElectricShield130xWhite.png/32px-ElectricShield130xWhite.png?03def)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Electric_Shield) [Electric Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Electric_Shield) projects a barrier that blocks incoming fire while strengthening allied gunfire that passes through it and can be carried around.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Discharge130xWhite.png/32px-Discharge130xWhite.png?300ec)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Discharge) [Discharge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Discharge) releases a powerful pulse that emits arcs of electricity on afflicted enemies.
## Abilities

### Passive
Traveling along the ground between  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VoltIcon272.png/31px-VoltIcon272.png?9e2f7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Volt) [Volt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Volt)'s attacks (by walking, running, or sliding) will build up static energy, adding bonus  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) damage toward Volt's next weapon attack or ability cast. For every **1** meter traveled, **10** points of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgElectricitySmall64.png/32px-DmgElectricitySmall64.png?c23d9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) [Electricity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage) damage are added, accumulating to a maximum of **1000** bonus damage.

### **[Shock](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shock)**
Launches a shocking projectile. It stuns and deals high damage to a single target and chains damage to nearby enemies.
> [!note]
> Lightning Bolt


### **[Speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Speed)**
Volt energizes his body and nearby Warframes, giving them increased speed and dexterity for a short time.

### **[Electric Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Electric_Shield)**
Volt deploys an obstacle of energy, providing cover in any situation.
> [!note]
> Creates a shield that increases critical hit damage when shot through


### **[Discharge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Discharge)**
Paralyze nearby hostiles with a damaging electric charge, this also shocks approaching enemies.
