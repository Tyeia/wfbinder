**Release Date:** February 5th, 2014

Light-footed and graceful, the beautiful and deadly **Zephyr** is in her element while airborne. With the power to bend air currents, she is an agile skyterror who delivers swift judgement from above. Zephyr soared to the skies in [Update 12.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_12#Update_12.0 "Update 12") (2014-02-05).

**Zephyr** is a [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that commands wind which grants her damage and crowd control. Her [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") reduces her falling speed while increasing [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") while airborne.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TailWind130xWhite.png/32px-TailWind130xWhite.png?37c13)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tail_Wind) [Tail Wind](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tail_Wind) launches Zephyr in a direction, or sustains her altitude to hover.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Airburst130xWhite.png/32px-Airburst130xWhite.png?0776b)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Airburst) [Airburst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Airburst) launches a blast of wind that forcefully pushes or groups enemies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Turbulence130xWhite.png/32px-Turbulence130xWhite.png?f3112)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Turbulence) [Turbulence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Turbulence) summons a shield that deflects enemy projectiles.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Tornado130xWhite.png/32px-Tornado130xWhite.png?e6bd4)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tornado) [Tornado](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tornado) summons deadly tornados that roam, hovering enemies in ragdoll while absorbing elemental damage.

## Abilities

### Passive
While airborne,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ZephyrIcon272.png/31px-ZephyrIcon272.png?32601)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zephyr) [Zephyr](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zephyr) falls slower and is more maneuverable, and her equipped weapons gain a **150%** [Critical Chance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Chance "Critical Chance") bonus that [additively stacks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Additive_Stacking "Additive Stacking") to the bonuses of critical chance [Mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mods "Mods").

### **[Tail Wind](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tail_Wind)**
From the ground, charge and release to launch Zephyr into an airborne hover. From the air, tap to dash forward, or aim down to dive bomb enemies below.

### **[Airburst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Airburst)**
Launch a burst of massively dense air. Hold to send enemies flying, tap to pull them toward the burst. Damage increases per enemy hit.

### **[Turbulence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Turbulence)**
Creates a wind shield around Zephyr, redirecting all incoming projectiles.

### **[Tornado](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tornado)**
Create deadly tornadoes that seek out and engulf enemies. Tornadoes deal the elemental damage type they absorb the most. Shoot enemies engulfed in Tornadoes to do additional damage.
