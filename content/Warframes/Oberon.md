**Release Date:** December 19th, 2013

Endowed with zealous offensive powers and sacred protective skills, **Oberon** is a balanced fighter with assault and supportive capabilities. Oberon came to light in [Update 11.5](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_11#Update_11.5 "Update 11") (2013-12-19).

**Oberon** is a paladin [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that provides support. His [Passive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Passive "Passive") rejuvenates [Companions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Companion "Companion") with [Health](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health "Health"), [Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield "Shield"), and [Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor") Links and grants them an additional [revive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death "Death").  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Smite130xWhite.png/32px-Smite130xWhite.png?9e6e7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smite) [Smite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smite) releases an energy of light on the target.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HallowedGround130xWhite.png/32px-HallowedGround130xWhite.png?e7d86)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hallowed_Ground) [Hallowed Ground](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hallowed_Ground) creates a field that applies  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgRadiationSmall64.png/32px-DmgRadiationSmall64.png?91a72)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Radiation_Damage) [Radiation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Radiation_Damage) to enemies and [Status Effect](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect "Status Effect") cleanse and immunity to allies.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Renewal130xWhite.png/32px-Renewal130xWhite.png?2dea1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Renewal) [Renewal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Renewal) projects a field around him that bolsters health regeneration over time, armor, and bleedout timer reduction.  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Reckoning130xWhite.png/32px-Reckoning130xWhite.png?3f641)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reckoning) [Reckoning](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reckoning) forcibly lifts enemies before slamming them into the ground, with slain enemies having increased [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb") drop chance; enemies that are Reckoned over Hallowed Ground receive a reduction to their armor.

## Abilities

### Passive
Guarded by nature, when  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/OberonIcon272.png/31px-OberonIcon272.png?8611f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Oberon) [Oberon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Oberon) picks up a regular [Health Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Health_Orb "Health Orb"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Dispensary130xWhite.png/32px-Dispensary130xWhite.png?b3988)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary) [Dispensary](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary) produced Empowered variant, or [Universal Orb](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Universal_Orb "Universal Orb"), he blesses himself and allies within [Affinity Range](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Affinity_Range "Affinity Range") with **Righteous Negation** for up to **3** charges. Incoming damage is blocked to consume a charge while also becoming [Invulnerable](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable") for **0.25** seconds (**0.5** seconds on the final charge). Charges last **indefinitely** until consumed.

- Each guarded Warframe consumes stacks individually and do **not** reduce the stacks on other guarded Warframes.
- When possessing Righteous Negation, the Warframe is surrounded by up to three energy shields with antler-like protrusions.
- Amount of remaining stacks are shown on the ability icon and as a buff icon beside Oberon's hitpoints indicator.
### **[Smite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smite)**
Focuses deadly energy within a target, dealing massive damage and removing all of its defenses, including Overguard. Enemies near the target also take a portion of the damage.

### **[Hallowed Ground](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hallowed_Ground)**
Sanctifies the ground before Oberon, inflicting [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RadiationSymbol.png/14px-RadiationSymbol.png?700cc)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:RadiationSymbol.png)Radiation Damage to all enemies within the radius. Also grants protection against Status Effects for Oberon and his allies.

### **[Renewal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Renewal)**
Generates a protective aura that grants bonus Armor to Oberon and his allies and restores allies’ Health over time. Healing is doubled while within the radius of Hallowed Ground.

### **[Reckoning](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reckoning)**
Lifts enemies into the air and then hurls them down with conviction, removing their Armor. Enemies hit by this ability will grant Oberon additional Armor and have a chance to spawn a [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HealOrb.png/14px-HealOrb.png?ab29e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:HealOrb.png)Health Orb when they die.
