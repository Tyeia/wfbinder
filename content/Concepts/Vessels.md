The **Vessels** are unfinished experiments of the prominent  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconOrokinOn.png/32px-IconOrokinOn.png?07e7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin) scientist, [Albrecht Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati").
# Overview
The Vessels are titanic, bio-mechanical, humanoid automatons that lay unfinished within [Albrecht's Laboratories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht%27s_Laboratories "Albrecht's Laboratories") under [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"). One of Albrecht's last experiments before his disappearance, these simulacra were created as a last resort to defend the Origin System from the eldritch forces of the [Indifference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_the_Wall "The Man in the Wall").

Despite their seemingly inert state, the Vessels' heartbeats and breathing can be heard while exploring the labs. A constant reminder that they are indeed alive.

The Vessels appear to be large-scale replicas of the human body, complete with functioning internal organs such as brains and blood vessels. Similar to the [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes"), they were cultivated from a unique  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) Strain, this one being the [Grey Strain](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grey_Strain "Grey Strain").
# Lore & History
### Albrecht's Notes

_Main article: [Fragments/Albrecht](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fragments/Albrecht "Fragments/Albrecht")_

After traveling to the year [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999"), Albrecht returned with samples from his [Helminth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth "Helminth")-[infused](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arthur "Arthur") [volunteers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aoi "Aoi"), and began to study and cultivate them within his [Labs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht%27s_Laboratories "Albrecht's Laboratories") under [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"). As the moon was overrun with the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Grey Strain](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grey_Strain "Grey Strain"), Albrecht's assistant [Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid_\(Original\) "Loid (Original)") pointed out how the Strain stimulates the growth of the afflicted, causing them to grow to monstrous dimensions.

With this new source of inspiration, Albrecht experimented with combining both the Grey Strain and samples from Arthur and Aoi together. Using precise biochemical engineering, combined with the anatomical perfection of [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas")' [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes"), along with the titanic potency of the Grey Strain and the humanity of the [Protoframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protoframe "Protoframe"), he created the equivalents of Warframes, but to a titanic scale; constructs which could stand a chance against [The Man in the Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_the_Wall "The Man in the Wall") and its eldritch forces, assuming an [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") could be found to pilot them.

### [Whispers in the Walls](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Whispers_in_the_Walls "Whispers in the Walls")

While most of the Vessels currently remain unfinished, they are still used to access parts of the Labs, with one having its head converted into a teleporter. While presumably meant to actually physically battle The Man in the Wall's wall-form directly, the Vessels proved capable of pacifying another Void-corrupted Vessel not with violence, but by recreating a moment of tenderness between Loid and Albrecht.

The first attempt to [Transference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Transference "Transference") on a Vessel results in the Operator briefly time travelling into [Arthur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arthur "Arthur")'s perspective in the year [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999").

### [The Hex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Quest\) "The Hex (Quest)")

Loid makes further modifications to the Transference process that allows the [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") time travel properly, but is rejected by Arthur when they attempt to Transference into him. They eventually discover that Albrecht intended for them to befriend  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HexIcon.png/32px-HexIcon.png?c8c7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) [The Hex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) which allows them to pilot Arthur's body to safety and thus properly gain control of the Vessels.
