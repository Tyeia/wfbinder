> What do you reckon it's like then... being brain-shelved? Just... dark, I reckon.

—A Solaris rig-jockey in [Fortuna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fortuna "Fortuna")

**Brain-shelving** is a punishment that is earned when a [Solaris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris "Solaris") is unable to fully pay off the debt of their cybernetic modifications.
