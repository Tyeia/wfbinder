Continuity, in its very essence, is the destructive transfer of consciousness from one body to another, with the mind-altering properties of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Kuva.png/32px-Kuva.png?0db18)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_\(Resource\)) [Kuva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_\(Resource\)) playing a key role in it.

During the Orokin Era, Continuity was typically performed during an event called the **Yuvan Ceremony**. During these rituals, an Orokin, whose current body has grown old and frail, would invade the mind of a prospective host (ritualistically referred to as a **Yuvan**) under the influence of the liquid Kuva until only the Orokin's mind remained inside the body, erasing the host's existing consciousness.

Lead and organized by the **[Yuvan Clerisy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvan_Clerisy "Yuvan Clerisy")**, these festival-like ceremonies were generally held in Yuvan theaters, where the young and beautiful were paraded around and put on display for numerous elderly and dying Orokin to inspect. The Orokin would then barter with each other on which Yuvan they would get to choose in their Continuity.

Locations with a strong connection to the Void seemed to aid in the success of the ceremony, likely because of its metaphysical nature being influenced by emotions and memories. These connections typically appeared on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua") as **Void Conjunctions**, with the locations of such events being converted into two of the holiest [Yuvan temples](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvan_Theater "Yuvan Theater"); the **[Circulus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Circulus "Circulus")** and the **[Yuvarium](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yuvarium "Yuvarium")**. Additionally, as long as the proper steps were taken, the Continuity rituals held in these temples would be a guaranteed success. Because of this, these two temples were exclusively reserved for use by the Seven alone.

It also is suggested by [Cephalon Fragment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fragments/Cephalon "Fragments/Cephalon") recordings and the [Worm Queen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens")'s dialogue that Orokin can also participate in Continuity even after their body has died. Additionally, as described in  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VorunaIcon272.png/31px-VorunaIcon272.png?f024e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna) [Voruna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna)’s [Leverian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leverian#_Voruna "Leverian"), Continuity can even be performed on recent deceased individuals, and even on multiple individuals at once. Whether or not the success of a ritual performed on a deceased individual is determined by how recently they died, and how fresh their body is, is unknown.

The Worm Queen currently uses her harvested Kuva to create [**Kuva Liches**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Lich "Kuva Lich"), Grineer super soldiers who are rendered all but seemingly immortal. The only way for a Warframe to properly combat these Liches is to mod their [Parazon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parazon "Parazon") with [Requiem Mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Requiem_Mods "Category:Requiem Mods") to create a unique passphrase or Requiem Sequence, to permanently sever their Continuity. This allows the Warframe to take down a Lich for good, or purify their Kuva and convert them into a loyal ally.
# Yuvan
A **Yuvan** is a person whose body has been chosen as a replacement for an elderly Orokin's dying body during the ritual of Continuity.

Some may have willingly become Yuvans, while others were kidnapped and forced to become one against their will. Subservient colonies, such as one on [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"), would have their children kidnapped by the Orokin, who they referred to as the 'Golden Skymen'. The abductees would then be taken to various Yuvan theaters to be used as unwilling candidates in the Yuvan Ceremony.

During The War Within, the [Elder Queen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens") refers to the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") as her Yuvan, and attempts to conduct the ritual of Continuity on them.

The person that became  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/YareliPrimeIcon272.png/31px-YareliPrimeIcon272.png?6e720)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli/Prime) [Yareli Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli/Prime) was a Yuvan who captivated [[Ballas]], but she openly disrespected the theater which angered him so much that she was converted into a Warframe instead of being sacrificed by the ritual.
# Ritual Process
The exact procedure taken to initiate the ritual of Continuity is unknown, although some steps can be inferred from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VorunaIcon272.png/31px-VorunaIcon272.png?f024e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna) [Voruna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna)'s [Leverian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leverian#Voruna "Leverian") entry, and from what we currently know.

A Soprano was appointed to ‘bridge the gap between worlds’ with her song. Should a Soprano not be appointed, a [Mandachord](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mandachord "Mandachord") may be used to play automated notes. A spell was then used to imbue a container of Kuva with eldritch energies, causing it to boil and steam. This energized Kuva was then used to transfer the mind of an elderly Orokin into the body of their chosen Yuvan.

During the ritual, it may be possible that the invading mind is able to see and experience the memories of the host, as the Elder Queen was able to recall the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator")’s memories of being aboard the [Zariman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero"), despite her never being aboard it herself.

If the energized Kuva is poured over two beings, then it appears to biologically fuse them together into one single entity. Due to this, the fused parts of the candidate then seem to take on the appearance of the host, as seen with the wolf heads fused to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VorunaIcon272.png/31px-VorunaIcon272.png?f024e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna) [Voruna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voruna)'s shoulders and hips.
