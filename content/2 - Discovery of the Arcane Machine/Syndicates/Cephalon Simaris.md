> Through synthesis we can rebuild and preserve. Create memory-immortals within this data oasis. Will you become enlightened Tenno? Will you hunt for me?

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CephalonSimarisSigil.png/32px-CephalonSimarisSigil.png?f0cb7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Simaris) Cephalon Simaris (or referred to as **Irmis** by [Cephalon Suda](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Suda "Cephalon Suda")) is a [Cephalon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon "Cephalon") construct that resides within the [Sanctuary](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sanctuary_\(Cephalon_Simaris\) "Sanctuary (Cephalon Simaris)") Enclave, a room found in all [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") [Relays](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Relay "Relay"). A seeker of knowledge, his ultimate goal is to "immortalize" creatures through the process known as [Synthesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synthesis "Synthesis"), that deconstructs a being and preserves them as data for further research within the Sanctuary.
