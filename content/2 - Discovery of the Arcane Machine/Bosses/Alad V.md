**Alad V** was a high ranking  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) member, seated on the [Corpus Board of Directors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus_Board_of_Directors "Corpus Board of Directors") and the head of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) Relations. He is the most recurring Corpus [boss](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Boss "Boss") in terms of his involvements with various [Events](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Event "Event").

Originally the creator of the ambitious [Zanuka Project](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka_Project "Zanuka Project"), an initiative to create [advanced robotics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka "Zanuka") by using [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") components, its eventual failure saw him removed from the Board of Directors and replaced with his longtime rival, [Frohd Bek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Frohd_Bek "Frohd Bek"). He would later form unlikely alliances with his enemies, namely the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested), the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno), and even the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient)s, to produce new schemes or ensure his survival while on the run from his former  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) backers and the [Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stalker "Stalker")'s [Acolytes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acolytes "Acolytes").

# Lore
### General

Alad V was a member of the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) Board of Directors and the head of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) Relations. In spite of his position, Alad showed a particular contempt towards the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) (whom he refers to as 'dogs') and to a lesser extent, the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno). Over the course of his career, Alad would develop an obsession with the Tenno and (later on) the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) and the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient), believing that he could harness their power for his own benefit.

### [Operation Sling-Stone](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sling-Stone_Event "Sling-Stone Event")

During one set of negotiations, the [Twin Queens](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Twin_Queens "Twin Queens") demanded that Alad reduce the price of some materials after several stockpiles were discovered, threatening to unleash a [Fomorian-class 'supership'](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fomorian "Fomorian") if he refused. Alad decided this was a bluff, unaware that these Fomorian ships did exist. When he realized his mistake, he turned to the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) for assistance.

Although the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") did not want to play any part in the conflict, the threat of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) power expanding convinced her to reconsider Alad's plea. This resulted in [Operation Sling-Stone](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sling-Stone_Event "Sling-Stone Event"), where the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) would sabotage the Fomorians' reactors by corrupting them with  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) material. Eventually the last of these ships fell and the Grineer withdrew, although Alad dispatched scouts to [Pluto](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pluto "Pluto") in response to rumors of surviving ships.

### [Operation: Arid Fear](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Arid_Fear "Operation: Arid Fear")

Ultimately, the Queens agreed to Alad's initial contract, but decided to pay more than what he asked for in exchange for his assistance; the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) had several [unknown settlements](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Settlement "Grineer Settlement") that they wanted to hide from the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno). Knowing that the Tenno are aware of their methods via their [capture missions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Capture "Capture"), Alad decided to send his scouts and navigators to [The Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"), in the hopes of hiding the information as long as he could. Despite this, the Lotus discovered his covert operations (in what became [Operation: Arid Fear](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Arid_Fear "Operation: Arid Fear")) and sent the Tenno to hunt down his scouts. Despite Alad's best efforts, the Tenno recovered the information and were able to find the settlements' planet (which was originally [Phobos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Phobos "Phobos") at the time the event took place, but has presumably been retconned to [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars") in [Update 19.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_19#Update_19.0 "Update 19") (2016-11-11)).

### [The Gradivus Dilemma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Gradivus_Dilemma "The Gradivus Dilemma")

Later on, Alad would focus his resources on the [Zanuka Project](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka_Project "Zanuka Project"), after discovering a cache of unawakened Warframes near [Sedna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sedna "Sedna"). Upon hearing this, [General Sargas Ruk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/General_Sargas_Ruk "General Sargas Ruk") of the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) ordered Alad to hand the cache over, on the grounds that they were found in Grineer territory. Alad responded by setting up a defensive force on [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars") in order to repel the inevitable offensive that would follow his refusal. Enraged, Ruk demanded that all  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) territory would be handed over to the Grineer, or else there would be swift consequences.

This information was picked up by the Lotus, who was unwilling to sway the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) towards either side. Instead, she left the Tenno with two options; support the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) to rescue the sleeping Tenno, at the cost of the Grineer gaining more control, or support the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) to drive off the Grineer, sacrificing their fellow Tenno in the process. The ensuing conflict would later be known as [The Gradivus Dilemma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Gradivus_Dilemma "The Gradivus Dilemma").

After several skirmishes, the Board of Directors decided to stop bankrolling Alad due to the resources that were being wasted, resulting in a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) victory. The Board also stopped the funding of the Zanuka Project, forcing Alad to continue his project without their assistance. He subsequently set up [research facilities](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus_Gas_City "Corpus Gas City") on [Jupiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jupiter "Jupiter").

### [The Hunt For Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hunt_For_Alad_V "The Hunt For Alad V")

Following the Dilemma, the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) discovered that Alad had [a captured Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr "Valkyr") he was experimenting on as part of the Project. Alad's former colleague [Frohd Bek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Frohd_Bek "Frohd Bek") was tracking him down in response to the massive debts he had accrued during the Dilemma (though Bek had his own reasons, namely fearing that Alad could gain more power in the Board should the Zanuka Project prove successful). In response, Alad diverted the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) to Frohd's ships, overwhelming them in the process and slowing down Frohd's progress.

Frohd Bek decided to cut a deal with the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno): eliminate the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) from his ships so he could locate Alad, then offer said location to the Tenno. Despite the Lotus' initial distrust, she ultimately agreed to Frohd's terms, and the ensuing [hunt for Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hunt_for_Alad_V "The Hunt for Alad V") ultimately proved successful. Although he was able to construct a working [Zanuka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka "Zanuka") prototype by the time he was found, the Tenno defeated him and rescued the Warframe from his clutches. Despite this, Alad continues to work on the Project and occasionally sends out [Zanuka Hunters](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka_Hunter "Zanuka Hunter") to search for more "donors".

### [Suspicious Shipments](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Shipments "Suspicious Shipments")

After a long period of inactivity, the Lotus discovered [several Corpus ships](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Shipments "Suspicious Shipments") that were traveling well outside usual shipping routes. These ships were heavily armored, and used several measures to keep themselves hidden from prying eyes. Suspicious about their purpose, the Lotus sent the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) to investigate these ships and their mysterious cargo.

The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) discovered that many of these ships contained samples of  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) tissue, heavily guarded by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) platoons, though some of these ships had suffered containment breaches and had been overrun with the Infested. Alad contacted the Tenno during their investigations, explaining that he disagreed with the Corpus ban on Infested Biotechnology, and had decided to focus his resources on this forbidden line of research.

### [Operation: Breeding Grounds](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation_Breeding_Grounds "Operation Breeding Grounds")

The Lotus learned that Alad's experiments had allowed the Infestation to evolve, allowing it to infect and corrupt [inorganic beings](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mutalist "Mutalist"). Lotus also discovered that Alad had also developed [Infested Hives](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested_Hive "Infested Hive") as a means of directing the spread of the  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested); these hives could corrupt new ships or colonies almost immediately.

After hearing word of [a massive Infestation outbreak](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation_Breeding_Grounds "Operation Breeding Grounds") near [Eris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eris "Eris"), Lotus ordered the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) to intercept the [Infested ships](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested_Corpus_Ship "Infested Corpus Ship") and destroy the hives in order to halt the outbreak, fearing that the newly evolved strain would quickly overrun the System if left unchecked.

Although the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) succeeded in stopping the outbreak, Alad sent out [one last message](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Infestedaladreply.png "File:Infestedaladreply.png") that mocked the Tenno's efforts, claiming that their actions had only left Alad with the hardiest, most vicious strains at his disposal.

### [Operation: Mutalist Incursions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Mutalist_Incursions "Operation: Mutalist Incursions")

With the evolved  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) at his disposal, Alad unveiled his plan to unleash a new "Mutalist Empire," infecting  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer), and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) alike in an attempt to unify and subordinate them. At this point the Lotus believed that the Infestation had corrupted Alad's mind, describing him as the Infestation itself. During this time, [Mutalist Incursions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Mutalist_Incursions "Operation: Mutalist Incursions") would periodically surface across the Origin System, and the Tenno were tasked to purge them with the help of the Corpus and the Grineer.

Although the valiant and combined effort of the three factions quickly pushed the Mutalist Infestation back to Eris, they ultimately failed to fully exterminate the new breeds, which remained a permanent threat to the system.

### [Patient Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Patient_Zero "Patient Zero")

In the midst of the Mutalist Incursions, the Lotus also directed the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) to once again [locate Alad](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Patient_Zero "Patient Zero") - who at this point had [hybridized himself with Infested tissue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mutalist_Alad_V "Mutalist Alad V") - and destroy his research labs. During their searches, Alad contacted the Tenno and revealed that he had [another Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mesa "Mesa") in his possession, planning to infuse it with  [![|30](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Infestation_w.svg?f34ff)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested) tissue. As the search continued, the [Infested Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested_Mesa "Infested Mesa") - the epitome of Alad V's Mutalist experiments - was finally revealed and the Lotus instructed the Tenno to put it out of its misery.

Despite the destruction of his experiments, Alad expressed no grudge towards the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) and instead announced merely his "disappointment", remarking that this could have been a chance for them to redeem their past "mistakes". Eventually, Alad was located once again and defeated.

### [Operation: Tubemen of Regor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Tubemen_of_Regor "Operation: Tubemen of Regor")

After a long period of inactivity, it was revealed the work of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) researcher [Tyl Regor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tyl_Regor "Tyl Regor") had attracted Alad's attention. Alad, regretting his involvement with the Infestation, sent a message to the Lotus, asking for the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) to obtain samples of Tyl Regor's research before sabotaging his labs in the hopes of finding a cure for himself. The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) aristocrat [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo"), however, saw this plea to the Lotus as a threat to his new position on the Corpus Board and immediately broadcast his alternate proposition for the Tenno: to destroy Tyl Regor's lab outright and prevent Alad V from possibly developing a cure for himself.

As both sides offered the locations of Regor's labs, the Lotus refused to sway the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) into a specific choice, and it was ultimately up to them to decide which of the two was the lesser evil. In the end, Alad V proved victorious in the PC and XB1 builds of the game, whereas Nef Anyo was triumphant in the PS4 build.

### [The Second Dream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Second_Dream "The Second Dream")

Alad V, whose Infestation had significantly receded compared to his [last encounter with the Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Tubemen_of_Regor "Operation: Tubemen of Regor"), offered his assistance in locating the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow")'s remains, in exchange for a future "favor". He remained in contact with the Lotus as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) pursued the [Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stalker "Stalker") to the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") and expressed a desire to dissect a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Battalyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Battalyst "Battalyst") once one was encountered.

Alad makes clear his amazement when it is revealed that the Lotus had hidden the [Orokin-era Moon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Moon "Orokin Moon"), [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"), deep in the Void, to protect the Tenno "heart" from the Sentients. Alad, remembering his own experiments with dissecting Warframes, wonders why this heart is not in their chest, suggesting that he had some slight awareness of Warframes not being what they seem. During the conflict, [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow") addresses the meddlesome Alad as "[Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin")" on several occasions, ultimately threatening to kill him. This forces Alad to flee, but not before reminding the Lotus about his favor.

### [Operation: Shadow Debt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Shadow_Debt "Operation: Shadow Debt")

Calling in the favor he obtained during [The Second Dream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Second_Dream "The Second Dream"), Alad requests the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno)'s assistance in locating and hunting down the [Acolytes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acolyte "Acolyte") deployed to hunt him down. He instructs the Tenno to break into  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) systems to collect security data, and from there uses said data to locate the Acolytes for the Tenno to hunt down.

Once most of the Acolytes were defeated, Alad found out that there was [one more](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Misery "Misery") Acolyte heading his way. Fearing that this last Acolyte was possibly the strongest out of the group, he asked the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) to defend him directly. After the Tenno protected Alad from the Acolytes, he gave the Tenno [the](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lacera_Scorn_Skin "Lacera Scorn Skin") [rewards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vulkar_Wraith "Vulkar Wraith") he had promised, asking them not to tell anyone about their deal.

### [The Index](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Index "The Index")

Alad V is known to have worked closely with Loan Reclamation Division of [The Index](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Index "The Index"), and despite his involuntary retirement due to his body being taken over by the Infestation, some still say that [Nako Xol](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nako_Xol "Nako Xol") and [Ved Xol](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ved_Xol "Ved Xol") of the said division still undertake his assignment acting on his behalf. A [Zanuka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka "Zanuka")-like proxy, [Lockjaw](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lockjaw_%26_Sol "Lockjaw & Sol"), which was delivered as payment for services rendered only serves to strengthen this suspicion.

### [Wolf of Saturn Six](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wolf_of_Saturn_Six "Wolf of Saturn Six")

Preluding to [The Jovian Concord](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V#The_Jovian_Concord), Alad V received  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) inmates from [Gharn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gharn "Gharn"), the Warden of the Grineer Max-Pen [Saturn Six](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Saturn#Lore "Saturn") that was previously undisclosed and named for being "_some six leagues deep in the gas_". The pen's model prisoner, the [Wolf of Saturn Six](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wolf_of_Saturn_Six "Wolf of Saturn Six"), discovered this trafficking of his fellow inmates and broke out, but instead of fleeing he hacked into the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) communications network on [Jupiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jupiter "Jupiter") and sent them a demand to "**DELIVER GHARN**".

Alad V replied "**GHARN IS YOURS**", drawing the Wolf out only to imprison him again as an experiment for his [Amalgam](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam "Amalgam") project, with him and his pack becoming infused with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) hybridization, and then sent him to hunt down the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno). However, the Wolf broke free from Alad's control and shortly thereafter disappeared.
