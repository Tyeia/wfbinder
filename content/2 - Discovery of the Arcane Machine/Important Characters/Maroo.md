**Maroo** is a thief who helps the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") and the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") uncover the [Arcane Codices](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Codices "Arcane Codices") after being hunted by her former employers, the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer"), during the [Stolen Dreams](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stolen_Dreams "Stolen Dreams") [quest](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quest "Quest"). As a thief, her interests lay in anything that helps her in the pursuit of money and riches. She runs her own relay on [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"), exclusively for [Trading](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Trading "Trading") with other players and to access her [Weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maroo#Weekly_Mission).

# Lore
### General

Maroo's loyalties lie only to herself or to the highest bidder, as she was first shown working for the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") in recovering the [Arcane Codices](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Codices "Arcane Codices"), only to betray them and sell them to the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") instead. She has very little respect for either the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") or the Tenno, condescendingly referring to the latter as "_Tin Suits"_. She has a considerable level of skill, as she was able to locate and recover the Codices (located deep in an [Orokin Derelict](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Derelict "Orokin Derelict")) without serious injury (where several Grineer troops have failed and/or perished trying to do so) and was capable of initiating conflict between the Grineer and the Corpus stationed on [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"), with the intent of stealing anything of value amidst the confusion.

### [Stolen Dreams](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stolen_Dreams "Stolen Dreams")

Maroo was initially contracted by [Tyl Regor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tyl_Regor "Tyl Regor") to find the Arcane Codices, only to sell the last three pieces of the Codices to the Corpus and subsequently go into hiding. Curious as to what the Codices contain, the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") sends the Tenno to a Corpus outpost on [Europa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Europa "Europa") to subdue her. Under the Tenno's custody Maroo initially refuses to reveal where the last Codex fragments are located, but when the Lotus threatens to hand her back to Regor, Maroo quickly points the location where the Codices are stored. As the Tenno [infiltrate the Corpus' data vaults](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spy "Spy"), [Ordis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ordis "Ordis") theorizes that the Arcane Codex could lead to an [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") treasure, much to Maroo's delight.

Once the Tenno collect all five of the Arcane Codices, Ordis explains that any machine that could read the Codex has probably degraded beyond use, only for Maroo to disclose that she extracted the Codices from an Arcane Machine within an [Orokin Derelict](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Derelict "Orokin Derelict"). Theorizing that the Machine could read the combined Codex, the Tenno head out to [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars") (which has a portal leading to said Derelict) to access the Arcane Machine. Once they insert the Arcane Codex however, the Machine broadcasts [a cryptic message](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:ArcaneCodexMessage.ogg "File:ArcaneCodexMessage.ogg") before vanishing without a trace. Disappointed that nothing of value was found, Maroo parts ways with the Tenno.

### Project Undermine [Tactical Alert](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tactical_Alert "Tactical Alert")

In the midst of unrest between the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") and the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"), the Lotus intercepts a bizarre distress signal broadcasted from [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"), and traces it to a prison located on [Ceres](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ceres "Ceres"). The Tenno head to Ceres to free the unknown prisoner, who ultimately turns out to be Maroo. She explains that she initiated the very conflict being waged between the Corpus and the Grineer, in the hopes of stealing items of value unnoticed. Unfortunately, the chaos attracted the attention of the Grineer's elite [Nightwatch Corps](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nightwatch_Corps "Nightwatch Corps"), who subsequently ended up discovering Maroo and capturing her. Thanking the Tenno for the rescue, she points them to a location to thin out the Corps' numbers and ultimately drive them off.
