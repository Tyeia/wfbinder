**Parvos Granum** is the original founder of the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin")-era [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") merchant cult and the inventor of [Specter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Specter "Specter") technology. As part of a deal he made with the Orokin, he received the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ProteaIcon272.png/31px-ProteaIcon272.png?6b6d2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea) [Protea](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea) Warframe as a bodyguard. He was presumed dead for millennia after being betrayed by the [Corpus Board of Directors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus_Board_of_Directors "Corpus Board of Directors"), where an assassination attempt resulted in his ship being lost during a faulty [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") translation.

  
Unlike the current-era Corpus, most notably [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo"), who have achieved their wealth through slothful means, Parvos Granum is a cold, calculating, and intelligent individual who values hard work and will actively take steps and measures to achieve his goals. Disappointed in how low the Corpus have fallen on his return from the Void, Parvos proclaims he will restructure the Corpus or will otherwise separate himself from them to form a splinter faction. Following his return, the current Corpus Board of Directors have been largely absent as Parvos leads Corpus forces in [space](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Empyrean "Empyrean"), formed the [Sisters of Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos "Sisters of Parvos") with [Vala Glarios](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vala_Glarios "Vala Glarios") as his assistant, has dispatched salvage teams to the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero"), and hired [Drusus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drusus "Drusus") to acquire [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati "Entrati") artifacts from [Albrecht's Laboratories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht%27s_Laboratories "Albrecht's Laboratories").

Parvos Granum grew up during the height of the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") era, together with his brother [Cladius](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cladius "Cladius"), who he admired for his physical strength while being frustrated with his naiveté and lack of ambition, occasionally imagining he had a clever sister instead. They lived with their father on a small farm as grain farmers, the same as dozens of generations of their family before them. After years and years of back-breaking manual labor, one-day Parvos left for the nearby Orokin city. He was awestruck by the decadent splendor of their gem-encrusted gold and ivory architecture, flaunting their wealth accrued through their greed upon the simple living people like himself and his family. As he shouted "I take what I am owed!" he ripped a handful of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Rubedo.png/32px-Rubedo.png?1da20)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rubedo) [Rubedo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rubedo) jewels from the gates and tried to run, but was promptly caught by the Orokin servants which dragged him to the town square. As punishment for theft, his left hand was cut off with a plasma dagger but while the gathered crowd cheered he managed to swallow one of the Rubedo gemstones.

With the Rubedo burning up inside him Parvos managed to crawl back to the farmstead, where his father and brother found him collapsed and near death. After coming back to his senses he threw up the molten Rubedo, a feat that Parvos affirms would have killed any man of lesser conviction, and that the single stone was worth more than ten generations' farm work.

The next day Parvos returned to the city, pawning the gem for a financial loan. His newfound wealth in hand he then loaned parts of it to other poor folk like him, who desired more of their life. With each new investment or associate, Parvos taught each one of them his philosophy of the evils of contentment and idleness as well the gift of **Desire**, while his partners themselves went on to further loan their money to others and spread his teachings, gathering more followers with each transaction.

In time his fortune had multiplied exponentially and he commissioned a golden prosthesis for his missing left hand.

One day during his journeys news reached him that his father had finally collapsed under the toiling farm work. Parvos rushed back to his home to find his father had already died.

Committed to ending the history of his family's brutal and extorted labor, he gave the order to flatten and demolish the farmstead and the surrounding fields, despite Cladius' protests. In their place, he erected a new city of the future called "**Corposium**", where even the smallest of men would be welcome to apply his wit and desire to make his own wealth.

With the precious sample obtained, development is accelerating. The [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati "Entrati") have kept their secrets long enough.
> 
> I am calling it the [Specter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Specter "Specter") Particle. As my Archimedian explains, mimicry is in its nature. An echo. Exposed to precept circuits, we are able to imprint behaviors! Like an infant mirroring her surrogate. Only this infant can set the world on fire.
> 
> The [palace birds](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") think they can control us. They hit our research site with a Warframe assault team. Of course they did. I baited them to do it. One of Parvos Granum's accomplishments was the creation of the **[Specter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Specter "Specter") Particle Theory** which would set the foundation for Specter technology that enables the imprinting of rudimentary behavior into bodies.

—[Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") and Parvos Granum on  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ProteaPrimeIcon272.png/31px-ProteaPrimeIcon272.png?cbc0c)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea/Prime) [Protea Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea/Prime)
Having acquired the [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati "Entrati")'s research into time, Parvos Granum conceived the designs for the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ProteaIcon272.png/31px-ProteaIcon272.png?6b6d2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea) [Protea](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea) Warframe to serve as his bodyguard. As part of an undisclosed deal, [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") obliged into creating the time-manipulating "child of two fathers."

Granum was later betrayed by the [Corpus Board of Directors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus_Board_of_Directors "Corpus Board of Directors"), whose assassins sabotaged his transport's [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") drive. However, he was saved by Protea's time powers, but this resulted him being trapped within a part of the Void which would later be known as the [Granum Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Granum_Void "Granum Void").
### [The Deadlock Protocol](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Deadlock_Protocol "The Deadlock Protocol")
> 'My name'. Hmm... I have watched you. Monuments to narcissism. Demanding others sweat in your stead. Gorging upon that which you have not earned. Watched... as you claimed for yourself my teachings of self-reliance, perverting them into a flaccid philosophy of sloth. Of... Idleness. Is that you, my son?
> 
> No, that must be a different son.

—Parvos Granum's disapproval of [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo")'s actions in the Corpus.
As the Corpus Board of Directors continuously reach a voting impasse, [Nef Anyo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nef_Anyo "Nef Anyo") invokes The Deadlock Protocol, a contingency initiative to instate a successor to the Founder. Nef Anyo makes the claim that he himself is the genetic heir which, if true, would give him total control over the Board. He intends to prove this by searching for traces of  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ProteaIcon272.png/31px-ProteaIcon272.png?6b6d2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea) [Protea](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protea), using Solaris workers as a sacrifice for Void-temporal jumps to reach the [Granum Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Granum_Void "Granum Void").

In an effort to thwart Nef Anyo's plans, the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") aids [Solaris United](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United "Solaris United") and gets to the Granum Void before Nef Anyo's henchmen do. There, they discover that Parvos Granum had survived from Protea's sacrifice by trapping them in a temporal prison, at the cost of Protea becoming a [Specter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Specter "Specter") of herself. The Warframe Specter becomes hostile and sends its [Errant Specters](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Errant_Specter "Errant Specter") throughout Nef Anyo's fleet; Parvos Granum does not deny Nef Anyo being his son but is disgusted with his twisted and slothful methods within the Corpus, stating he simply used him to summon the Tenno to reignite Protea into action.

Angered by this deception and the threat of the Protea Specter, Nef Anyo calls truce with Solaris United and delivers the Tenno the blueprint to the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Xoris.png/32px-Xoris.png?5c2e5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xoris) [Xoris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xoris), a glaive fashioned with Parvos Granum's Specter Particle Theory. Seeing Parvos Granum's high intelligence being a threat, Solaris United reluctantly accept the weapon. However, as the Errant Specters overrun Nef Anyo's fleet, he quickly breaks the truce and decides to self-destruct his ships. The Tenno jump into Granum Void once more and use the Xoris to save the trapped Solaris workers, and Parvos uses the chance to escape to real-space. He remarks dryly that the destruction of Anyo's fleet has left the Tenno trapped in the Void with the Protea Specter, but wishes them a polite goodbye during their final battle.

In the aftermath, Parvos Granum is unfazed by the defeat of the Protea Specter, sending the Tenno an inbox message stating he intends to restructure the Corpus to counter the [impending threat from Tau](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War"). Otherwise, he will abandon his children to create a new doctrine, inviting the Tenno and Solaris United a seat next to him.
### Corpus Proxima

All Corpus space territory in the [Venus Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venus_Proxima "Venus Proxima"), [Neptune Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neptune_Proxima "Neptune Proxima"), [Pluto Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pluto_Proxima "Pluto Proxima"), and [Veil Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veil_Proxima "Veil Proxima") are claimed to be "Granum space" by their [Corpus Captains](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus_Captain "Corpus Captain").

### [Call of the Tempestarii](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Call_of_the_Tempestarii "Call of the Tempestarii")

[Vala Glarios](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vala_Glarios "Vala Glarios") claims to be a servant of Parvos Granum in her quest for vengeance against the Tempestarii, an Old War Railjack that left her stranded in an attack. The Tenno thwarts Vala's efforts, using a Tempestarii ordinance to banish her into the Void. She appears in the [Granum Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Granum_Void "Granum Void") and collapses, where she is greeted by Parvos Granum who extends his golden hand to her.

### [Sisters of Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos "Sisters of Parvos")

Seeking to fulfill his desire of having a reliable sister, Parvos Granum recruits [Candidates](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Candidate "Candidate") to rise above the masses and become his Sister, providing them [Hound](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hound "Hound") companions, Warframe Specter guardians, and latent technological weapons in the form of [Tenet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenet "Tenet") weaponry. [Vala Glarios](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vala_Glarios "Vala Glarios") serves as his right-hand woman, overseeing the growth of the Sisters.

### [Angels of the Zariman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Angels_of_the_Zariman "Angels of the Zariman")

After [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War"), the long-lost Orokin colony ship [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero") reappeared from the Void. Immediately, both Parvos Granum and the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") [Worm Queen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens") sent their loyal subordinates to claim the legendary vessel in their name. Power aboard the Zariman periodically shifts in either side's favor as both the Tenno and the [beings](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel") now inhabiting the ship strike back at them to protect their home.

### Dante Unbound

Seeking to acquire artifacts pertaining to [Albrecht Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati"), Parvos Granum hired [Leverian](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leverian "Leverian") curator [Drusus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drusus "Drusus") for assistance. Desperate from lack of visitors and low donations, Drusus signed a deal, but when he failed to make payment on time,  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DanteIcon272.png/31px-DanteIcon272.png?aae25)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dante) [Dante](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dante)'s Leverian was seized as compensation. Drusus was forced to turn to the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") behind Parvos's back for assistance.

Desiring to weaponize  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JadeIcon272.png/31px-JadeIcon272.png?f1fb2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) [Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade), Parvos Granum's forces assault the [Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stalker "Stalker") on [Uranus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Uranus "Uranus") and seize Jade's  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VolatileMotes.png/32px-VolatileMotes.png?8b0ee)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Belly_of_the_Beast) [Volatile Motes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Belly_of_the_Beast) to create an army of [Jade Light](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Light "Jade Light") [Eximus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eximus "Eximus"). He recognizes [Ordis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ordis "Ordis")' [[Ordis' Past|former identity]] as Ordan Karris, believing the Cephalon's efforts to help Jade are squandered and offers to restore Ordan Karris to his former glory.
