The **Sentients** are an artificial race from the [Tau System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau_System "Tau System") that were the chief enemies of the [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") during [The Old War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Old_War "The Old War"). Originally suspected to be a truly _alien_ race (whereas the Orokin, [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer"), [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") and [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") are all human or trans-human variations originating from [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"), and the [Infested](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested") are a successive mutation of them), [memory imprints](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synthesis "Synthesis") provided by [Cephalon Simaris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Simaris "Cephalon Simaris") revealed that they were originally created by the Orokin as terraforming machines, to be sent to the Tau System. For this purpose, they were gifted with an extreme degree of adaptability and resilience, bordering on true self-determination. As their name suggests, they inadvertently gained [sentience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentience "wikipedia:Sentience"). Coupled with their own innate abilities, they proved to be a formidable threat for the Orokin Empire, almost leading it to its downfall. All Sentients deal  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgTauSmall64.png/32px-DmgTauSmall64.png?f7727)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Tau_Damage) [Tau](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Tau_Damage) damage, alluding to their home system.

[Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Sentient) enemies take increased damage from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgColdSmall64.png/32px-DmgColdSmall64.png?f2506)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) [Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgRadiationSmall64.png/32px-DmgRadiationSmall64.png?91a72)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Radiation_Damage) [Radiation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Radiation_Damage), but resist  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage). They also have a hidden weakness to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgVoidSmall64.png/32px-DmgVoidSmall64.png?e62bb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Void_Damage) [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Void_Damage), as it resets the damage adaptation that some Sentient units have.

# Lore & History
### Orokin Era

The [Detron Crewman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Detron_Crewman "Detron Crewman")'s [memory imprint](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synthesis "Synthesis") seems to suggest that the Sentients were originally developed as terraforming tools bound for the Tau system. The Orokin crafted them with the ability to adapt to any sort of damage that they would encounter as well as the ability to replicate from damaged components. However, they were developed with a flaw, a weakness to the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") to interfere with their adaptation and replication, which would theoretically have left them stranded in the Tau system once they had finished their purpose. Normally, the Orokin would be opposed to such creations fearing that they would betray their creators, but [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") feigned vehement opposition to manipulate the other Executors into approving them out of desperation for their dying [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System") and the Sentinent's supposed flaw.

However, the Orokin's fears did come to pass; the sentience they had gained also caused them to realize that their creators would also lay waste to the Tau System, and thus they returned to the Origin System to wage war with the Orokin. With their advanced adaptability they were taking over the most sophisticated Orokin technology, and gradually gaining powerful resistances even to the most advanced of Orokin weaponry. In desperation the Orokin utilized the [survivors](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") of the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero") incident, to whom the Void had gifted immense power, to fight back against the Sentient threat. They were trained in the old ways of battle and given [conduits](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") that could withstand, channel and amplify their power. With the use of the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno"), the Orokin managed to turn the tide of battle.

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Hunhow_face.png/236px-Hunhow_face.png?856b0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Hunhow_face.png)

Hunhow's ancient body, destroyed and submerged in the depths of Uranus' oceans

However, the Orokin were betrayed by Ballas, who desired his own rule and had grown vengeful over the death of his beloved [Margulis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Margulis "Margulis"). Using information provided to him by Ballas, [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow") sent his daughter [Natah](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Natah "Natah"), under a human appearance, to serve as a [mole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mole_\(espionage\) "wikipedia:Mole (espionage)") within the Origin System. She would destroy the Empire from within and exterminate the Tenno.

Natah completed Hunhow's main objective with the purge of the Orokin but she could not bring herself to kill the Tenno. Instead, Natah chose to adopt them as her own children, as her human predecessor, Margulis, had done before. She hid the Reservoir, the Tenno's only weakness, in the Void, keeping it safe from the retaliation of the Sentient. Denying her old identity, Natah took the name of the flower most beloved by Margulis, the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus"), and became the Tenno's custodian. The Sentients soon came to believe Natah had been reprogrammed by the Orokin when she encountered her brother [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") on a mission with several Operators, but did not recognize him despite his prodding. This provoked a long-standing disgust Hunhow's family has with Tenno in general, who view Operators as manipulative parasites.

### Plains of Eidolon

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Sentients.jpg/236px-Sentients.jpg?b6158)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Sentients.jpg)

Excalibur facing an army of [Conculysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conculyst "Conculyst") and [Battalysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Battalyst "Battalyst") in the 'Tombs of the Sentient' trailer.

Although the majority of Sentient forces were driven back in what is now called the Old War, the [Glass Shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fragments#Glass_Shard_Fragments "Fragments") encountered during [Saya's Vigil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Saya%27s_Vigil "Saya's Vigil") tell of one massive Sentient that managed to land on [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"). This powerful being laid waste to all but one of the cities and Towers: the Tower of the [**Unum**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Quills "The Quills"). The tower's defenses held firm with the help of its people and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GaraIcon272.png/31px-GaraIcon272.png?3956f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara) [Gara](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara), who stayed to help defend the tower while other Tenno had scattered. The Sentient, with its power waxing and waning with the dusk and dawn, would besiege the Unum's Tower by nightfall and retreat by morning. Gara would search for the Sentient's resting place during the daylight as the Unum had forbidden her from fighting the Sentient when it was at the height of its power. To aid Gara's search, the Unum gave her refined Temple [Kuva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva "Kuva") to her followers, and instructed them to feed it to the local wildlife, connecting their consciousnesses to her own and allowing the Unum to discover the Sentient's refuge as the animals roamed.

The Unum's plan had unintended consequences however, as when the Sentient captured one of the infused animals for study, it discovered that Kuva could restore its lost ability to replicate. Emboldened by the hope of rebirth, it escalated its nightly attacks as a result. As the Sentient besieged the Unum, Gara chose to confront the threat, carrying a bomb to its core and falling in battle. As the vile Sentient descended upon the mortally wounded Gara, the bomb detonated, devastating the Sentient in the process and ending its threat on the Unum once and for all.

As testament of this final, climactic battle, the Sentient's fossilized wreckage is littered across the [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon"), a peculiar landscape with potent and unique resources. The being's dark power still stains the ground, waxing with the fall of darkness and waning at the crack of dawn, [altering the environment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon#Magnetized_Water "Plains of Eidolon") each night as the [Eidolons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon"), its now-mindless fragments, wander the land in an endless search for completeness.

After Gara's sacrifice, the very site of her decisive battle with the Sentient left what the [Ostrons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron "Ostron") now call **Gara Toht Lake**, but unknown to all but the Unum and her most loyal followers [The Quills](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Quills "The Quills"), the Sentient's main consciousness lay submerged somewhere in the lake, and it would attempt to make itself whole once more when night fell. However, a Warframe known solely as the Warden would arrive, driving the Sentient back night after night. This trial would continue until one fateful evening, where the Warden foolishly tried to make contact with the Sentient and was overwhelmed. Discovering that the Sentient was using him as a means to reform itself, the Warden cast himself into the lake to seal it. The time spent as an "anchor" to the Sentient would infuse the Warden with its eldritch energies, transforming him into the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RevenantIcon272.png/31px-RevenantIcon272.png?186c5)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Revenant) [Revenant](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Revenant).

### Present-Day

Although the Sentients have been wiped out, they began to gradually resurface after [Tyl Regor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tyl_Regor "Tyl Regor") unearthed and reawakened [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow")'s remains on [Uranus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Uranus "Uranus"). Despite being beaten back by the Tenno and resealed, Hunhow and the [Shadow Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadow_Stalker "Shadow Stalker") are able to reveal [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua")'s presence to force the Tenno's [true form](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") out of hiding.

[Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") eventually returned, reverting the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") back to [Natah](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Natah "Natah") before manipulating both the Tenno and the Sentients into [a new war](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War"): he deceives the Tenno into believing that Natah is the true villain, and has [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)"), who believes his race is being led to peace and prosperity, deceive Natah into rallying the Sentient forces, who also manipulates [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V") into creating [Amalgam](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam "Amalgam") soldiers for the Sentients. Despite the Tenno unifying with the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") and [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") to fight back against a shared enemy, Ballas succeeds in banishing the Tenno and the Lotus into a Void portal.

Without the Tenno, the Origin System succumbs to the Sentients as they subjugate all into [Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer "Narmer"), with Ballas at the head. However, this victory is threatened by the [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter"), a paradoxical version of the Tenno's Operator, who recovers the Lotus and receives Hunhow's assistance to save their shared loved one, fighting off Erra's Sentient-Warframe [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon") in the process. An enraged Ballas responds by having the Sentient mothership [Praghasa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa") devour the Origin System's sun, abandoning Narmer so he can escape to [Tau](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau "Tau"). Erra, realizing Ballas intends to doom the Sentients, defects and sacrifices himself, ultimately allowing the Tenno and the Lotus to kill Ballas once and for all.

Narmer's losses are short-lived, however, as they are regrouped by their new leader [Pazuul](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pazuul "Pazuul"), a new Archon that hijacked Erra's corpse. Having revived the other Archons, Pazuul continues Narmer's and the Sentient's subjugation of the Origin System.

With the conclusion of the New War, Hunhow has come to regret the circumstances surrounding himself and his family - his daughter effectively abandoning him, and his son being reanimated by forces beyond his control, all while he remains underneath the seas of Uranus, effectively helpless. He advises the Stalker to let go of his own hatred of the Tenno, though to little success. The Stalker eventually leaves Uranus himself after coming to terms with  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JadeIcon272.png/31px-JadeIcon272.png?f1fb2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) [Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) and his own past, leaving Hunhow alone in the depths of the planet.

# Tech & Weaponry
Before [The Second Dream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Second_Dream "The Second Dream") events, the Sentients were considered a distant memory by most of the Origin System's population, and even though there are [Sentients that survived](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Vomvalyst "Eidolon Vomvalyst") the Old War, they were few and mostly fragments. After Sentients progressively began to be a threat to the Origin System again, their technology has started to be studied and used by many factions, including the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") themselves, a good example being the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Basmu.png/32px-Basmu.png?5c634)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Basmu) [Basmu](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Basmu), crafted with Sentient materials, and the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Phantasma.png/32px-Phantasma.png?245de)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Phantasma) [Phantasma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Phantasma). The reclusive [Quills](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quills "Quills") in particular, with the assistance of the Unum who witnessed  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GaraIcon272.png/31px-GaraIcon272.png?3956f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara) [Gara](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gara)'s final battle, have utilized resources from the [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon") to develop modular components for a [hand-mounted blaster](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amp "Amp"), which allows [select individuals](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") to channel the [energies of the Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Void_Damage "Damage/Void Damage") into more coherent forms for combat. Other weapons directly crafted by salvaging Sentient body parts such as the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Shedu.png/32px-Shedu.png?173ce)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shedu) [Shedu](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shedu) prove that it is indeed possible to use their technology against themselves, by removing their innate damage resistances. The  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Paracesis.png/32px-Paracesis.png?f1231)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis) [Paracesis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Paracesis), crafted by using [Eidolon Shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Shard "Eidolon Shard"), is considered the "Sentient slayer" due to its innate ability to channel void energy, removing their ability to adapt.

Sentient technology as a whole is organic in appearance, emphasizing the use of curves, arches, and symmetry. Even their [warships](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") are considered living beings, and are seemingly capable of reconstructing themselves. The average Sentient is formed around a central core of energy which, coupled with their independent development from the rest of the [Orokin Empire](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") and its descendants, gives them a suitably "alien" appearance compared to the native technologies of the Origin System. This design philosophy has its drawbacks, as any force capable of ripping a Sentient's "torso" and exposing the core to damage will kill the Sentient easily. Although their ability to replicate from broken components is stunted due to the Void's properties, there's at least [one example](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Broken_War "Broken War") of a functional weapon created from the remains of a [damaged one](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/War "War").

As knowledge of the Sentients has been limited for a very long time, as well as their natural ability to subvert advanced techology, very little progress has been made of developing Sentient-based technology that _isn't_ based on salvaging Sentient remains; [Cephalon Simaris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Simaris "Cephalon Simaris") has found a means to create [artificial copies](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synthetic_Eidolon_Shard "Synthetic Eidolon Shard") of [Eidolon Shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Shard "Eidolon Shard"), though they're nowhere near as potent as [actual](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brilliant_Eidolon_Shard "Brilliant Eidolon Shard") [Shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radiant_Eidolon_Shard "Radiant Eidolon Shard") when it comes to [the power they contain](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus "Focus"). Surprisingly, the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") have developed [a crude means](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Lure "Eidolon Lure") of containing Sentient energy, which is deployed to troops stationed on the outskirts of [Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus") as a means of fending off [smaller Eidolons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Vomvalyst "Eidolon Vomvalyst") at night. Regardless, the Grineer still err on the side of caution and position their base camps and outposts away from the path of [larger Eidolons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Teralyst "Eidolon Teralyst").

Concerningly, the [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") are developing their own Sentient-derived projects. One location in the [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis") holds nothing less than a largely intact [Conculyst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conculyst "Conculyst") surrounded by several smaller fragments of Sentient devices within a special containment cell, whilst a nearby testing chamber also features a firing range with a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Battacor.png/32px-Battacor.png?8b844)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Battacor) [Battacor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Battacor). Their eventual breakthroughs resulted in a [garguantan combat platform](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Profit-Taker_Orb "Profit-Taker Orb") whose shields possess the same damage adapation ability that the Sentients have, which combined with other factors makes it all but seemingly invulnerable. Sites within the [Corpus Gas City](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus_Gas_City "Corpus Gas City") under [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V")'s command have also created [Amalgams](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam "Amalgam"), hybridizing [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") and Corpus alike with Sentient technology. The Corpus's advancements are stated to "chisel weapons from their bones", implying that they are incorporating Sentient bodies to craft weapons. The most evolved Amalgams, however, can be considered part of the Sentients themselves, and will therefore attack their former faction.

# Biology
As the product of very advanced [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") technology, the way in which "Sentient life" differs from conventional organic life is unclear. They superficially appear to incorporate elements of both advanced biotechnology, cybernetics and energy manipulation, but whether they are composed of nanorobots or some kind of cell-like structures remains unknown. Sentients are [immune](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon_Phylaxis "Eidolon Phylaxis") to the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested "Infested") and can be [amalgamated](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam "Amalgam") with both [traditional](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam_Machinist "Amalgam Machinist") [human](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam_Heqet "Amalgam Heqet") [biology](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam_Alkonost "Amalgam Alkonost") and [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") [robotics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam_MOA "Amalgam MOA"), and at least one example of a [Sentient-Warframe hybrid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Caliban "Caliban") exists. Like [Warframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframes "Warframes"), Sentient [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon") are referred to as "synthetic lifeforms" and susceptible to the radiation of [Archon Shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard "Archon Shard").

The Sentients are highly adaptable beings, being able to change and morph their bodies in order to withstand whatever damage they take. Their adaptability appears fundamental to their nature and is not affected by their [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void")-induced reproductive "sterility". However, they are easily damaged by [Void energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Void_Damage "Damage/Void Damage"), which clears away whatever adaption they have gained.

This ability to adapt is not universal to every Sentient, however. Some units, like the [Brachiolysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brachiolyst "Brachiolyst") and [Tyro](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Battalyst "Battalyst") [units](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conculyst "Conculyst") do not possess it, so this ability may only show in older, more developed Sentients.

Sentients appear to (at least occasionally) reproduce via fragmentation, i.e. a broken-off piece of a larger Sentient can sometimes become a new being. In the case of [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow"), at least, these new beings remain part of his singular mind; he refers to the [Conculysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conculyst "Conculyst") and [Battalysts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Battalyst "Battalyst") that serve him as both "his fragments", "his body" and "his children". Meanwhile, the [Eidolons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eidolon "Eidolon") roaming at night outside [Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus") are known to be pieces of a much larger dead Sentient, visible in the distance on the Plains' western horizon. Fragmentation is not the only method of reproduction, however, as Hunhow is also said to have a "womb". According to Pazuul, Natah was pulled out of a "manifold" when she was born/created. Aboard [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") ships, some smaller Sentients can also be seen being 'born' from pools of writhing black biofluid.
