After the failure of the [Zanuka Project](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka_Project "Zanuka Project"), [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V") ventures off to research the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infestation "Infestation"), only to return with a [new look](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mutalist_Alad_V "Mutalist Alad V"), being exposed to the Mutalist Infested strain that he has been experimenting on. He introduces existing Corpus proxies blended with Mutalist flesh such as the [Mutalist Osprey](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mutalist_Osprey "Mutalist Osprey"), along with new Infested units like [Brood Mother](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brood_Mother "Brood Mother") and [Boilers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Boiler "Boiler"). Under the partial control of the Infested, he declares a new Mutalist Empire, boasting his research as the next evolution of humanity.

To counter this threat, the Tenno successfully purged most of these Mutalist enemies from the Origin System, reducing their danger to the [Orokin Derelicts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Derelict "Orokin Derelict") and the Infested planet [Eris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eris "Eris"). However, Alad's actions have left the system vulnerable to random [Infestation Outbreaks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invasion#Infestation_Outbreaks "Invasion"), leaving the Tenno to fend them alongside the [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") and [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") in the future.
