The [[Lotus]] intercepted information on Grineer scientist [[Tyl Regor]]'s secret cloning labs in the depths of [Uranus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Uranus "Uranus"). Deciding it was the perfect opportunity to weaken the Grineer Empire, who were already struggling to revert the effects of their [Cloning Decay Syndrome](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cloning_Decay_Syndrome "Cloning Decay Syndrome"), the Lotus instructs the Tenno to go to the [Grineer Sealabs](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Sealab "Grineer Sealab"), allowing them to choose whether to destroy Regor's research or to steal it.

[[Alad V]], a shadow of his former self, begs the Tenno to find a cure within those labs, in hopes to cure himself of the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infestation "Infestation") he was exposed to during his prior research. In response, Corpus plutocrat [[Nef Anyo]] offers to pay the Tenno to destroy the cure in spite of Alad V's traitorous actions to the [[Corpus]].

Regardless of who the Tenno sided with, Alad V eventually finds a cure, becoming one of the few survivors of the Infestation.
