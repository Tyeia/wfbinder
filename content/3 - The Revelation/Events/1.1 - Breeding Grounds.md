With the [Zanuka Project](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zanuka_Project "Zanuka Project") slowing to a halt, [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V") decided to look into the [Infestation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infestation "Infestation") for answers to his disappointing portfolio. His studies resulted in a new development of the Infestation, the Mutalist strain, having the ability to infect inorganic beings such as the robotic [MOA](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Swarm_Mutalist_MOA "Swarm Mutalist MOA"). To transmit his creation throughout the Origin System, he engineered [Infested Hives](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Infested_Hive "Infested Hive"), tumors that are capable of corrupting ships and colonies in a matter of seconds.

The Lotus learns of a massive Infested outbreak off [Eris](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eris "Eris") and sends the Tenno to destroy the Infested Hives, fearing a system-wide outbreak. Despite temporarily hindering Alad's plans, he mocks the Tenno's futile efforts on stopping the inevitable: the domination of his Mutalist empire.
