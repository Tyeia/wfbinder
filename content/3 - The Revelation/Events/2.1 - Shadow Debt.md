> The Stalker has found others as twisted as himself. He calls them his Acolytes and he's sent them after ME?!
> 
> SHHH. I have to be very quiet, they cloak, they could be hiding anywhere… listening to this.
> 
> I'm calling in my favor. Break into Corpus and Grineer installations and steal their security data. With enough data, I can determine their cloaking patterns and turn the tables on these… betrayers."

—A message from Alad V

After helping the Tenno during the events of [The Second Dream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Second_Dream "The Second Dream"), [Shadow Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadow_Stalker "Shadow Stalker")'s followers, the [Acolytes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acolytes "Acolytes"), hunt [Alad V](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Alad_V "Alad V") in vengeance. Alad V requests the Tenno to protect him as part of their deal for helping them unveil [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow")'s tomb. To triangulate the Acolytes' locations, the Tenno acquire security data from Grineer and Corpus systems through a series of [Spy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Spy "Spy") and [Mobile Defense](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mobile_Defense "Mobile Defense") missions. The hunter becomes the hunted as the Tenno pursue Acolytes across the Origin System. In the final confrontation with the Acolyte [Misery](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Misery "Misery"), the Tenno successfully fend off the assassin. Alad V is safe, for now.
