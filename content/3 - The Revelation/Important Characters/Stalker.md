The **Stalker** is an ominous, vengeful figure that infrequently appears during missions, armed with powerful weapons and abilities to hunt down [[Tenno]] who have his [Death Mark](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death_Mark "Death Mark").

The Stalker is a sapient [Warframe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warframe "Warframe") and is able to use ability powers belonging to various other Warframes. His arsenal is similar to that of the Tenno, utilizing a primary, secondary, and melee weapon. The Stalker's signature weapons are the bow  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Dread.png/32px-Dread.png?06d56)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dread) [Dread](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dread), the scythe  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Hate.png/32px-Hate.png?bbaf9)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hate) [Hate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hate), and the throwing knives  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Despair.png/32px-Despair.png?14725)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Despair) [Despair](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Despair).

# Lore
### General

Before he became the Stalker, he was once a Low Guardian named **Sorren** who served the [[Orokin]], and was engaged in an unsanctioned relationship with an individual known as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JadeIcon272.png/31px-JadeIcon272.png?f1fb2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) [Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade), with whom he sired a child, an act that the Orokin legems considered taboo. They were caught by [[4 - The Lotus' Disappearance/Important Characters/Ballas|Ballas]] who turned them into Warframes as punishment; blaming himself, the Stalker repents and served as the Orokin's devoted dog in hopes Ballas would allow him to reunite with Jade and their child.

During [The Collapse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Story_and_History#The_Collapse "Story and History") of the Orokin Empire, he noticed that the [[Tenno]] weren't as stoic and silent but instead waiting and poised, but failed to warn his masters of the slaughter the Tenno were prepared to carry against them. The Tenno went in pursuit of him as well, but he was rescued by Jade in his time of need.

However, he did not recognize Jade and believed the Orokin's downfall dashed any hopes of reuniting with his family. Therefore, the Stalker sought vengeance against the Tenno and hunted their numbers across the Origin System.

### [The Second Dream](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Second_Dream "The Second Dream")

The Stalker allies with [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]] and is bestowed the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/War.png/32px-War.png?42c4e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/War) [War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/War) greatsword while upgraded with [[Sentient]] powers, transforming into the [Shadow Stalker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shadow_Stalker "Shadow Stalker"). As they scheme to destroy the heart of the Tenno, the [[3 - The Revelation/Important Characters/Natah|Lotus]] receives assistance from [[Alad V]], but fail to stop the Shadow Stalker from revealing the Tenno's weakness: the [Reservoir](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reservoir "Reservoir") on [Lua](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lua "Lua"), hidden by the Lotus in the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void").

Upon discovering the Tenno's [true form](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator"), however, the Shadow Stalker hesitates, confused if he himself was a Tenno. He leaves, but later resolves himself to fight the Tenno on board their [Orbiter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orbiter "Orbiter"). He gains the upper hand until the Warframe destroys the greatsword, causing the Stalker to seemingly experience immense pain before vanishing.

### [Operation: Shadow Debt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Shadow_Debt "Operation: Shadow Debt")

For [[Alad V]]'s interference in The Second Dream, the Stalker sends his [Acolytes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acolytes "Acolytes") - warriors as mysterious as the Stalker himself - to hunt him. Alad V invokes his favor from the Tenno and has them repel the Acolytes.
