**Kahl-175** is a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Lancer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lancer "Lancer") introduced in [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War"). Formerly in service to the [Grineer Queens](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens") directly under [Councilor Vay Hek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Councilor_Vay_Hek "Councilor Vay Hek"), he was captured into  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconNarmer.png/32px-IconNarmer.png?1337d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer) [Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer) service.

After escaping in [Veilbreaker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veilbreaker "Veilbreaker"), Kahl recognizes that the Grineer Queens have abandoned him, but despite defecting from the Grineer Empire he now chooses to fight for all of his Grineer brothers. He allies with the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TennoIcon.png/32px-TennoIcon.png?3c7f6)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno) and [Daughter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Daughter "Daughter")  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/EntratiIcon.png/32px-EntratiIcon.png?cafef)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati) [Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entrati) in their shared goal of defeating their new enemy and rescuing any veiled - including  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconCorpusOn.png/32px-IconCorpusOn.png?9a680)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus) [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/OstronSigil.png/32px-OstronSigil.png?ba21e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron) [Ostron](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SolarisUnited1.png/32px-SolarisUnited1.png?9ee79)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United) [Solaris United](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United) - while forming  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GarrisonIcon.png/32px-GarrisonIcon.png?3d907)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl%27s_Garrison) [Kahl's Garrison](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl%27s_Garrison).
# Lore
### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War")

Kahl is one of many Grineer soldiers that were fighting against the invading [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") in the [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon") and part of [Councilor Vay Hek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Councilor_Vay_Hek "Councilor Vay Hek")'s "Eight Squad."

A [Tusk Firbolg](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tusk_Firbolg "Tusk Firbolg") crashes after being knocked out by a Sentient fighter, and Kahl picks up a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Grakata.png/32px-Grakata.png?fb846)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grakata) [Grakata](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grakata) from a dead Lancer from the crashed Firbolg. Vay Hek orders Kahl to regroup at his beacon. When he arrives at the beacon, a [Tusk Bolkor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tusk_Bolkor "Tusk Bolkor") crashes right in front of him, then a [Tusk Dargyn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dargyn_\(Enemy\) "Dargyn (Enemy)") gets shot out of the sky and crashes. Kahl goes to help the [Dargyn Pilot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dargyn_Pilot "Dargyn Pilot"), fighting past several Sentients, but the Pilot is already dead when he arrives. Kahl then asks what his mission is now.

Vay Hek tells Kahl that there is no mission, as even going near the [Condrix](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condrix "Condrix") would only get him eaten alive, but Kahl has an idea: to feed the Condrix a bomb. Vay Hek agrees with his idea, and orders Kahl to hurry and find an explosive with a high enough yield to destroy the Condrix. Kahl heads to a mining camp to retrieve the "Big Bomb," when he spots Sentient Searches, and successfully sneaks past them. He then enters a tent where the bomb is, picks it up and hesitates, then decides to sacrifice himself for the [Queens](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens"). He exits the tent and is immediately blown away by an exploding [Tusk Thumper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tusk_Thumper "Tusk Thumper").

Kahl wakes up and takes off his mask, only to spot a Sentient killing nearby Grineer Lancers. He quickly looks around and notices a fallen  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/RhinoIcon272.png/31px-RhinoIcon272.png?8aabe)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rhino) [Rhino](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rhino), crawls up to the body and grabs his weapon, shooting and killing the Sentient just in time. He stands up, spits to the side and pumps his newly acquired  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CorinthPrime.png/32px-CorinthPrime.png?5c1bb)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corinth_Prime) [Corinth Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corinth_Prime), and heads to the Condrix to "feed" it the explosive. He reaches the Condrix, collapses from exhaustion, and then activates the bomb. As he begins to declare his speech for the Queens, he abruptly pauses and instead declares for his brothers.

Kahl is awoken by a red light, and realizes that the bomb didn't detonate and he is somehow still alive. He looks up, horrified, as [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") stands in front of him, telling him to not be afraid.
### [Veilbreaker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veilbreaker)
> [!quote] Kahl-175
> Who help Grineer today? Not Queens. Not Vay Hek. Only Tenno and Blue Girl help brothers. Other Brothers still have Veil. So Kahl break. This camp little. But soon, it get big. You see. Kahl not afraid. And now Kahl not alone.

Kahl was revealed to have survived, forced into  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconNarmer.png/32px-IconNarmer.png?1337d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer) [Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer) service. However, he breaks from control of his [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil") in a [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") over [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos") and, remembering everything, decides to resume his fight. He sends out a distress beacon which is picked up by [Daughter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Daughter "Daughter"), who then relays the signal to the Tenno in a response effort, only to be shocked to find out that its owner is a "vat rat" Grineer. Referred to by Kahl as "Blue Girl", she reluctantly guides him to free his brothers and any other veiled (be they [Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus"), [Ostron](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ostron "Ostron"), or [Solaris United](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Solaris_United "Solaris United")) in exchange for information on Narmer's new leader [Pazuul](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pazuul "Pazuul"), and the heroic Lancer ultimately escapes aboard a Tenno [Railjack](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Railjack "Railjack").

Kahl reveals that he no longer fights for the [Grineer Queens](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer_Queens "Grineer Queens") or [Councilor Vay Hek](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Councilor_Vay_Hek "Councilor Vay Hek"), as they had no involvement in his escape and believes they had abandoned him. Taking refuge in the [Drifter's Camp](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter%27s_Camp "Drifter's Camp"), Kahl allies with the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") - being the ones who responded to his distress signal despite his being Grineer - and forms [Kahl's Garrison](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl%27s_Garrison "Kahl's Garrison"). As Kahl fights against Narmer, he fashions himself with a self-made Veilbreaker device created out of an EMP ram to forcibly remove veils without killing the enthralled.
