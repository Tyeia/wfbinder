**Kira** was once a passenger aboard the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero"). She was the former fifth member of [The Holdfasts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Holdfasts "The Holdfasts"); Void manifestations of the crew aboard the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero"). She transformed into a [Void Angel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel") after succumbing to their song during the [[Zariman ARG]]
# Lore & History
### [[Zariman ARG]]

Kira featured prominently in the **Zariman ARG** event, as she is one of the Holdfasts who took the risk of attempting to recover logs made by her husband describing the events surrounding the process of her going insane from [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") exposure and subsequent death. She regrettably suffered the consequences and succumbed to the song, which transformed her into the [Void Angel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel").

### [Angels of the Zariman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Angels_of_the_Zariman "Angels of the Zariman")

After fully succumbing to the influence of the Void, Kira reappears as a fully fledged Void Angel. First seen roosting in the ceiling of the Zariman, Kira stuns the Tenno, leading to the rest of the Holdfasts to rescue them. After repairing the Zariman's Exolizers, Kira attempts to make her way to the the [Reliquary Drive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reliquary_Drive "Reliquary Drive") and feed off of it. However, she is ultimately slain by the Tenno.
