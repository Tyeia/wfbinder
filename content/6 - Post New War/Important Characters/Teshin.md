# Lore
### [Angels of the Zariman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Angels_of_the_Zariman "Angels of the Zariman")

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Teshin%27s_Swords_in_the_Dormizone.jpg/300px-Teshin%27s_Swords_in_the_Dormizone.jpg?74b45)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:Teshin%27s_Swords_in_the_Dormizone.jpg)

Teshin's swords propped up against the Dormizone's door to Duviri wreathed in Void-like unlight.

When entering the [Dormizone](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dormizone "Dormizone") as the [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter"), Teshin's swords would mysteriously manifest for a time until [Update 32.3](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_32#Update_32.3 "Update 32") (2023-02-15), leaned up against a closed door and infused with grey Void-like energy, serving as an early nod to the Teshin of [The Duviri Paradox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Duviri_Paradox "The Duviri Paradox"). This door would later open up in [Update 33.0](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_33#Update_33.0 "Update 33") (2023-04-26) to be a gateway into the realm of [Duviri](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duviri "Duviri"), into [Teshin's Cave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Teshin%27s_Cave "Teshin's Cave").

### [The Duviri Paradox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Duviri_Paradox "The Duviri Paradox")

Within the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"), it is revealed that Teshin was pulled into the portal during [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War") after Erra crushed his helmet, along with his [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil"), which sits tarnishing, unused, on a Narmer banner in his cave. As he fell through the sky, he crashed into the lake on the island now known as **Hermit's Landing**. Teshin was then dragged back to shore and healed by [Mathila](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mathila "Mathila").

He appears in [Duviri](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duviri "Duviri") as an old, wizened, and retired Dax carrying around a walking stick, and owns a pet [Rablit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rablit "Rablit") which the [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") named Sol, Terra, or Lua. When the Drifter arrives in [Teshin's Cave](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Teshin%27s_Cave "Teshin's Cave"), Teshin instructs them on using Warframes received from outside the walls of the Void while offering his swords  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Sun%26Moon.png/32px-Sun%26Moon.png?58a53)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sun_%26_Moon) [Sun & Moon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sun_%26_Moon).

Meanwhile, the mad ruler [Dominus Thrax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dominus_Thrax "Dominus Thrax") grows increasingly agitated with the Drifter's defiance. He eventually locates and captures Teshin, leaves behind an imposter who is later killed by the Rablit, and has him executed. Teshin dies before the Drifter, requesting they take care of his pet and acknowledges their growth.

The Drifter confronts Dominus Thrax and, after learning that the Drifter was the real master of Duviri all along, uses the throne's/drifter's time-rewinding ability to resurrect Teshin, but returns Thrax to power which they deem to be a fair trade.
