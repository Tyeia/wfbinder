# Lore
### [The Reaper's Lament](https://www.warframe.com/news/the-reapers-lament)

Hunhow converses with the [[6 - Post New War/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]], revealing regrets with [his](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Natah "Natah") [family](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") and urging him to make amends with the Tenno. He also urges the Stalker to wield the scythe once more (as unlike  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/War.png/32px-War.png?42c4e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/War) [War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/War), which is solely a weapon meant to kill, the scythe can be used as a tool of cultivation), and let go of the past, as the [[Orokin]] were not worthy of the Stalker's loyalty. Hunhow adds that he saved a  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/YareliIcon272.png/31px-YareliIcon272.png?0b164)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli) [Yareli](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli) (labeling it as an "insult of rescue") and granting her the ability to [adapt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Yareli_Pandea_Skin "Yareli Pandea Skin"), yet her spirit remained unbroken. When the Stalker refuses, Hunhow warns that he will always have a target painted on his back and that his "actions will have consequences".

### [Jade Shadows](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Shadows "Jade Shadows")

[[6 - Post New War/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]] attempts to nurse his savior during [The Collapse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Collapse "The Collapse"),  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JadeIcon272.png/31px-JadeIcon272.png?f1fb2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) [Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade), to no avail. Hunhow urges Stalker to seek the [[Tenno]]'s help, though the Stalker initially disregards this advice, until eventually going to the Tenno after another failure to heal Jade.

In the aftermath of the quest, Hunhow informs the Tenno that the Stalker has fled with Jade's newborn and will not return. He entrusts them with the blueprint of the Jade Warframe, believing that while the Stalker may not have wished this, she would.
