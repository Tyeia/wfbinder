During [The Collapse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Collapse "The Collapse"), [[6 - Post New War/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]] served as a Low Guardian to the [[Orokin]] who were slaughtered by the [[Tenno]], to which the Stalker sought vengeance. As Stalker himself became a target, he was protected by  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JadeIcon272.png/31px-JadeIcon272.png?f1fb2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) [Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade).

In the present, following the events of [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War"), Stalker has been nursing Jade who has fallen critically ill. His attempts to administer  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Bioplasma.png/32px-Bioplasma.png?9e871)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bioplasma) [Bioplasma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Bioplasma) fail; [[6 - Post New War/Important Characters/Hunhow|Hunhow]] urges him to seek the Tenno's assistance, who suggests he use  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/Cortichrome.png/32px-Cortichrome.png?42972)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cortichrome) [Cortichrome](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cortichrome) from the [[Infested]]. Meanwhile, the Bioplasma theft alerts [[Corpus]] Captain [Xeto](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xeto "Xeto"); she orders the Stalker tracked, believing what he has in his possession will promote her to a [Sister of Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos "Sisters of Parvos").

While administering the Cortichrome, Jade is revealed to be pregnant and passes during childbirth. Stalker secures her child and protects it from Xeto's forces. When the Stalker sustains an injury, the child cries, causing Xeto to order a cease fire and allow Stalker to pass.

Before becoming Warframes, Stalker was known as Sorren and was engaged with Jade, siring a child despite being forbidden by Orokin legems. He names the child Sirius or Orion.
