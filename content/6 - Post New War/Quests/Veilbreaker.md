> Daughter Entrati gets more than she bargained for when she enlists the Tenno to investigate a call for reinforcements aboard a Narmer Murex.

On a [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") above [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"), [Kahl-175](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl-175 "Kahl-175") removes his [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil"). His distress signal is picked up by [Daughter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Daughter "Daughter"), who lends her support in exchange for information on Narmer's new leader [Pazuul](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pazuul "Pazuul"). After freeing captives and escaping, Kahl sets up a [base of operations](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl%27s_Garrison "Kahl's Garrison") at the [Drifter's Camp](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter%27s_Camp "Drifter's Camp"). His [campaign](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Break_Narmer "Break Narmer") to free his brothers is only just beginning.
