Shortly after  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/JadeIcon272.png/31px-JadeIcon272.png?f1fb2)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade) [Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade)'s death in [Jade Shadows](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade_Shadows "Jade Shadows"), [Parvos Granum](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parvos_Granum "Parvos Granum") and his forces besiege [[6 - Post New War/Important Characters/Stalker|Stalker]]'s now-abandoned hideout, attempting to harvest the remnants of Jade's energies as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/VolatileMotes.png/32px-VolatileMotes.png?8b0ee)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operation:_Belly_of_the_Beast) Volatile Motes to refine Granum's [Jade Light Eximus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eximus#Jade_Light "Eximus") project. Both the Tenno and [[Ordis]] stand in his way, hijacking Granum's operations and disposing the collected motes into the sun, with Stalker, [now empowered by the love between him and Jade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protector_Stalker "Protector Stalker"), potentially assisting his enemies in defending his lover's resting place. Along the way, additional Mote stores across the Origin System have made themselves known, giving the Tenno alternative means of collecting Motes in exchange for fighting scores of Jade Light-empowered troops.
