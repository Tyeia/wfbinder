 **[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconNarmer.png/32px-IconNarmer.png?1337d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer) Narmer** is a [Faction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Faction "Faction") introduced in [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War") quest, ruled by [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") after [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)")'s  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/SentientFactionIcon.png/32px-SentientFactionIcon.png?19c23)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) [Sentients](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient) took over the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System") by brainwashing its inhabitants into blind subservience. Individuals under Narmer control are identified by golden [Narmer Veils](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil") masking their faces, which produce a red hallucinogenic mist that brainwashes its victims, leading them to repeatedly chant "All as one."

All [Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Narmer), regardless of their former faction, take increased damage from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgSlashSmall64.png/32px-DmgSlashSmall64.png?bab47)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) [Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgToxinSmall64.png/32px-DmgToxinSmall64.png?8dc5f)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) [Toxin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage), but resist  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgMagneticSmall64.png/32px-DmgMagneticSmall64.png?80be3)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) [Magnetic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage).

# Overview
While the concept of Narmer is almost omnipresent throughout the Origin System, thanks to the actions of Erra and Ballas, its core definition remains shrouded in obfuscation. Narmer could be the name of the new Sentient Empire, but there are elements of its structure that seem more in line with a religious movement, such as the [Narmer Deacons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Deacon "Narmer Deacon") and [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon"). The members of Narmer treat the subject with extreme reverence, and refer to Narmer as either a philosophy, religion, empire, or all three at once.

### Beliefs

Narmer as a whole views Tau as a sacred place. [Pazuul](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pazuul "Pazuul")'s transmissions and his [propaganda](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pazuul/Quotes "Pazuul/Quotes") sermons during [Break Narmer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Break_Narmer "Break Narmer") missions make reference to it as a sort of paradise, where he intends to lead his followers once they have been "purified". He references the "Lords of Tau" as the beings that currently rule over it, and declares himself their "minister".

# Technology
Narmer appears to use a marriage of both [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") and [Orokin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin "Orokin") technology. Supplies, weapons, equipment, and even the dropships used by Narmer forces are all adorned with red Sentient-themed holograms and golden accents. Their more advanced equipment, such as their propaganda drones and the weapons used by the [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon"), display a more Sentient-like nature, reflected by their organic curves and flesh-like innards.

Narmer also employs the use of large, circular, mirror-like constructs called **All-Seeing Eyes**. These constructs can be found in highly populated areas, such as [Cetus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cetus "Cetus") and [Fortuna](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fortuna "Fortuna"), and may be used in facilitating the control of Narmer Veils. They appear to be filled with a red substance (perhaps [Kuva](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva "Kuva"), or a liquid form of the hallucinogen they emit). Miniaturized versions of the Eyes are built into the Veils themselves.

# Lore & History
### [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War)

> **Erra:** What does it matter? We-- you have won. Narmer spreads across the system. Monuments rise to your glory. All who live, bios and not, kneel before you--  
   **Ballas:** --but not [THEM](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno")! You see some great triumph here? That this dullard herd worships me? Wailing 'freedom' from their rotten gobs when the truth is... they all thirst for that piss-warm comfort of being told what to think! They wanted to kneel...

—[Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") fails to placate [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas"), who describes Narmer as a hollow victory

Despite the efforts of the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno")-[Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer")-[Corpus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corpus "Corpus") alliance to repel the [Sentient](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient "Sentient") invasion, [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") banishes both the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") and the [Lotus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lotus "Lotus") into a [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") portal. Without their presence, the Sentients led by [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") crush the alliance and are easily able to take over the [Origin System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Origin_System "Origin System"). They install [Narmer Veils](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil") onto subjects to brainwash them and subjugate loyalty to Narmer while defaming the Tenno, with [Narmer Deacons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Deacon "Narmer Deacon") constantly keeping watch and are able to remotely detonate the Veils and kill its wearer should they detect any resistance. Furthermore, Erra's [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon"), known as [Nira](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Nira "Archon Nira"), [Boreal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Boreal "Archon Boreal"), and [Amar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Amar "Archon Amar"), served to instill fear in dissidents and ensure loyalty throughout the system. However, a few individuals such as [Little Duck](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Little_Duck "Little Duck") and [Nora Night](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nora_Night "Nora Night") have managed to evade capture.

After an uneasy alliance between the [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") and [Hunhow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunhow "Hunhow") to save the Operator and the Lotus and the subsequent deaths of two Archons to achieve this goal, the empire began to disintegrate as Ballas recalled all fleets to the Sentient mothership [Praghasa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praghasa "Praghasa") to devour the sun, enraged at the Tenno's return and scheming to sacrifice the Origin System and his Narmer empire to escape to the [Tau System](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau_System "Tau System"). Erra, realizing Ballas also intends to doom the Sentients, defects and sacrifices himself to guide the Tenno to the third and final Archon hoping it may fully restore the Lotus. After the final confrontation between the Drifter/Operator and the Lotus against Ballas and his subsequent death, the Narmer Empire became heavily destabilized with their leader effectively becoming a martyr and creating a power vacuum that the loyalists scramble to fill, allowing other factions of the Origin System to slowly recover.

Following The New War, remnants of Narmer remain active in the post-war ravaged [Plains of Eidolon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Plains_of_Eidolon "Plains of Eidolon") and [Orb Vallis](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orb_Vallis "Orb Vallis"), with both [Konzu](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Konzu "Konzu") and [Eudico](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eudico "Eudico") suggesting that someone else is pulling the strings.

### [Veilbreaker](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Veilbreaker "Veilbreaker")

A [Murex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murex "Murex") emerges near [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos") revealing that Narmer continues their machinations of enslaving the Origin System. The [Archons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon "Archon") have been revived, with [Erra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Erra_\(Character\) "Erra (Character)") himself being transformed into one and now serves as their leader under the name [Pazuul](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pazuul "Pazuul").

[Kahl-175](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl-175 "Kahl-175"), a [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer "Grineer") soldier that fought in The New War, breaks free from his [Narmer Veil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narmer_Veil "Narmer Veil"). He fights alongside the Tenno to free his brothers from Narmer oppression and forms  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GarrisonIcon.png/32px-GarrisonIcon.png?3d907)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl%27s_Garrison) [Kahl's Garrison](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kahl%27s_Garrison) resistance movement.

### [Archon Hunts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Hunt "Archon Hunt")

Kahl, [Daughter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Daughter "Daughter"), and [Chipper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chipper "Chipper") provide the Tenno with information on the Archon's locations, prompting the Tenno to go out and hunt them down. During these missions, the Lotus discovers that Pazuul is harvesting massive amounts of resources for some unknown creation.
