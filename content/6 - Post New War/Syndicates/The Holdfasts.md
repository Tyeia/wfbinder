> Granted a second existence through the mysterious power of the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"), the four Holdfasts struggle to keep their sanity and their vessel intact. The return of the Tenno may turn the tide.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TheHoldfastsIcon.png/32px-TheHoldfastsIcon.png?8bad1)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Holdfasts) The Holdfasts are a [Syndicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate "Syndicate") comprised of the four last "survivors" of the adult crew of the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero") and conducting their operations within the [Chrysalith](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chrysalith "Chrysalith"). Returned to life by the mysterious powers of the [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void"), they have fought off conversion into [Void Angels](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel"). While not strictly human anymore, instead being Void manifestations resurrected through the [Conceptual Embodiment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Conceptual_Embodiment "Conceptual Embodiment") of the memories of the original Zariman Crew, they retain their human mindset and guide the [Tenno](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tenno "Tenno") in preventing the further degradation of the Zariman.

### Other

#### Kira

_Main article: [Kira](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kira "Kira")_

**Kira** was formerly part of the Holdfasts of the Zariman, until she succumbed to the Song and transformed into a [Void Angel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel") seen by the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator")/[Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") before they woke up in their [Dormizone](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dormizone "Dormizone") during the early part of the Angels of the Zariman quest line. After her defeat in the last quest, the Holdfasts made an altar to her memory at the western part of the Chrysalith's main lobby.

Kira featured prominently in the [Zariman Alternate Reality Game](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_ARG "Zariman ARG") as she asked help from the players to recover logs of her husband describing the events of her death, which also detailed several events prior to the Operator's/Drifter's return during the [Angels of the Zariman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Angels_of_the_Zariman "Angels of the Zariman") quest.

[![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/CephalonMelica.png/180px-CephalonMelica.png?1fed8)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:CephalonMelica.png)

#### Cephalon Melica

_Main article: [Cephalon Melica](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon_Melica "Cephalon Melica")_

**Melica** is a [Cephalon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cephalon "Cephalon") that was tasked with instructing the children aboard the Zariman. One of her consoles is found at the pathway just after the Chrysalith's main entrance and before Quinn's position, although she cannot be directly interacted with. She requests "students" to find [Zarium Accolades](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zarium_Accolade "Zarium Accolade") for her in the Zariman.

#### The Husband

_Main article: [The Husband](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Husband "The Husband")_

**The Husband** is an unnamed character only known as [Kira](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kira "Kira")'s husband through the Zariman logs recovered by her during [Zariman ARG](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_ARG "Zariman ARG"). He was one of the survivors of the early events following the Void-Jump Incident before succumbing to the Void.

#### Gyra

_Main article: [Gyre/Main#Lore](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gyre/Main#Lore "Gyre/Main")_

**Gyra** was a passenger notable for her graceful movements and was sent aboard by Orokin Executor [Ballas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ballas "Ballas") to act as a figurehead, but eventually became a victim herself of the Void-Jump accident. She is the likely basis for the Warframe  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/GyreIcon272.png/31px-GyreIcon272.png?4c4ae)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gyre) [Gyre](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gyre).
