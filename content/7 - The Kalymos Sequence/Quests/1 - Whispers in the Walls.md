Due to [[Ballas]]'s attempts to open [Void](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void "Void") portals on an unprecedented scale during [The New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War "The New War"), the [Indifference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_the_Wall "The Man in the Wall") grows increasingly restless. In response, [Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid "Loid") awakens "The Kalymos Sequence" precept and urges the [[Tenno]] to [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos "Deimos"). Behind the [[Entrati]] family's back, Loid guides them to [Albrecht's Laboratories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht%27s_Laboratories "Albrecht's Laboratories") hidden beneath the [Necralisk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Necralisk "Necralisk"), revealing that [[Albrecht Entrati]] has been engaged in an ongoing battle with the Indifference's forces: [[The Murmur]].

After awakening the Sleeper, the [original human Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid_\(Original\) "Loid (Original)"), and meeting with the [[Cavia]], Albrecht's animal experiments that speak only [Voidtongue](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Voidtongue "Voidtongue"), the Tenno interact with Albrecht's incomplete [Vessels](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vessels "Vessels") project and discover that Albrecht Entrati fled to the year [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999") to escape the Indifference. Meanwhile, Loid becomes increasingly aggravated as the Murmur escalates their attacks.

Tracking down Albrecht's messages, the Tenno are able to produce the Jahu Gargoyle that auto-translates the Cavia's Voidtongue, who reveal one final message from Albrecht that Loid threw away. The message reveals Albrecht's praise for Loid's devotion, displaying an emotion of love that Loid long since craved which pacifies the Indifference. With renewed determination, Loid makes preparations to send the Tenno to 1999 and find Albrecht.
