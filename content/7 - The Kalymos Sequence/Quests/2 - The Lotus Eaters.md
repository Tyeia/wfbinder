[[Lotus]]

> A strange call comes from beyond and must not go unanswered.

At [Sanctum Anatomica](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sanctum_Anatomica "Sanctum Anatomica"), the Lotus warns the [Operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Operator "Operator") that she is influenced by [The Indifference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Indifference "The Indifference") and requires their [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") paradox to return to the past to find [Albrecht Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati") before The Man in the Wall does. Meanwhile, [Loid](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loid "Loid") is preparing Albrecht's [Vessel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vessel "Vessel") for the Drifter to travel through to find Albrecht in [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania") in [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999").
