[[2.9 - 1999 ARG]]
[[The Hex]]

> The desperate hunt for Albrecht Entrati has led the Drifter to the city of Höllvania on December 31st, 1999. Despite the Scaldra lockdown in the face of the Techrot menace, the citizens do their best to celebrate. But this has all happened before... and ended in disaster.

On New Years 2000, [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania")'s nuclear reactor goes up in a mushroom cloud due to the Y2K Problem, vaporizing the city.

24 hours before the explosion, a group of [Protoframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protoframe "Protoframe") called  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HexIcon.png/32px-HexIcon.png?c8c7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) [The Hex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) - consisting of members [Arthur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arthur "Arthur"), [Aoi](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aoi "Aoi"), [Amir](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amir "Amir"), [Quincy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quincy "Quincy"), [Leticia](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Leticia "Leticia"), and [Eleanor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eleanor "Eleanor") - are searching for [Dr. Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati"), battling the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ScaldraIcon.png/32px-ScaldraIcon.png?55f29)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scaldra) [[Scaldra armed forces and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TechrotIcon.png/32px-TechrotIcon.png?5b58e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot) [[Techrot]] Infestation. The [Drifter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Drifter "Drifter") arrives in Höllvania through a [Helminth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth "Helminth") mouth with an  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ExcaliburIcon272.png/31px-ExcaliburIcon272.png?3bcf7)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) [Excalibur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur) Warframe, who uses Transference on Arthur to deliver him visions of the future, but is forcefully ejected and captured. After interrogating the Drifter, Arthur decides to chance on their story and join forces under a common goal.

Meanwhile, Dr. Entrati has been captured by the Scaldra's commanding forces [Major Neci Rusalka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Major_Neci_Rusalka "Major Neci Rusalka") and [Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lieutenant_Viktor_Vodyanoi "Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi"). The former is revealed to be possessed by the [Indifference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Indifference "Indifference").

While making attempts to find Entrati and stop the nuke, the Drifter encounters [Kalymos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kalymos "Kalymos"), who leads them to a recording from Entrati. He warns they are trapped in an altered time loop and in order to escape they require a rare spark of energy: the nuclear explosion. Thus, they would have to prevent the Hex from stopping the detonation.

However, the Hex learn of the Scaldra escorting a VIP and move to intercept, while Rusalka attempts to intervene. Entrati urges the Drifter to kill her to prevent the Indifference from further influencing the time loop, but they refuse. The Indifference assumes their Operator form and whisks the three away to the [Zariman Ten Zero](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zariman_Ten_Zero "Zariman Ten Zero"), showing the Drifter that the Hex will die.

Minutes before the explosion, the Hex make their move on the nuclear reactor. Eleanor attempts to gain control the Techrot, but succumbs to the Infested hivemind and attacks Leticia. Quincy is killed while trying to hold off the Scaldra, Aoi dies from overexertion in an attempt to secure the reactor, Amir is fatally shot by Entrati, and Arthur succumbs to the nuclear reactor's radiation poisoning. Entrati leaves for [Tau](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tau "Tau"), but the Drifter chooses to stay behind to save the Hex. Entrati warns that they cannot save the Hex unless the Drifter forms a personal bond with them.

The Drifter uses their [Duviri](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duviri "Duviri") powers to rewind time and create a new loop, starting at the beginning of the year of 1999.

After building up relationships with the rest of the Hex, the Drifter is able to intervene in the events in the reactor on New Years Eve, preventing the deaths of all of the Hex, and successfully defusing the threat, completing the Kalymos Sequence.
