> Formerly human operatives, the six members of the Hex were transformed into Protoframes by Albrecht Entrati. Filled with uncanny power and mourning their lost humanity, they continue their mission of mercy in beleaguered Höllvania.

 [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HexIcon.png/32px-HexIcon.png?c8c7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) The Hex are a [Syndicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate "Syndicate") comprised of the [Protoframes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Protoframe "Protoframe"), humans who exhibit Warframe traits through [Dr. Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati")'s experiments. They operate out of the [Höllvania Central Mall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania_Central_Mall "Höllvania Central Mall"), wrestling [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania") out of the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/ScaldraIcon.png/32px-ScaldraIcon.png?55f29)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scaldra) [Scaldra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scaldra) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TechrotIcon.png/32px-TechrotIcon.png?5b58e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot) [Techrot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot)'s control while in search of the missing doctor.
