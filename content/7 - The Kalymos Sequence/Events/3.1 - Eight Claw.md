After the events of [The Hex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Quest\) "The Hex (Quest)"), [The Indifference](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Indifference "The Indifference") has retreated from [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999"), taking [Major Neci Rusalka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Major_Neci_Rusalka "Major Neci Rusalka") with it. They turn their attention to [Duviri](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duviri "Duviri") and overthrow [Dominus Thrax](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dominus_Thrax "Dominus Thrax"), who is forced to turn to the Tenno for aid against the usurper.
