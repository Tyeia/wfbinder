> **Your attention, please.**
> 
> The Murmur threat demands immediate action. Our first order of business must be surveillance. An early detection system lest we be caught sans trousers.
> 
> You would agree that a Vigile Jahu Gargoyle installed within each Tenno Dojo is the obvious solution. Not a whisper could be uttered in the system without the connected Dojos overhearing it first.
> 
> Your compliance is appreciated. The Vigile Jahu Gargoyle awaits you within your Dojo - simply place it as a Decoration. The enemy does not hesitate. We must do no less.

—Fibonacci

To prevent the [Murmur](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Murmur "Murmur")'s encroachment into reality, [Fibonacci](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fibonacci "Fibonacci") requests the Tenno [Clans](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Clan "Clan") to each build a Vigile [Jahu Gargoyle](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jahu_Gargoyle "Jahu Gargoyle") to create an Origin System-wide surveillance system to detect any Void breaches. However, a last-minute recalibration necessitates the Tenno to hunt down [The Fragmented](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Fragmented "The Fragmented") and collect their Curses to ensure the system can warn of both incoming and outgoing threats. Further complicating matters are the presence of [Void Angels](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Angel "Void Angel") in [Albrecht's Laboratories](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht%27s_Laboratories "Albrecht's Laboratories"), who will consume any Curses before the Tenno can return with them. Both The Fragmented and the Void Angels must be defeated before extraction in order to obtain these Curses.
