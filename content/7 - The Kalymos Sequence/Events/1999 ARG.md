The **1999 ARG** is an [Alternate Reality Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternate_Reality_Game "wikipedia:Alternate Reality Game") (ARG) that began during [TennoCon 2024](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/TennoCon/2024 "TennoCon/2024"). It was used to build up the release of [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999") and introduce the story around [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania"), alongside key players such as  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/HexIcon.png/32px-HexIcon.png?c8c7d)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) [The Hex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Syndicate\)) and [Albrecht Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati").
# Discovery
The ARG officially began on October 25, 2024 with the appearance of a commercial at the end of [Devstream 182](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Devstream_182 "Devstream 182"). At 1:48:27 in [the broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/live/wKxj68CxWm0?si=2kpZS09Wc7UsZ_aB&t=6507), footage of various aspects of 1999 and [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania") were shown. Scattered throughout the transmission were various groups of letters, and at the end of the transmission, the text **???.?????????????.com** was displayed.

By piecing together those letters, the site **www.doktorentrati.com** was revealed. Players who followed the link discovered the **Seek The Signal** subpage, containing the eye that adorns many pieces of 1999 technology with the following written beneath:

# Suspicious Correspondence
The **Suspicious Correspondence** page (officially titled "Suspicious correspondence flagged for review"), known post-Devstream 182 as the **Classified Archive: Inflammatory Evidence**, is [a hidden page](https://www.warframe.com/1999/doktorentrati) on the official Warframe website, which mimics a Pom-2 that hosts fictional archived chat messages between several unknown parties during [the late 90s](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999"), discussing the state of affairs during that period of time, as well as the arrival of one [Dr. Entrati](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Albrecht_Entrati "Albrecht Entrati"). Additional files that can be uncovered in the page also reveal the story of a Sam Paige, who ends up trapped within what she was told was an office of Digital Extremes.

The first set of chat logs were gradually updated prior to [TennoCon 2024](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/TennoCon/2024 "TennoCon/2024"), while the password-protected files were added after [Devstream 182](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Devstream_182 "Devstream 182").

### 02_Cheat.kin

To get this file, you need to go to **KINEMANTIC-ONLINE.APP** (magnifying glass) to open **ASCLOID** (search engine) and look for _cracking encrypted zip files_. You get the **DE/CRYPTR.APP**, which you can use on the **Oubliette** encrypted folder. To correctly decrypt it, click the locks in the correct sequence, which is 2-1-4-3.

The password for this file is hidden in the [twitter advertisement](https://twitter.com/PlayWarframe/status/1850981710030418109) for War Sector. The paragraph in blue, with the faded _3, 5, 7..._ next to it, is the hint. This sequence is the [prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_numbers "wikipedia:Prime numbers"), which other text in the ad hints towards. Getting all the prime numbered words in the paragraph, you get the phrase _combine all the prime words together and then multiply by nine_. There is a total of 31 words in that paragraph, meaning you need the sum of all the prime numbers up to 31, which is 160. Times that by 9, you get the password, which is **1440**.

### 05_Bite.kin

To get this file, you need to go to **Documents**. There are a couple images and .kin files there. By noting down the outer letters of each word, starting from the top-middle and going clockwise, you get the password: **CALLBACK**.

### 06_Contact.kin

To get this file, you need to open the Hardware Manager by clicking the two-rectangle icon at the bottom of the screen. This will pull up a window showcasing four corrupted hard drives, three of which have their drive name partially obscured with Zalgo text, while the fourth has its name reduced to gibberish. The drive names for the first three drives are **Cerulean**, **Ochre**, and **Vermillion**. Each drive can be repaired by clicking the Repair button, and specifying which drive to repair via the following command:

chkdsk \[drivename] /r

The first three drives, while repairable, remain inaccessible, but the progress bar on each repair command is the clue for the fourth drive: Cerulean's progress contains an image of [Earth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Earth "Earth"), Ochre's progress contains an image of [Venus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venus "Venus"), and Vermillion's progress contains an image of [Mars](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mars "Mars"). By this logic, the fourth drive is related to [Mercury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mercury "Mercury"), and based on the names of the previous drives, we get "**Argent**", reflecting Mercury's [real-world colors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_\(planet\) "wikipedia:Mercury (planet)") rather than the brown tones seen in game. Once Argent is repaired, we can recover 06_Contact.kin from the drive.

The password for this file is hidden in [a video posted on Warframe's Official Twitter account](https://x.com/PlayWarframe/status/1856432399560913031), showcasing footage of someone, presumably Sam Paige, frantically running around Digital Extremes' empty offices and occasionally putting up paper with Höllvanian script written on them. The first piece of paper, shown around 10 seconds into the video, reads "Techrot", while the second piece of paper, briefly seen 22 seconds in the video before getting covered up in more paper, reads "**Firebreak**", the password to unlock the sixth file.

# Seek the Signal
> Good. The stage is set, as it were, but the signal must be spread. I will share more details in a manner similarly encoded as my messages up to this point.
> 
> Once you have the proper order, enter it here. I have secured, appropriately enough, a most "viral" medium to carry the signal. Yet you will need to work in tandem to boost its reach. Find as many like-minded individuals as you can to enter the code.
> 
> Do not fail.

The page can be accessed by [entering its address directly](https://www.warframe.com/1999/doktorentrati) or by accessing [1999's official update page](https://www.warframe.com/1999), scrolling down to the **"Featured Pages"** section and clicking on the **"doktorentrati.com"** thumbnail.

Prior to [Update 38](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_38 "Update 38")'s release on December 13, 2024 it could also be accessed through the [home page of the official Warframe site](https://www.warframe.com/) by clicking on the game's logo in the top left corner or on the main page view as soon as it changed its appearance to the "glitched" icon with Lidless Eye symbol).

Seek The Signal has two versions, depending on whether the user is logged into a Warframe account while accessing the page:

- **For logged-out users**, it will display two button prompts: "**Log In**" and "**Begin**". "Log In" will redirect the user to the standard login page, then back to Seek The Signal. "Begin" will redirect the user to [Suspicious Correspondence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Correspondence "Suspicious Correspondence") ARG page.
- **For logged-in users**, the "Log In" button will be replaced with new prompt: "**End**". Depending on specific actions performed by the user in-game (prior to update's release) or on the [official 1999 promotional site](https://www.warframe.com/1999) (after update release) and in the [Suspicious Correspondence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Correspondence "Suspicious Correspondence") ARG, the button will be **greyed out and inactive**, or **black and active**. Clicking on the **active button** will redirect the user to the **Spread The Signal ARG** page.
### Enabling the "End" button - post-Update 38.0

The user must complete [Suspicious Correspondence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Correspondence "Suspicious Correspondence") ARG up to the point of unlocking the last file - **06_Contact.kin** - and triggering of the "dead drop" on the [City Wide Metro](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/City_Wide_Metro "City Wide Metro") ARG page. After entering the Metro page and interacting with the mobile phone on the left seat, the user must head to the [1999 promo page](https://www.warframe.com/1999) and scroll down to the **"Featured Pages"** section at the bottom.

An interactive object in the form of a hanging landline phone playing a call failure tone will be attached to the frame of the bottom-right thumbnail (**"doktorentrati.com"**) - clicking on it will hang up the phone and enable the next part of the puzzle.

The user must then access the [Suspicious Correspondence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Correspondence "Suspicious Correspondence") site, activate **Bandbooster.app** from the bottom toolbar (click on the icon with a long rectangle and a branching symbol) and click "Connect". After a moment of waiting (_"Dialing... attempting to connect"_), the app will display the following message:
>Connection Successful!
> 
> You must end where you began...

**Note:** it is recommended to keep the browser tab/window with Suspicious Correspondence active while Bandbooster.app is "connecting" - if the site switches to screensaver mode, connection process will automatically fail and require a new attempt.

After returning to the [main page of the ARG](https://www.warframe.com/1999/doktorentrati), the "End" button will become active, allowing access to Spread The Signal.
## Spread The Signal
> THE SIGNAL MUST BE SPREAD

Upon entering the main site of the ARG, the user is presented with a number of CRT monitors displaying a message from a mysterious sender, several repeats of the quotes "SPREAD THE SIGNAL" and "THE SIGNAL MUST BE SPREAD", a glitched "Compiling..." progress bar, as well as a number of hand-drawn images.

The images include:

- A six-sided die displaying numbers 3 (top), 6 (front) and 5 (side)
- A coffin with "RIP" written on its lid
- A six-armed snowflake
- An acoustic guitar
- An ant
- A devil-like mascot character

One of the monitors displays a monochrome desktop screen - clicking it will redirect the user to the [Suspicious Correspondence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Suspicious_Correspondence "Suspicious Correspondence") ARG website.

### Input Genetic Sequence

One of the monitors on the site contains six ports which are used to input six different genetic sequences, as well as a **<SUBMIT\>** button below the ports.

These genetic Sequences are found using the [Big Bytes Pizza](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Big_Bytes_Pizza),[City Wide Metro](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/City_Wide_Metro), and On-Lyne webpages on the Warframe website.

Going from left to right and top to bottom, the sequences are as follows:
#### First Genetic Sequence
HOHHLKLLKOKO
#### Second Genetic Sequence
OHLKKOOLLHHK
#### Third Genetic Sequence
KOOKLHLKOHLH
#### Fourth Genetic Sequence
OLOKKOKLHLHH
#### Fifth Genetic Sequence
LLOHLHKKOOHK
#### Sixth Genetic Sequence
LKLOKHHOLHOK

Succesfully entering all six sequences will play a success jingle and display the following message:
>Excellent. Your efforts are added to the signal. We need many voices, however, and I know just the carrot to entice more to join the chorus... In the meanwhile, here is a small token of gratitude.

Attached to the message is a promo code: "**TH3GR8D3SP41R**". Entering it on Warframe's [promo code redeem page](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Promo_Codes "Promo Codes") will gift the user with an in-game item: On-Lyne Poster [Orbiter decoration](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Decorations/Orbiter_Decorations "Decorations/Orbiter Decorations").

After entering the correct sequences, one of the larger CRT screens with the "Compiling..." message will change it to read "COUNTDOWN" and display a clock counting down to **November 29th 2024, 2PM ET** - date and time of then-upcoming [Devstream #183](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Devstream_183 "Devstream 183"). All smaller screens will also change to images of the Lidless Eye.

Pressing the button after entering an incorrect or incomplete sequence, or no sequence at all will play an error sound and make no changes to the page's contents.
#### Post-Devstream #183

After the airing of [Devstream #183](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Devstream_183 "Devstream 183") on November 29th 2024, the site was further changed. The puzzle was reverted to its initial state (empty "Input Genetic Sequence" fields, hand-drawn images displaying on smaller CRTs) even if the user had successfully entered the genetic sequences before.

**Note:** All six input fields support copy-paste prompts/key combinations - genetic sequences do not need to be entered manually each time if the puzzle resets.

Submitting correct genetic sequences now causes the Countdown screen to display a functional "Compiling..." progress bar - once it reaches 100%, a button labeled **"DEPLOY ANTIVIRUS"** will appear. Clicking the button directs the user to a [YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JbDG9cyE4hI) - an official song release of the "newest" [On-lyne](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/On-lyne "On-lyne") single titled "[THE GREAT DESPAIR](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Great_Despair "The Great Despair")".

After returning to the Seek the Signal page, the button will change to a glitched version, with the label now reading "**THANKS KIDDO**". Clicking the button again will play a soundbyte consisting of strange noise and a child's sinister laughter, then change all CRTs on the page to display either a low-resolution monochrome image of [Man in the Wall](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Man_in_the_Wall "The Man in the Wall")'s face or the message "THANKS KIDDO".

**Note:** Audio volume on the soundbyte is relatively high and can catch some users off-guard - it is advised to lower the playback device's volume, mute the site by clicking the loudspeaker icon in the bottom right corner of the page or mute the browser tab/window before clicking the button.
