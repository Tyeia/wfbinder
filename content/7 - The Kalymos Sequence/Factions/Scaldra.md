The **Scaldra** are a [faction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Factions "Factions") encountered in [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania"), first appearing in [The Hex](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Quest\) "The Hex (Quest)") quest.

Ruthless aggression was Höllvania's answer to the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TechrotIcon.png/32px-TechrotIcon.png?5b58e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot) [Techrot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot). Wielding highly corrosive [Efervon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Efervon "Efervon"), Scaldra soldiers now patrol the streets to purge any Infestation. With martial law now declared, the Scaldra have total control. Led by [Major Neci Rusalka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Major_Neci_Rusalka "Major Neci Rusalka"), her command brings the entire military might of Höllvania to bear against you.

The [Scaldra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Scaldra) take increased damage from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgImpactSmall64.png/32px-DmgImpactSmall64.png?27a4e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Impact_Damage) [Impact](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Impact_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) Damage, but reduced damage from  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgGasSmall64.png/32px-DmgGasSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage) [Gas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage).

# Overview
The Scaldra are made up of human soldiers and are one of the two enemy factions encountered in [Warframe 1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Hex_\(Quest\) "The Hex (Quest)"), the other being the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/TechrotIcon.png/32px-TechrotIcon.png?5b58e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot) [Techrot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot).

Similar to the  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/IconGrineerOn.png/32px-IconGrineerOn.png?28616)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer) [Grineer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Grineer), Scaldra units possess [armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor "Armor"), have troops similar to the riot shield [Lancer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lancer "Lancer") ([Scaldra Barbican](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scaldra_Barbican "Scaldra Barbican")) and the hulking unit [Nox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nox "Nox") ([Scaldra Dedicant](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Scaldra_Dedicant "Scaldra Dedicant")), and have an identical weakness to  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgImpactSmall64.png/32px-DmgImpactSmall64.png?27a4e)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Impact_Damage) [Impact](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Impact_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage).

Most infantry also carry Efervon canisters on their backs which serve as [weakpoints](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts") in addition to their heads.

# Lore & History
Scaldra is a private military contractor with close ties to the Höllvanian government. In the face of the [Techrot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot "Techrot") outbreak in [1999](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/1999 "1999"), [Höllvania](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H%C3%B6llvania "Höllvania") declared martial law and tasked the Scaldra with curbing the spread of the infestation. The Scaldra now keep the nation under a strict quarantine, locking down any roads in and out. Additionally, the Scaldra forces control the media, even limiting communications between citizens. They mainly use Efervon to fumigate districts of Höllvania to exterminate any Techrot. Despite the Scaldra's purpose to save the city, they appear to be unconcerned with any remaining civilians.

Led by the ruthless [Major Neci Rusalka](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Major_Neci_Rusalka "Major Neci Rusalka") and [Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lieutenant_Viktor_Vodyanoi "Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi"), the Scaldra display an unyielding sense of duty, and will even manically charge headlong into any opposing forces. Who else assumes Scaldra's wider command is unknown, but it is known that all wine in Höllvania was requisitioned by them a few days into the outbreak. [Velimir Volkov II](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Velimir_Volkov_II "Velimir Volkov II") and [Minerva Hendricks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Minerva_Hendricks "Minerva Hendricks") consider it a mercenary cult, supported by Viktor's reference to mandatory [dualistic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dualism "Dualism") prayer sessions and ritual purification. Minerva's recent investigations led her to believe they use religion as a cover for criminal operations, at the behest of major corporations.

Throughout the city, they can be seen aggressively mobilizing against the Techrot, arriving in TI-94 armored personnel carriers, or airdropped via plane. Despite their proliferation of the corrosive Efervon chemical agent, the success of the Scaldra's ongoing containment of the outbreak is dubious at best; the Techrot continues to spread throughout the Höllvanian underground, and skirmishes between Scaldra soldiers and infested creatures often break out into the city streets.

Despite warring against the Techrot, the Scaldra may be secretly attempting to weaponize them as shown with the [H-09 Efervon Tank](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H-09_Efervon_Tank "H-09 Efervon Tank")'s transformation into a Techrot creature, seemingly unbeknownst to Viktor.

# Equipment & Technology
The Scaldra mainly use ballistic and chemical weapons that deal  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png/32px-DmgCorrosiveSmall64.png?9c1be)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) [Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) and  [![](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/thumb/DmgGasSmall64.png/32px-DmgGasSmall64.png?60ae0)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage) [Gas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage) damage, and heavily employ the use of **[Efervon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Efervon "Efervon")**, a bright-green, highly corrosive chemical that is hazardous to organic life. All Scaldra weaponry incorporates Efervon in some manner.

Scaldra infantry are supported by autonomous drones and armored vehicles, including at least two types of advanced tanks. Some Scaldra units, like the elite Stormfall paratroopers, have access to extremely powerful equipment such as the Thermian RPG rocket launcher.

The pinnacle of the Scaldra's mechanized arsenal is the [H-09 Efervon Tank](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H-09_Efervon_Tank "H-09 Efervon Tank"), which is the successor to the [H-04](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/H-04_Efervon_Tank "H-04 Efervon Tank") design.

Despite their Efervon countermeasures, Scaldra tech is still susceptible to being overtaken by Techrot.
